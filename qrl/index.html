量子探针增强重力加速度常数测量Enhanced quantum sensing of gravitational acceleration constant

利用离域量子探针测量地球局部引力场，精度随波函数两分量间距的平方提升，实际位置测量下性能相对最终极限的下降不足一个数量级，并证明g与m联合估计的额外噪声可以忽略。

我们研究利用量子探针精确测定地球局部引力场强度。研究结果表明，离域探针通常优于局域探针，其精度提升随波函数两分量之间间距的平方增长。这种优势在真实位置测量下依然存在，此时性能相对最终极限的下降不到一个数量级。我们还讨论了地球表面的影响，证明其效应在粒子落到地面之前不久以前都可以忽略。最后，我们处理了引力加速度g与探针质量m的联合估计，证明由二者内在不相容性引起的额外估计噪声可以忽略。

Abstract (English)
We investigate the use of quantum probes to accurately determine the strength of the local gravitational field on Earth. Our findings show that delocalized probes generally outperform localized ones, with the precision enhancement scaling quadratically with the separation between the two wavefunction components. This advantage persists under realistic position measurements, where performance are degraded by less than one order of magnitude with respect to the ultimate bound. We also discuss the influence of Earth’s surface, demonstrating that its effect can be neglected until shortly before the particle hits the floor. Finally, we address the joint estimation of the gravitational acceleration g and the probe mass m , proving that the excess estimation noise arising from their inherent incompatibility is negligible.

Giorgio Stucchi, Matteo G.A. Paris

量子伊辛模型中长程相互作用与耗散的博弈Interplay between long-range interactions and dissipation in quantum Ising model

将长程相互作用量子伊辛模型拓展到开放系统，发现长程作用既能稳定铁磁畴、抑制噪声，又使每个自旋间接暴露于更大环境而加速记忆丧失，弱耗散下呈现依赖初态的丰富弛豫动力学。

长程相互作用模型展现出由偏离集体描述所驱动的丰富动力学现象。本工作将这些模型的研究拓展至开放量子系统，聚焦于非局域相互作用与局域耗散之间复杂的相互作用。结果表明，长程相互作用对弛豫的影响具有两面性：它们通过稳定铁磁畴、减小局域涨落来抑制噪声的影响，但同时通过间接使每个自旋暴露于更广阔的环境而加速记忆丧失。强耗散将系统驱入玻璃态、对作用范围不敏感的弛豫，而弱耗散区的结果则揭示出丰富的依赖状态的动力学。具体而言，长程集体行为保护了从极化态淬火后的系统早期相干性，却主动加速交错位形的弛豫。此外，即使在耗散淬火的近稳态阶段，非局域相互作用仍持续驱动记忆退关联。这些发现为使用长程相互作用的模拟量子模拟器的设计、控制和校准提供了及时的理论洞见。

Abstract (English)
Long-range interacting models exhibit rich dynamical phenomena driven by deviations from collective descriptions. This work extends the study of these models to open quantum systems, focusing on the complex interplay between nonlocal interactions and local dissipation. The results demonstrate that the impact of long-range interactions on relaxation is twofold: they mitigate the impact of noises by stabilizing ferromagnetic domains and reducing local fluctuation, yet simultaneously accelerate memory loss by indirectly exposing each spin to a broader environment. While strong dissipation drives the system into glassy, range-insensitive relaxation, the results in weak dissipation regime reveal rich state-dependent dynamics. Specifically, long-range collective behaviors protect the early-stage coherence of systems quenched from polarized states, whereas they actively accelerate the relaxation of staggered configurations. Furthermore, nonlocal interactions continue to drive memory decorrelation even in the near-steady stages of a dissipative quench. These findings offer timely theoretical insights for the design, control, and calibration of analog quantum simulators operating with long-range interactions.

量子计算有望在某些问题类别上相较经典方法实现计算加速；然而在近期量子与经典混合工作流中，整体性能常受经典处理开销制约。电路编译（包括门分解、拓扑感知的量子比特映射和深度优化）以及电路传输、测量结果交换等协议层通信所带来的时间成本，常常超过实际量子电路执行时间，已成为实际部署的关键瓶颈。本文提出QPanda 3.0，一个高性能量子编程框架，针对支撑NISQ时代混合集成的经典编译路径进行加速优化。借助优化的编译流水线、自定义二进制指令流（OriginBIS）和硬件感知执行策略，QPanda 3.0相比OpenQASM 2.0的编码速度最高提升86.9倍、解码速度最高提升35.6倍，并在四种评估的连通拓扑上相较Qiskit平均实现10.7倍编译加速，其中在方形拓扑上118量子比特W态电路的最高加速达597倍。这些能力使得在金融、材料科学和组合优化等多个领域开发可扩展量子算法成为可能，并支持混合计算环境中的工业部署与云端执行。

Abstract (English)
Quantum computing promises computational speedups over classical approaches for certain problem classes; however, in near term quantum and classical hybrid workflows, the overall performance is often constrained by classical processing overhead. The time cost associated with circuit compilation, which includes gate decomposition, topology aware qubit mapping, and depth optimization, together with protocol level communication such as circuit transfer and measurement result exchange, frequently exceeds the actual quantum circuit execution time and has become a critical bottleneck to practical deployment. In this paper, we propose QPanda 3.0, a high performance quantum programming framework optimized to accelerate the classical compilation path that underpins NISQ era hybrid integration. Leveraging an optimized compilation pipeline, a custom binary instruction stream (OriginBIS), and hardware aware execution strategies, QPanda 3.0 achieves up to 86.9 × faster encoding and 35.6 × faster decoding than OpenQASM 2.0, as well as an average 10.7 × compilation speedup over Qiskit across the four evaluated connectivity topologies, with a peak speedup of 597 × for a 118-qubit W-state circuit on a square topology. These capabilities enable the development of scalable quantum algorithms across various domains, including finance, materials science, and combinatorial optimization, supporting both industrial deployment and cloud based execution in hybrid computing environments.

光合作用是核心的生物化学过程，将太阳能转化为化学能并支撑地球生命。本综述阐明产氧与不产氧光养生物中结构特征、量子机制与能量传递效率之间的关系。我们考察光捕获复合体的不同几何构型如何通过电子耦合与振动相干优化能量传递效率。综述强调了支配这些效应的原理——能量梯度和簇状排列，并讨论了评估光合系统能量传递效率的常用计算方法。受自然光系统几何结构的启发，我们讨论这些原理如何指导人工光合系统的设计，重点在于对结构参数进行策略性调控以增强量子介导的能量传递。这一理论框架强调发色团排列、电子耦合和环境相互作用在提高能量捕获与转换效率方面的重要性。最后，我们展望未来方向，强调需要更清晰地理解这些结构特征如何转化到合成系统中。未来研究应有助于缩小自然与人工光合系统之间的差距，并支持可持续能源技术的发展。

Abstract (English)
Photosynthesis is a central biochemical process, which converts solar energy into chemical energy and supports life on Earth. This review elucidates the relationship among structural features, quantum mechanisms and energy transfer efficiency in both oxygenic and anoxygenic phototrophs. We examine how different geometric configurations of light-harvesting complexes optimize energy transfer efficiency through electronic coupling and vibronic coherence. The review highlights the principles, energy gradient and clustered arrangement, that govern these effects and discusses common computational methods for evaluating energy transfer efficiency in photosynthetic systems. Drawing inspiration from the geometric architectures of natural photosystems, we discuss how these principles may guide the design of artificial photosynthetic systems, focusing on the strategic tuning of structural parameters to enhance quantum-mediated energy transfer. This theoretical framework emphasizes the importance of chromophore arrangement, electronic coupling, and environmental interactions in improving energy capture and conversion efficiency. Finally, we consider future directions, stressing the need for a clearer understanding of how these structural features can be translated into synthetic systems. Future research should help narrow the gap between natural and artificial photosynthetic systems, and support the development of sustainable energy technologies.

主动光钟进展：近十年发展与未来展望Advances in active optical clocks: Past decade developments and future outlook

综述主动光钟近十年从概念走向实验的进展，介绍坏腔机制下抑制腔牵引、直接由原子跃迁产生相干钟光的原理，以及冷原子脉冲超辐射与热原子连续波两条技术路线。

原子钟按其运行方式可大致分为被动型和主动型。在微波波段，主动氢脉泽具有优异的短期稳定度，与铯喷泉标准一起被广泛用作守时系统中的飞轮振荡器。在光学波段，被动光钟已实现前所未有的准确度和稳定度，但代价是与超稳腔以及将原子制备在光晶格或离子阱中相关的大型复杂基础设施。相比之下，主动光钟在坏腔机制下直接从原子跃迁产生相干钟光，其中腔牵引被强烈抑制，从而降低了对腔波动的敏感度，并有可能简化光钟架构。过去十年间，主动光钟已从概念性提议发展成为快速推进的领域，包括基于冷原子的脉冲超辐射系统和基于热原子蒸气的连续波坏腔系统。本综述总结主动光钟的最新进展，并讨论该领域的未来方向。

Abstract (English)
Atomic clocks can be broadly classified into passive and active types according to their mode of operation. In the microwave domain, active hydrogen masers provide excellent short-term stability and are widely used as flywheel oscillators in timekeeping systems along with the cesium fountain standards. In the optical domain, passive optical clocks have achieved unprecedented accuracy and stability, but at the cost of large and complex infrastructures associated with ultrastable cavities and the preparation of atoms in optical lattices or ion traps. By contrast, active optical clocks generate coherent clock light directly from an atomic transition in the bad-cavity regime, where cavity pulling is strongly suppressed, thereby reducing sensitivity to cavity fluctuations and potentially simplifying optical clock architectures. Over the past decade, active optical clocks have developed from a conceptual proposal into a rapidly advancing field that includes pulsed superradiant systems based on cold atoms and continuous-wave bad-cavity systems based on thermal atomic vapors. This review summarizes recent progress in active optical clocks and discusses future directions of the field.

Chen Wang, Shougang Zhang, Jingbiao Chen, Deshui Yu

利用量子度规设计拓扑量子态传输Engineering topological quantum state transfer via quantum metric

提出以量子度规、最小能隙和循环时间刻画非绝热跃迁的几何绝热判据，并在两个广义SSH模型中优化泵浦路径，使单格点态与双格点纠缠态的拓扑量子态传输保真度和速度显著提升。

拓扑泵浦在拓扑物理中备受关注，在实现鲁棒量子态传输（QST）方面具有很高价值。然而，在绝热演化要求下加速拓扑QST仍具挑战。本工作提出一种几何绝热判据，采用有效量子度规，通过量子度规、最小能隙和循环时间刻画演化过程中的非绝热跃迁。利用两个广义Su–Schrieffer–Heeger模型，我们系统验证了该绝热判据在分别优化单格点态和双格点纠缠态拓扑泵浦路径方面的有效性。数值模拟表明，通过设计使有效量子度规最小化的泵浦路径，QST的保真度和速度可显著提升。我们的工作为通过量子度规优化实现高保真拓扑QST提供了通用框架。

Abstract (English)
Topological pumping has attracted great attention in topological physics and is highly valuable in the realization of robust quantum state transfer (QST). However, speeding up the topological QST under the requirement of adiabatic evolutions remains challenging. In this work, we propose a geometric adiabatic criterion with an effective quantum metric that characterizes non-adiabatic transitions during evolutions via the quantum metric, minimal energy gap and cycle time. Using two generalized Su–Schrieffer–Heeger models, we systematically validate our adiabatic criterion in optimizing topological pumping paths for transferring single-site states and two-site entangled states, respectively. With numerical simulations, we show that by designing pumping paths for minimizing the effective quantum metric, the fidelity and speed of the QST can be significantly improved. Our works provides a general framework for high-fidelity topological QST via quantum metric optimization.

基于量子频率梳的资源高效连续变量GHZ纠缠制备Resource-efficient generation of continuous-variable Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger entanglement based on a quantum frequency comb

利用同步泵浦光学参量振荡器产生的单一量子频率梳，经多像素零拍探测与本征模分解，在单光束内确定性制备三组分连续变量GHZ态，关联方差0.57、0.75、0.57均低于散粒噪声极限。

连续变量（CV）多组分纠缠态，尤其是Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger（GHZ）态，是量子网络和量子信息处理不可或缺的资源。生成CV GHZ态的传统方法依赖于通过复杂干涉网络组合多个独立制备的压缩光，这在可扩展性和稳定性方面带来重大挑战。在此，我们实验演示了一种紧凑且资源高效的方案，利用同步泵浦光学参量振荡器产生的单一量子频率梳生成三组分CV GHZ态。通过采用多像素零拍探测，我们同时测量多个频带的正交涨落，并在单次测量中重建完整的多模协方差矩阵。通过本征模分解，我们提取出一组独立的压缩超模，随后通过后处理中实现的定制线性幺正操作对其进行变换。利用该方法，我们从三个超模确定性地构建三组分GHZ态，并通过van Loock–Furusawa判据验证其纠缠，测得相应关联方差分别为0.57、0.75和0.57，均低于散粒噪声极限。该方法在单一光束内有效实现了可扩展的纠缠生成方案，无需复杂干涉网络。我们的结果为多模量子信息处理提供了可扩展且多功能的平台。

Abstract (English)
Continuous-variable (CV) multipartite entangled states, particularly Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger (GHZ) states, are an indispensable resource for quantum networks and quantum information processing. Conventional approaches to generating CV GHZ states rely on combining multiple independently prepared squeezed lights through complex interferometric networks, which impose significant challenges in scalability and stability. Here, we experimentally demonstrate a compact and resource-efficient scheme for generating a tripartite CV GHZ state from a single quantum frequency comb produced by a synchronously pumped optical parametric oscillator. 以量子费舍尔信息为诊断工具量化 QAOA 态对参数变化的敏感性与关联，发现完全图 Max-Cut 的 QFI 显著大于环形图，纠缠主要放大交叉参数关联且首个纠缠层后收益迅速饱和，据此提出 QFI 引导的变异启发式。

变分量子算法（VQA）是实现近期量子优势的主要候选方案，但关于参数相关性以及纠缠在塑造其行为中所起作用的重要问题仍然存在。本文采用量子费舍尔信息（QFI）作为诊断工具，量化 QAOA 态对参数变化的敏感性与关联。我们研究了环形图与完全图上的 Max-Cut，以及随机 Ising 模型（RIM）实例，比较了仅 RX 的混合器与 RX-RY 混合的混合器（深度至 ( )，可选纠缠阶段与模式）。跨各类问题，完全图 Max-Cut 实例产生的 QFI 本征值和协方差占比都明显大于环形图，超过散粒噪声标度（ ）而仍低于海森堡极限（ ）。纠缠主要放大交叉参数关联，且其收益迅速饱和：第一个纠缠阶段带来 QFI 的主要增长，而额外的阶段往往收益递减甚至产生不利影响。本工作的一项关键方法学贡献是使用在随机参数样本上平均的 QFI 矩阵，从而揭示稳健的全局趋势，并显示出在不同架构与深度下参数相关性呈现强烈的非均匀性。基于这些洞见，我们提出一种 QFI 引导的变异（QIm）启发式，利用 QFI 对角元来调整变异概率和步长。与均匀和随机重启基线相比，QIm 改善了收敛性与稳定性，在大深度和 RIM 能量景观中尤为明显。总体而言，我们的结果将 QFI 定位为 QAOA 拟设的结构性探针，以及在 NISQ 时代指导有效优化策略设计的实用资源。

Abstract (English)
Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQAs) are leading candidates for achieving near-term quantum advantage, yet important questions remain regarding parameter relevance and the role of entanglement in shaping their behavior. In this work, we employ the Quantum Fisher Information (QFI) as a diagnostic tool to quantify the sensitivity and correlations of QAOA states with respect to parameter variations. We study Max-Cut on cyclic and complete graphs, as well as random Ising model (RIM) instances, comparing RX-only and hybrid RX-RY mixers up to depth ( ) with optional entanglement stages and patterns. Across problem classes, complete-graph Max-Cut instances generate substantially larger QFI eigenvalues and covariance fractions than cyclic ones, exceeding the shot-noise scaling ( ) while remaining below the Heisenberg limit ( ). Entanglement primarily amplifies cross-parameter correlations, and its benefits saturate rapidly: the first entangling stage produces the dominant increase in QFI, while additional stages often yield diminishing or even adverse returns. A key methodological contribution of this work is the use of averaged QFI matrices over random parameter samples, which exposes robust global trends and reveals strongly non-uniform parameter relevance across architectures and depths. Leveraging these insights, we propose a QFI-Informed Mutation (QIm) heuristic that adapts mutation probabilities and step sizes using diagonal QFI entries. QIm improves convergence and stability over uniform and random-restart baselines, especially at large depths and in RIM landscapes. In general, our results position QFI as both a structural probe of QAOA ansätze and a practical resource to guide the design of effective optimization strategies in the NISQ regime.

反向偏置自由载流子耗尽增强的硅微环关联光子对源Correlated photon-pair source using silicon microring enhanced by reverse–biased free–carrier depletion

在硅微环旁嵌入 p-i-n 二极管并施加反向偏压以耗尽自由载流子，显著缩短载流子寿命，在 0.038 mW 腔内功率、30 V 偏压下获得近 5 GHz/mW² 的产生率与 231 的符合与偶然符合比。

本研究系统考察了在微环腔中引入自由载流子耗尽对关联光子对产生的影响。自由载流子耗尽通过在硅微环谐振器旁嵌入 p-i-n 二极管并施加反向偏压实现，从而降低自由载流子吸收造成的非线性损耗。实验结果表明自由载流子寿命显著缩短，改善了光子对产生的关键指标，包括符合计数率、符合与偶然符合比（CAR）以及预告效率。在 0.038 mW 腔内功率和 30 V 偏压下，实现了接近 5 GHz/mW² 的高光子对产生率，相应的 CAR 为 231。然而也观察到，高泵浦功率带来的线性噪声和热扰动等副作用可能压倒这些改善。过高的电压同样会使性能下降，因为消光比偏离最佳工作点。此外，还考虑了直流供电中存在的电源线干扰，它会在透射谱中引起显著振荡，并被证明不利于光子对的产生。

Abstract (English)
This study systematically investigates the impact of introducing free-carrier depletion to the microring cavity on the correlated photon-pair generation. The free-carrier depletion is realized through embedding a p-i-n diode adjacent to the silicon microring resonator and applying a reverse bias voltage, thus reducing the nonlinear loss caused by free carrier absorption. Experimental results show a significant reduction in free-carrier lifetime, which improves key metrics of photon-pair generation, including coincidence rate, coincidence-to-accidental ratio (CAR), and heralding efficiency. A high pair generation rate of near 5 GHz/mW 2 with an associated CAR of 231 is achieved at 0.038 mW on-cavity power and 30 V bias. However, it is observed that the side-effect such as linear noise and thermal disturbance due to high pump power can override the improvements. Excessively high voltage can also degrade the performance as the extinction ratio is deviated from the optimum operating point. Additionally, the power-line interference existing in the DC supply is considered, which induces significant oscillation in the transmission spectrum and is shown detrimental to the photon-pair generation.

保真度可自主调节的量子网络编码Quantum network coding with adjustable fidelity

提出一种基于线性网络编码的量子多播方案，借助发送方与接收方之间的GHZ态和非对称最优普适克隆，使各接收方能自主调节恢复态的保真度，仅需1次量子传输和2次经典传输即可完成多播。

未知量子态的多播是量子网络构建中不可回避的问题。现有大多数量子网络编码方案在多播时只能在一个目标节点上恢复未知态。本文提出一种在线性网络编码网络中多播未知量子态的方案。具体而言，每个发送方与所有接收方之间先制备GHZ态，随后未知态被发送并存储于接收方共享的纠缠态之中，最后接收方利用非对称最优普适克隆获得所需的态。该方案在经典通信受限的网络中只需1次量子传输和2次经典传输即可实现多播。与其他方案相比，本方案允许接收方自主调节保真度，而发送方无须知晓其需求。

Abstract (English)
Multicast of unknown quantum states is an unavoidable issue in the construction of quantum networks. Most existing quantum network coding schemes can only restore an unknown state at one of the target nodes during multicast. In this paper, we propose a quantum network coding scheme to multicast unknown quantum states in networks with linear network coding. Concretely, GHZ states are generated between each sender and all receivers. Unknown states are then sent and stored in the entangled state shared by the receivers. Finally, the receivers use asymmetric optimal universal cloning to obtain the required states. The scheme achieved to multicast within 1 quantum transmission and 2 classical transmissions in networks with constrained classical communication. Compared to other schemes, our scheme allows receivers to autonomously adjust the fidelity without the sender knowing the requirements.

Yuchen Liu, Tao Shang, Jiayu Liu

原子磁强计电磁感应成像：走向成熟Electromagnetic induction imaging with atomic magnetometers: Coming of age

综述以原子磁强计为传感器的电磁感应成像技术：原子磁强计在低频下仍保持极高灵敏度，突破了传统线圈的限制，使穿障成像与人体器官成像成为可能，并讨论了从医学到安检与工业监测的挑战与机遇。

电磁感应成像（EMI）是一种发展数十年的成熟成像技术，已被广泛用于评估金属结构中的裂纹以及探测和定位埋藏的金属物体。在标准配置下，EMI的应用范围因传感线圈在低频下灵敏度较差而受到严重限制，这使其无法开展需要低频工作或极高灵敏度的有趣应用，例如穿障成像或人体器官成像。2014年，利用原子磁强计（AM）作为EMI传感器的演示彻底改变了这一局面，为EMI开辟了新的前景，因为原子磁强计在低频下仍能保持极高的灵敏度。这篇综述文章考察了EMI-AM的最新技术水平，并讨论了该技术在从医学到安全与工业监测等诸多领域中的挑战与机遇。

Abstract (English)
Electromagnetic induction imaging (EMI) is a well established imaging technique, developed over several decades. It has been widely used to assess cracks in metallic structures and to detect and localise buried metallic objects. In its standard configuration, EMI’s remit of application is severely restricted by the poor sensitivity of the sensing coil at low frequency. This prevents the exploration of interesting applications where low-frequency operation and/or extreme sensitivity is required, such as through-barrier imaging or imaging of human organs. The demonstration in 2014 of the use of an atomic magnetometer (AM) as sensor for EMI completely changed this scenario, and opened up new perspectives for EMI, as atomic magnetometers maintain their extreme sensitivity at low frequency. This review article examines the state-of-the-art of EMI-AM and discusses challenges and opportunities of the technology in many sectors, from medicine to security and industrial monitoring.

超导光腔中Jarzynski等式的经典极限与量子资源理论Jarzynski equality in the context of superconducting optical cavities: Classical limit and its connection with quantum resource theory

用Dyson展开给出非线性Jaynes–Cummings模型平均指数化功的解析式，发现高温低Kerr强度为经典区、低温高Kerr强度为量子区，Kerr非线性因而可作为量子资源在有限温度下利用。

我们研究了Jarzynski等式量子版本的经典极限。模型采用非线性Jaynes–Cummings哈密顿量，它描述Kerr介质中超导光腔内的场。为评估平均指数化功，我们应用Dyson展开并在相互作用绘景中定义功算符。由此得到的解析表达式为在量子范畴内检验Jarzynski等式提供了有力的工具。我们的计算结果揭示了两个不同区域：高温、低Kerr强度下的经典区域，以及低温、高Kerr强度下的量子区域。由于系统与热浴处于平衡，Kerr非线性项的引入因此是量子区域形成的原因。这反过来可被认定为一种量子资源，用于在非零温度环境中获得量子计算上的优势。

Abstract (English)
We investigate the classical limit of a quantum version of Jarzynski equality. Our model is the nonlinear Jaynes–Cummings Hamiltonian, which represents a field within a superconducting optical cavity in a Kerr medium. To assess the averaged exponentiated work, we apply a Dyson expansion and define the work operator in the interaction picture. The resulting analytical expression provides a powerful tool for examining the Jarzynski equality in the quantum domain. Our computational results reveal two distinct regimes: a classical one for high temperatures and low Kerr intensities, and a quantum one for low temperatures and high Kerr intensities. Because the system is in equilibrium with the thermal bath, the introduction of the Kerr nonlinearity term is therefore responsible for the quantum regime. This in turn can be identified as a quantum resource and used to gain advantages in quantum computing in a non-zero temperature environment.

综述极性分子激光冷却与俘获的最新进展，涵盖磁光囚禁、亚多普勒冷却与长寿命囚禁，重点讨论相空间密度的提升，以及迈向激光冷却分子玻色–爱因斯坦凝聚所面临的挑战与可能的路径。

过去十年间，极性分子的激光冷却与俘获取得了显著进展，使其在量子信息、量子化学和精密测量中的应用成为可能。激光冷却、磁光囚禁、亚多普勒冷却以及极性分子的长寿命囚禁等技术均已成功演示。本综述重点介绍了若干关键成果，包括在相空间密度方面的进展，其长期目标是实现激光冷却分子的玻色–爱因斯坦凝聚。我们讨论了当前面临的挑战以及达成这一里程碑的可能策略。

Abstract (English)
Laser cooling and trapping of polar molecules has seen remarkable progress in the past decade, enabling applications in quantum information, quantum chemistry, and precision measurements. Techniques such as laser cooling, magneto-optical trapping, sub-Doppler cooling, and long-lifetime trapping of polar molecules have been successfully demonstrated. This review highlights key achievements, including advancements in phase space density, with the long-term objective of achieving Bose–Einstein condensation in laser-cooled molecules. We discuss the current challenges and potential strategies for reaching this milestone.

作为马约拉纳源与传输通路的拓扑约瑟夫森三结Topological Josephson trijunctions as a Majorana source and path

在垂直磁场下的拓扑绝缘体上构建三个约瑟夫森结交汇的三结，其超导相位跨越拓扑与非拓扑相边界时可实现马约拉纳束缚态的成对产生、弹射湮灭与定向输运，从而支持融合与编织操作。

马约拉纳束缚态（MBSs）是拓扑超导体中的零能局域态，服从非阿贝尔编织统计。对可移动 MBSs 的操控，包括其按需产生、传播与融合，是编织 MBSs 和操作拓扑量子比特的关键。我们证明，这种操控可由垂直磁场中拓扑绝缘体上形成的三结实现，那里有三个约瑟夫森结交汇。当三结中心的超导相位演化并跨越拓扑相畴与非拓扑相畴之间的边界时，三结会依据相边界表现出两种截然不同的动力学行为：源/漏与通路。在源/漏边界上，两个 MBSs 在中心成对产生，其中一个被弹射到约瑟夫森结中。其逆过程，即两个 MBSs 碰撞导致的成对湮灭，也可以发生。在通路边界上，一个 MBS 从一个约瑟夫森结移动到三结中心，反之亦然，而不涉及其他 MBSs。利用这两种动力学，可在三结网络中实现 MBSs 的融合与编织操作。我们以一个双三结装置为例加以说明，其中与 MBSs 相关的非阿贝尔态演化无需对装置进行动态控制即可被操控，并可通过约瑟夫森辐射谱学检测。

Abstract (English)
Majorana bound states (MBSs) are zero-energy localized states in topological superconductors, obeying non-Abelian braiding statistics. Manipulation of mobile MBSs, encompassing their on-demand generation, propagation, and fusion, is essential for braiding MBSs and operating topological qubits. We show that the manipulation is enabled by a trijunction formed on a topological insulator in a perpendicular magnetic field, where three Josephson junctions meet. While the superconducting phases at the trijunction center evolve to cross a boundary between topological and non-topological phase domains, the trijunction offers two distinct dynamics, source/sink and path, depending on the phase boundaries. At a source/sink boundary, two MBSs are pairwise generated at the center, and one of them is ejected to a Josephson junction. The reverse, pair annihilation by collision of two MBSs, can also happen. At a path boundary, an MBS moves from a Josephson junction to the trijunction center or vice versa, without involving other MBSs. Exploiting the two dynamics, fusion and braiding of MBSs can be operated in a trijunction network. We illustrate this with a double trijunction setup, where non-Abelian state evolutions associated with MBSs can be manipulated without dynamical control of the setup and detected by Josephson emission spectroscopy.

Kyungtae Kim, June-Young M. Lee, Hyeongseop Kim, H.-S. Sim

No. 04

非厄米趋肤效应对退相干的鲁棒性Decoherence resilience of the non-Hermitian skin effect

利用具有退相位和振幅阻尼两种可调退相干通道的光子量子行走，实验发现非厄米趋肤效应在退相位下可存活至完全非相干区，甚至被增强，而振幅阻尼的影响则强烈依赖于其作用顺序。

由与环境相互作用产生的退相干和耗散，会对物理系统中的输运产生双重影响：既抑制相干传播，又引入扩散并减轻无序系统中的局域化。非厄米物理提供了一种不同的情形，其中结构化耗散诱导出从体到边界的方向性输运，即非厄米趋肤效应。这种输运在退相干下能否持续、被增强还是受到阻碍，在很大程度上仍是一个开放问题。本文利用具有退相位和振幅阻尼这两种可调原型退相干通道的光子量子行走，对这一问题进行了实验研究。在退相位下，非厄米趋肤效应可存活至完全非相干区，并且观察到它甚至会被退相位增强，产生超过相干动力学漂移速度的漂移速度。相比之下，振幅阻尼表现出明显的顺序依赖性：当它作用于非厄米损耗算符之前时，会抑制并最终在完全非相干极限下消除非厄米趋肤效应；当它作用于其后时，非厄米趋肤效应会持续存在，并可在足够大的损耗强度下被增强。该工作连接了量子和经典非厄米动力学，证明了非厄米趋肤效应对退相干的鲁棒性，并为在噪声、非平衡系统中利用退相干增强方向性输运开辟了途径。退相干通常被认为不利于相干输运，但它反而可以增强非厄米趋肤效应，使其持续到完全非相干区；其影响可通过退相干的类型和实施方式来调节。

Abstract (English)
Decoherence and dissipation, arising from interactions with the environment, can exert a dual influence on transport in physical systems, suppressing coherent propagation while inducing diffusion and mitigating localization in disordered systems. Non-Hermitian physics offers a distinct scenario, where structured dissipation induces directional bulk-to-boundary transport, known as the non-Hermitian skin effect. Whether such transport can persist, be enhanced, or hindered under decoherence remains a largely open question. Here we experimentally address this question using photonic quantum walks with two tunable prototypical decoherence channels, dephasing and amplitude damping. Under dephasing, the non-Hermitian skin effect survives up to the fully incoherent regime and is observed to even be enhanced by dephasing, yielding drift velocities that exceed those of coherent dynamics. By contrast, amplitude damping shows a pronounced order dependence: applied before the non-Hermitian loss operator, it suppresses and ultimately eliminates the non-Hermitian skin effect in the fully incoherent limit; applied afterward, the non-Hermitian skin effect persists and can be enhanced at sufficiently large loss strengths. Our work bridges quantum and classical non-Hermitian dynamics, demonstrates the resilience of the non-Hermitian skin effect to decoherence, and opens avenues for harnessing decoherence to enhance directional transport in noisy, nonequilibrium systems. Decoherence, often viewed as detrimental to coherent transport, can instead enhance the non-Hermitian skin effect, which persists into the fully incoherent regime. Its effect can be tuned by the type and implementation of decoherence.

Kunkun Wang, Lei Xiao, Stefano Longhi, Peng Xue
Nat. Commun. (2026)
10.1038/s41467-026-78483-z

Si/SiGe 中经由 T 型结的传送带模式电子穿梭Conveyor-mode electron shuttling through a T-junction in Si/SiGe

通过 T 型结连接两条独立驱动的传送带式穿梭通道，在不增加控制线的情况下实现跨通道电子路由，测得电荷转移保真度接近 100%，并利用原子脉冲控制 54 个量子点的填充以实现电子图案交换。

门控 Si/SiGe 器件中的传送带模式穿梭能够以可扩展数量的信号线，将单个电子、电子图案以及束缚在量子点中的自旋量子比特绝热转移数微米。为充分发挥其潜力，线性穿梭通道必须连接成具有可控路由的二维网格。我们提出了一种连接两条独立驱动穿梭通道的 T 型结器件。跨结的电子路由除了每条传送带所需的四条通道外，无需额外的控制线。在 270 mm s⁻¹ 的瞬时电子速度下，我们测得通道间电荷转移保真度为 F=100.0000000^{+0}_{-9×10^{-7}} %。54 个量子点的填充由简单的原子脉冲控制，这使我们能够交换电子图案，为原生自旋量子比特 SWAP 门奠定基础。该 T 型结为实现具有灵活自旋量子比特路由、面向量子纠错的可扩展二维量子计算架构开辟了道路。

Abstract (English)
Conveyor-mode shuttling in gated Si/SiGe devices enables adiabatic transfer of single electrons, electron patterns and spin qubits confined in quantum dots across several microns with a scalable number of signal lines. To realize their full potential, linear shuttle lanes must connect into a two-dimensional grid with controllable routing. We introduce a T-junction device linking two independently driven shuttle lanes. Electron routing across the junction requires no extra control lines beyond the four channels per conveyor belt. We measure an inter-lane charge transfer fidelity of $$F={100.0000000}_{-9\times 1{0}^{-7}}^{+0}\,\%$$ F = 100.0000000 − 9 × 1 0 − 7 + 0 % at an instantaneous electron velocity of 270 mm s −1 . The filling of 54 quantum dots is controlled by simple atomic pulses, allowing us to swap electron patterns, laying the groundwork for a native spin-qubit SWAP gate. This T-junction establishes a path towards scalable, two-dimensional quantum computing architectures with flexible spin qubit routing for quantum error correction.

Max Beer, Ran Xue, Lennart Deda, Stefan Trellenkamp, Jhih-Sian Tu, Paul Surrey, Inga Seidler, Hendrik Bluhm, Lars R. Schreiber

No. 06

直接产生通信波段自旋-光子纠缠的贝尔非定域性Bell nonlocality with directly generated telecom-band spin–photon entanglement

量子纠缠

基于铷原子 1530 nm 偶极跃迁和腔辅助方案，从单原子直接发射 C 波段通信光子，产生保真度超过 91.4% 的自旋-光子纠缠，并以 2.455(77) 的贝尔不等式违背验证了贝尔非定域性。

量子非定域性通常通过量子网络中的纠缠分发来揭示，是量子信息科学的基石。纠缠的长距离分发要求信息载体，即飞行光子，工作在光纤的最低损耗通信波段。尽管人们已投入大量努力，直接产生 C 波段通信光子与各种固定自旋之间的纠缠，但量子非定域性的验证仍是一项未完成的挑战。本文利用铷原子中波长为 1530 nm 的偶极跃迁和腔辅助方案，实现了单原子对 C 波段通信光子的共振激发和直接发射，产生了自旋-光子纠缠，测得的贝尔态保真度超过 91.4%。随后，我们利用这一高质量纠缠对，通过观察到 2.455(77) > 2 的贝尔不等式违背，验证了贝尔非定域性。这些结果将单原子量子发射器的波长扩展到通信 C 波段，实现了足够高保真度的自旋-光子纠缠，从而最终验证了贝尔非定域性。作者展示了从单个中性原子直接产生通信 C 波段自旋-光子纠缠，实现了 >91.4% 的贝尔态保真度和 2.455(77) 的 CHSH 违背，从而在光纤兼容的量子网络平台上确立了贝尔非定域性。

Abstract (English)
Quantum nonlocality, typically revealed through entanglement distribution across quantum networks, is a cornerstone of quantum information science. Long-distance distribution of entanglement requires the information carrier, i.e., flying photons, to operate in the minimum-loss telecom band of optical fiber. While extensive efforts have been devoted to the direct generation of entanglement between C-band telecom photons and various stationary spins, the verification of quantum nonlocality remains an outstanding challenge. Here, utilizing a dipole transition in rubidium atoms with a wavelength of 1530 nm and a cavity-assisted protocol, we achieve resonant excitation and direct emission of C-band telecom photons from a single atom, generating spin–photon entanglement with a measured Bell state fidelity exceeding 91.4%. We then verify Bell nonlocality by observing a Bell inequality violation of 2.455(77) > 2 using this high-quality entangled pair. These results extend the wavelength of a single-atom quantum emitter to the telecom C-band, achieving sufficiently high-fidelity spin–photon entanglement to finally verify Bell nonlocality. The authors demonstrate the direct generation of telecom C-band spin-photon entanglement from a single neutral atom, achieving >91.4% Bell-state fidelity and a CHSH violation of 2.455(77), thereby establishing Bell nonlocality in a fiber-compatible quantum networking platform

Dong-Yu Huang, Jian Wang, Xiao-Long Zhou, Ze-Min Shen, Si-Jian He, Qi-Yang Huang, Yi-Jia Liu, Yu-Shu Chen, Quan Jiang, Chuan-Feng Li, Guang-Can Guo

基于远程砷化镓量子点的光子纠缠交换Photonic entanglement swapping with remote quantum dots

量子纠缠

利用两个独立砷化镓量子点产生的光子对，并在混合半导体—压电装置中补偿差异，使不同量子点的纠缠光子近乎全同，实现了保真度达0.71(2)的光子纠缠交换，为量子中继网络提供了确定性光源方案。

纠缠交换是一种协议，描述如何在原本互不相关的粒子之间产生纠缠。其光子版本以分离量子系统所产生的光子对之间的干涉为中介，在量子网络中有多种不同的应用。迄今为止，使用来自远程系统的纠缠光子进行的纠缠交换仅借助概率性光源实现。然而，量子网络的规模化需要非概率性量子发射体，且不必在纠缠度与光子对产生速率之间做出取舍。在此，我们展示了利用两个独立GaAs量子点产生的光子对实现的光子纠缠交换。这些发射体被确定性地嵌入混合半导体—压电装置中，使来自两个不同量子点的纠缠光子几乎完全全同。纠缠交换以高达0.71(2)的保真度实现，超过经典极限10个标准差以上。实验数据可由一个理论模型定量解释，该模型还给出了提升该协议性能的途径。我们的工作为在量子中继网络中利用量子点纠缠光子源开辟了道路。

Abstract (English)
Entanglement swapping is a protocol that details how to create entanglement between previously uncorrelated particles. Its photonic version, mediated by the interference of photon pairs generated by separate quantum systems, finds disparate applications in quantum networks. So far, entanglement swapping with entangled photons from remote systems has been implemented only using probabilistic sources. However, the scaling up of quantum networks requires nonprobabilistic quantum emitters without a trade-off between the degree of entanglement and photon-pair generation rate. Here, we demonstrate photonic entanglement swapping using photon pairs generated by two separate GaAs quantum dots. The emitters are deterministically embedded in hybrid semiconductor-piezoelectric devices that make the entangled photons from two dissimilar quantum dots nearly identical. Entanglement swapping is demonstrated with a fidelity as high as 0.71(2), more than 10 SDs above the classical limit. The experimental data are quantitatively explained by a theoretical model that also suggests how to boost the protocol performance. Our work opens the path to the exploitation of quantum dot entangled-photon sources in quantum repeater networks.

磁性量子点点亮创伤性脑损伤坏死神经元并解析MRI细胞来源Lighting up necrotic neurons from traumatic brain injury with magnetic quantum dots to decode the cellular origin of MRI

制备锰掺杂近红外量子点，把高灵敏光学成像与临床兼容的磁共振成像融为一体，借助全脑透明化三维成像与体外细胞实验，揭示增强信号源自激活小胶质细胞对坏死神经元的趋向性吞噬与迁移。

创伤性脑损伤（TBI）的诊断仍严重受限于常规成像技术在可视化决定疾病进展与结局的病理事件方面的能力不足。为应对这一临床挑战，我们报道了一种锰掺杂近红外量子点（QD），它将高灵敏光学成像与临床兼容的磁共振成像（MRI）内在地整合起来。借助全脑透明化驱动的光学成像与细胞分辨率三维（3D）全景成像的优势，结合针对TBI所涉及各类细胞的系统体外细胞研究，我们通过磁性量子点的高灵敏荧光揭示了TBI成像所反映的可解读病理过程，即全身给药后，纳米探针经受损的血脑屏障外渗，随后被激活的小胶质细胞选择性吞噬，这些小胶质细胞继而迁移至富含死亡神经元与神经元碎片的区域。这种由小胶质细胞介导的趋向性解读了纳米尺度造影剂所增强MRI的信号来源，从而将MRI从解剖学工具转变为病理学报告工具。我们还将尺寸相近的生物相容Fe3O4纳米颗粒与上述磁性量子点进行比较，以验证纳米颗粒在TBI的MRI诊断中的转化优势。

Abstract (English)
The diagnosis of traumatic brain injury (TBI) remains heavily constrained by the limitations of conventional imaging techniques in visualizing the pathological events that determine the disease progression and outcome. To address this clinical challenge, we report a manganese-doped near-infrared quantum dot (QD) to intrinsically integrate the highly sensitive optical imaging with clinical compatible magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). Taking the advantages of optical imaging powered by whole-brain clearing and cellular resolution three-dimensional (3D) panoptic imaging, in combination with the systematic in vitro cell studies on different types of cells involved in TBI, we disclosed the interpretable pathology reflected by TBI imaging through the highly sensitive fluorescence of magnetic QDs, i.e., after systemic administration, the nanoprobes were extravasated through the compromised blood-brain barrier and then selectively phagocytosed by activated microglia, which subsequently migrated into the regions rich in dead neurons and neuronal debris. This microglia-mediated tropism decodes the signal origin of MRI enhanced by the nanometer-sized contrast agent, thereby transforming the MRI from an anatomical tool into a pathological reporter. A biocompatible Fe 3 O 4 nanoparticle of similar size was also compared with the magnetic QD mentioned above for validating the translational merits of nanoparticles in MRI diagnosis of TBI.

The bottom-up van der Waals (vdW) assembly of quantum materials from low-dimensional building blocks provides a flexible and structurally defined route for designing materials with tailored quantum properties. Wide-span spectral and noise characterization at millimeter-wave and terahertz frequencies is increasingly important for emerging wireless, sensing, and spectroscopy systems, yet remains challenging for conventional electronic instrumentation because of the complexity and calibration burden of extender-, multiplier-, and mixer-chain architectures. Here we show that photonics can provide attractive alternatives to this highly challenging electronic instrumentation through an antenna-coupled thin-film lithium niobate electro-optic receiver.

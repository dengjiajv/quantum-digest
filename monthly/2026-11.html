No. 02

直接产生通信波段自旋—光子纠缠的贝尔非定域性Bell nonlocality with directly generated telecom-band spin–photon entanglement

量子纠缠

用铷原子 1530 nm 偶极跃迁加腔辅助，从单个原子直接发出 C 波段通信光子，得到保真度 91.4% 的自旋—光子纠缠，并以 2.455(77) > 2 的贝尔不等式违背验证了非定域性。

量子非定域性通常通过量子网络中的纠缠分发来体现，是量子信息科学的基石。要实现纠缠的长距离分发，信息载体（即飞行光子）必须工作在光纤损耗最低的通信波段。尽管已有大量工作致力于在 C 波段通信光子与各类静止自旋之间直接产生纠缠，量子非定域性的验证仍是一项突出的挑战。本文利用铷原子中波长为 1530 nm 的偶极跃迁并结合腔辅助方案，实现了单个原子对 C 波段通信光子的共振激发与直接发射，产生自旋—光子纠缠，测得的贝尔态保真度超过 91.4%。随后，我们利用这对高质量纠缠对，观测到 2.455(77) > 2 的贝尔不等式违背，从而验证了贝尔非定域性。这些结果将单原子量子发射体的波长拓展至通信 C 波段，获得了足够高保真度的自旋—光子纠缠，最终实现了贝尔非定域性的验证。

Abstract (English)
Quantum nonlocality, typically revealed through entanglement distribution across quantum networks, is a cornerstone of quantum information science. Long-distance distribution of entanglement requires the information carrier, i.e., flying photons, to operate in the minimum-loss telecom band of optical fiber. While extensive efforts have been devoted to the direct generation of entanglement between C-band telecom photons and various stationary spins, the verification of quantum nonlocality remains an outstanding challenge. Here, utilizing a dipole transition in rubidium atoms with a wavelength of 1530 nm and a cavity-assisted protocol, we achieve resonant excitation and direct emission of C-band telecom photons from a single atom, generating spin–photon entanglement with a measured Bell state fidelity exceeding 91.4%. We then verify Bell nonlocality by observing a Bell inequality violation of 2.455(77) > 2 using this high-quality entangled pair. These results extend the wavelength of a single-atom quantum emitter to the telecom C-band, achieving sufficiently high-fidelity spin–photon entanglement to finally verify Bell nonlocality.

Dong-Yu Huang, Jian Wang, Xiao-Long Zhou, Ze-Min Shen, Si-Jian He, Qi-Yang Huang, Yi-Jia Liu, Yu-Shu Chen, Quan Jiang, Chuan-Feng Li, Guang-Can Guo

磁性量子点点亮创伤性脑损伤坏死神经元以解码MRI细胞来源Lighting up necrotic neurons from traumatic brain injury with magnetic quantum dots to decode the cellular origin of MRI

报道了一种锰掺杂近红外量子点，将高灵敏光学成像与临床兼容的磁共振成像内在融合，并借助全脑透明化三维成像与体外细胞实验，揭示MRI增强信号源于小胶质细胞向坏死神经元区域的趋向迁移。

创伤性脑损伤（TBI）的诊断仍严重受限于传统成像技术在可视化决定疾病进展与结局的病理事件方面的不足。为应对这一临床挑战，我们报道了一种锰掺杂近红外量子点（QD），将高灵敏光学成像与临床兼容的磁共振成像（MRI）内在地结合起来。借助全脑透明化与细胞分辨率三维（3D）全景成像所支持的光学成像，结合对TBI涉及的不同类型细胞的系统性体外细胞研究，我们通过磁性量子点的高灵敏荧光揭示了TBI成像所反映的可解释病理过程，即：经全身给药后，纳米探针经受损的血脑屏障外渗，随后被活化的小胶质细胞选择性吞噬，这些细胞继而迁移至富含死亡神经元和神经元碎片的区域。这种小胶质细胞介导的趋向性解码了纳米尺度造影剂增强MRI的信号来源，从而将MRI从解剖学工具转变为病理学报告工具。我们还将尺寸相近的生物相容性Fe 3 O 4 纳米颗粒与上述磁性量子点进行比较，以验证纳米颗粒在TBI的MRI诊断中的转化优势。

Abstract (English)
The diagnosis of traumatic brain injury (TBI) remains heavily constrained by the limitations of conventional imaging techniques in visualizing the pathological events that determine the disease progression and outcome. To address this clinical challenge, we report a manganese-doped near-infrared quantum dot (QD) to intrinsically integrate the highly sensitive optical imaging with clinical compatible magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). Taking the advantages of optical imaging powered by whole-brain clearing and cellular resolution three-dimensional (3D) panoptic imaging, in combination with the systematic in vitro cell studies on different types of cells involved in TBI, we disclosed the interpretable pathology reflected by TBI imaging through the highly sensitive fluorescence of magnetic QDs, i.e., after systemic administration, the nanoprobes were extravasated through the compromised blood-brain barrier and then selectively phagocytosed by activated microglia, which subsequently migrated into the regions rich in dead neurons and neuronal debris. This microglia-mediated tropism decodes the signal origin of MRI enhanced by the nanometer-sized contrast agent, thereby transforming the MRI from an anatomical tool into a pathological reporter. A biocompatible Fe 3 O 4 nanoparticle of similar size was also compared with the magnetic QD mentioned above for validating the translational merits of nanoparticles in MRI diagnosis of TBI.

Mingxia Jiao, Xin Zhao, Jingyun Wang, Wanru Tian, Yao Li, Xuan Cheng, Kun Zhang, Xiliang Luo, Peisen Zhang, Mingyuan Gao

金刚石钟形探针实现局域驱动光电流的亚波长中红外成像Sub-wavelength mid-infrared imaging of locally driven photocurrents using diamond campanile probes

提出金刚石基金属—绝缘体—金属钟形探针，把10微米中红外光绝热压缩到约0.7微米，用于石墨烯局域光电流的亚波长成像，光电流信号密度增强约10³倍。

将中红外（mid-IR）光精确、高效地汇聚到亚波长体积，对于探测低能激发和实现强场增强至关重要，但长波长下的吸收损耗与耦合效率低下阻碍了这一目标。本文提出一种基于金刚石的金属—绝缘体—金属钟形探针，可将自由空间中的中红外光（10 μm）绝热压缩至约0.7 μm的尺度。该探针集成到扫描光电压显微镜中，实现了石墨烯中局域驱动光电流的亚波长成像，在低至约0.1 eV的能量下分辨出偏振相关且对接触敏感的响应。实验揭示出光电流信号密度增强达10 3 倍、光功率耦合效率高于1%、近场光通量密度提高18倍，与数值模拟结果一致。该探针在量子级联激光器和自由电子激光器上的运行表明，它是一个稳健、光谱可调的平台，可用于高分辨率探索原子级薄材料中的低能载流子动力学，为中红外光电子学和量子光子学开辟了机会。

Abstract (English)
Precise and high efficiency concentration of mid-infrared (mid-IR) light into sub-wavelength volumes is essential for probing low-energy excitations and achieving strong field enhancements, which is hindered by absorption losses and coupling inefficiencies at long wavelengths. Here, we introduce a diamond-based metal-insulator-metal campanile probe that adiabatically compresses free-space mid-infrared light (10 μm) into ≈ 0.7 μm domain. Integrated into a scanning photovoltage microscope, the probe enables sub-wavelength mapping of locally driven photocurrents in graphene, resolving polarization-dependent and contact-sensitive responses at energies down to ≈ 0.1 eV. Experiments reveal a photocurrent signal density enhancement of 10 3 , coupling light power efficiencies above 1%, and an 18-fold increase in near-field light flux density, in agreement with numerical simulations. Operation of the probe with quantum cascade and free-electron lasers demonstrates a robust, spectrally tunable platform for high-resolution exploration of low-energy carrier dynamics in atomically thin materials, opening opportunities for mid-IR optoelectronics and quantum photonics.

Rajasekhar Medapalli, Nathan D. Cottam, Khushboo Agarwal, Benjamin T. Dewes, Nils Dessmann, Sergio Gonzalez-Munoz, Wenjing Yan, Vaidotas Mišeikis, Sergey Kafanov, Rostislav V. Mikhaylovskiy, Samuel P. Jarvis, Camilla Coletti, Britta Redlich, Amalia Patanè, Oleg V. Kolosov

由一维纳米带原子级编织合成二维量子材料Synthetic 2D quantum materials from atomic weaving of 1D nanoribbons

提出原子尺度乐高式策略，将石墨烯纳米带交织成稳定周期性的二维网络，带隙由带宽决定、带间距调控带宽并在费米能级附近形成平带，还构筑出对称保护的笼目与方格晶格。

由低维构建单元自下而上范德华（vdW）组装量子材料，为设计具有定制量子性质的材料提供了一条灵活且结构明确的途径。虽然堆叠二维（2D）范德华材料已经产生了高度可调的演生拓扑与关联现象平台，并开辟了具有广泛横向设计自由度的三维（3D）层状结构之路，但其他低维材料的自下而上范德华组装在很大程度上仍未被探索。本文提出一种原子尺度乐高式构建策略，从一维纳米带组装出二维材料，通过对晶格几何、堆叠和边缘构型的原子尺度控制，在结构层面嵌入设计自由度。以石墨烯纳米带（GNRs）为原型，我们发展了一种通用编织方法，将平直或弯曲的纳米带交织成稳定、周期性的二维网络——这类似于把二维薄片堆叠成三维晶体，只不过是从一维单元推进到二维架构。由此得到的编织石墨烯纳米带（w-GNRs）表现出依赖于几何的电子行为：带宽度决定带隙，而带间间距控制带宽并在费米能级附近导致平带形成。通过编织弯曲纳米带，我们构建了具有对称保护平带和轨道简并的笼目与方格晶格构型。这一原子乐高框架使得由一维组分可规模化构建人工二维材料成为可能，拓展了具有演生量子性质的高度可调二维体系的设计空间。

Abstract (English)
The bottom-up van der Waals (vdW) assembly of quantum materials from low-dimensional building blocks provides a flexible and structurally defined route for designing materials with tailored quantum properties. While stacking two-dimensional (2D) vdW materials has yielded highly tunable platforms for emergent topological and correlated phenomena and has opened pathways toward three-dimensional (3D) layered architectures with extensive lateral design flexibility, the bottom-up vdW assembly of other low-dimensional materials remains largely unexplored. Here, we propose an atomic-scale Lego construction strategy that assembles 2D materials from 1D nanoribbons, embedding design freedom at the structural level through atomic-scale control over lattice geometry, stacking, and edge configuration. Using graphene nanoribbons (GNRs) as a prototype, we develop a general weaving approach that interlaces flat or curved ribbons into stable, periodic 2D networks-analogous to stacking 2D sheets into 3D crystals, but instead progressing from 1D elements to 2D architectures. The resulting woven GNRs (w-GNRs) exhibit geometry-dependent electronic behavior: ribbon width determines the band gap, while inter-ribbon spacing controls the bandwidth and leads to flat-band formation near the Fermi level. By weaving curved ribbons, we construct kagome and square lattice configurations that host symmetry-protected flat bands and orbital degeneracies. This atomic Lego framework enables scalable construction of artificial 2D materials from 1D components, expanding the design space for highly tunable 2D systems with emergent quantum properties.

基于斯塔克梳与可扩展里德堡气室阵列的按需瞬时带宽微波接收机On-demand instantaneous bandwidth microwave receiver via scalable Rydberg Vapor cell array with Stark comb

提出斯塔克梳空间复用的可扩展架构，使每个里德堡气室成为独立频率选择性外差通道，实验验证210兆赫兹瞬时带宽接收，灵敏度326.6 nVcm⁻¹·Hz⁻¹ᐟ²。

基于里德堡原子的微波（MW）传感器具有高灵敏度和本征可溯源性，但其瞬时带宽从根本上受原子相干时间限制。本文提出一种可扩展的传感架构，通过用斯塔克梳对原子探测通道进行空间复用，克服了这一限制：其中微波频率梳（MFC）与位置相关的斯塔克场使每个里德堡气室都成为一个独立的频率选择性外差接收机。我们用21线微波频率梳对各个斯塔克频移通道进行顺序表征，实验验证了210 MHz瞬时带宽的信道化接收机架构，并在载波共振频率调谐160 MHz的情况下仍保持326.6 nVcm −1 Hz −1/2 的整体灵敏度。要在210 MHz带宽上实现完全同时运行，需要所有通道的并行实现与读出。这种可扩展架构在保持灵敏度的同时实现了按需瞬时带宽，为雷达、通信和频谱监测中的实时宽带微波探测提供了一条途径。

Abstract (English)
Rydberg-atom-based microwave (MW) sensors offer high sensitivity and intrinsic traceability, but their instantaneous bandwidth is fundamentally limited by atomic coherence time. Here, we introduce a scalable sensing architecture that overcomes this limitation by spatially multiplexing atomic detection channels with a Stark comb, in which an MW frequency comb (MFC) and a position-dependent Stark field enable each Rydberg vapor cell to function as an independent frequency-selective heterodyne receiver. We experimentally validate the 210 MHz instantaneous-bandwidth channelized receiver architecture through sequential characterization of individual Stark-shifted channels using a 21-line MFC, while maintaining an overall sensitivity of 326.6 nVcm −1 Hz −1/2 despite a 160 MHz tuning of the carrier resonance frequency. Full simultaneous operation across the 210 MHz bandwidth requires parallel implementation and readout of all channels. This scalable architecture enables on-demand instantaneous bandwidth while preserving sensitivity, providing a pathway toward real-time broadband MW detection for radar, communications, and spectrum monitoring.

Yuechun Jiao, Yuwen Yin, Yunhui He, Jinlian Hu, Cheng Lu, Jingxu Bai, Zhengyang Bai, Weibin Li, Suotang Jia, Jianming Zhao

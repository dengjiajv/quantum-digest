金刚石钟形探针实现局域驱动光电流的亚波长中红外成像Sub-wavelength mid-infrared imaging of locally driven photocurrents using diamond campanile probes

研制金刚石基金属—绝缘体—金属钟形探针，将10微米中红外光绝热压缩至≈0.7微米，并在石墨烯中实现低至≈0.1电子伏特的亚波长光电流成像，信号密度增强达10^3、光功率耦合效率超过1%。

精确且高效地将中红外光聚焦到亚波长体积，对于探测低能激发和实现强场增强至关重要，但长波长下的吸收损耗和耦合低效阻碍了这一目标。为此，我们提出一种金刚石基金属—绝缘体—金属钟形探针，可将自由空间中红外光（10 μm）绝热压缩到≈0.7 μm的区域。将该探针集成到扫描光电压显微镜中，可在石墨烯中对局域驱动的光电流进行亚波长成像，分辨出能量低至≈0.1 eV的偏振相关和接触敏感响应。实验显示光电流信号密度增强达10^3、光功率耦合效率超过1%，近场光通量密度提高18倍，与数值模拟结果一致。该探针在量子级联激光器和自由电子激光器下均可工作，展示了用于高分辨率探索原子级薄材料中低能载流子动力学的稳健、光谱可调平台，为中红外光电子学和量子光子学开辟了机会。

Abstract (English)
Precise and high efficiency concentration of mid-infrared (mid-IR) light into sub-wavelength volumes is essential for probing low-energy excitations and achieving strong field enhancements, which is hindered by absorption losses and coupling inefficiencies at long wavelengths. Here, we introduce a diamond-based metal-insulator-metal campanile probe that adiabatically compresses free-space mid-infrared light (10 μm) into ≈ 0.7 μm domain. Integrated into a scanning photovoltage microscope, the probe enables sub-wavelength mapping of locally driven photocurrents in graphene, resolving polarization-dependent and contact-sensitive responses at energies down to ≈ 0.1 eV. Experiments reveal a photocurrent signal density enhancement of 10 3 , coupling light power efficiencies above 1%, and an 18-fold increase in near-field light flux density, in agreement with numerical simulations. Operation of the probe with quantum cascade and free-electron lasers demonstrates a robust, spectrally tunable platform for high-resolution exploration of low-energy carrier dynamics in atomically thin materials, opening opportunities for mid-IR optoelectronics and quantum photonics.

Rajasekhar Medapalli, Nathan D. Cottam, Khushboo Agarwal, Benjamin T. Dewes, Nils Dessmann, Sergio Gonzalez-Munoz, Wenjing Yan, Vaidotas Mišeikis, Sergey Kafanov, Rostislav V. Mikhaylovskiy, Samuel P. Jarvis, Camilla Coletti, Britta Redlich, Amalia Patanè, Oleg V. Kolosov
Commun. Phys. (2026)
10.1038/s42005-026-02954-4

No. 02

一维纳米带原子编织合成二维量子材料Synthetic 2D quantum materials from atomic weaving of 1D nanoribbons

提出原子尺度乐高策略，以石墨烯纳米带为原型把平直或弯曲的一维纳米带编织成稳定二维网络，带隙由带宽决定、带间距调控带宽并诱导平带，还构筑出对称性保护的Kagome与方格子晶格。

由低维构筑单元自下而上范德瓦尔斯（vdW）组装量子材料，为设计具有定制量子性质的材料提供了一条灵活且结构确定的途径。虽然堆叠二维（2D）vdW材料已经产生了用于涌现拓扑与关联现象的高度可调平台，并为具有广泛面内设计自由度的三维（3D）层状结构开辟了道路，但其他低维材料的自下而上vdW组装仍基本未被探索。在此，我们提出一种原子尺度乐高构建策略，从一维纳米带组装二维材料，通过对晶格几何、堆叠和边缘构型的原子尺度控制，在结构层面嵌入设计自由度。以石墨烯纳米带（GNRs）为原型，我们发展了一种通用编织方法，将平直或弯曲的纳米带交织成稳定、周期性的二维网络——这类似于将二维薄片堆叠成三维晶体，但改为从一维单元推进到二维结构。所得编织GNRs（w-GNRs）表现出依赖几何的电子行为：纳米带宽度决定带隙，而带间间距控制带宽并在费米能级附近导致平带形成。通过编织弯曲纳米带，我们构建了Kagome和方格子晶格构型，它们承载对称性保护的平带和轨道简并。这一原子乐高框架能够从一维组分可扩展地构建人工二维材料，拓展了具有涌现量子性质的高度可调二维系统的设计空间。

Abstract (English)
The bottom-up van der Waals (vdW) assembly of quantum materials from low-dimensional building blocks provides a flexible and structurally defined route for designing materials with tailored quantum properties. While stacking two-dimensional (2D) vdW materials has yielded highly tunable platforms for emergent topological and correlated phenomena and has opened pathways toward three-dimensional (3D) layered architectures with extensive lateral design flexibility, the bottom-up vdW assembly of other low-dimensional materials remains largely unexplored. Here, we propose an atomic-scale Lego construction strategy that assembles 2D materials from 1D nanoribbons, embedding design freedom at the structural level through atomic-scale control over lattice geometry, stacking, and edge configuration. Using graphene nanoribbons (GNRs) as a prototype, we develop a general weaving approach that interlaces flat or curved ribbons into stable, periodic 2D networks-analogous to stacking 2D sheets into 3D crystals, but instead progressing from 1D elements to 2D architectures. The resulting woven GNRs (w-GNRs) exhibit geometry-dependent electronic behavior: ribbon width determines the band gap, while inter-ribbon spacing controls the bandwidth and leads to flat-band formation near the Fermi level. By weaving curved ribbons, we construct kagome and square lattice configurations that host symmetry-protected flat bands and orbital degeneracies. This atomic Lego framework enables scalable construction of artificial 2D materials from 1D components, expanding the design space for highly tunable 2D systems with emergent quantum properties.

Xin Wang, Qiaoling Xu, Dongdong An, Tao Zhang, Lanting Shi, Xianliang Zhou, Bo Lin, Kaiwei Wan, Zhuqin Zhang, Majeed Ur Rehman, Lede Xian
Commun. Phys. 9 (2026)
10.1038/s42005-026-02936-6

基于斯塔克梳可扩展里德堡蒸气池阵列的按需瞬时带宽微波接收机On-demand instantaneous bandwidth microwave receiver via scalable Rydberg Vapor cell array with Stark comb

提出以可扩展的里德堡蒸气池阵列结合斯塔克梳，构建按需瞬时带宽微波接收机，旨在兼顾大瞬时带宽与快速可调谐接收。

里德堡原子具有高灵敏度、宽载波带宽和可溯源性，在微波（MW）测量方面潜力巨大。然而，微波接收机的瞬时带宽窄限制了它的应用。提升接收机的瞬时带宽仍是一项持续的挑战。本文报道了借助带有斯塔克梳的可扩展里德堡蒸气池线性阵列，实现任意瞬时带宽的微波接收机；其中斯塔克梳由微波频率梳（MFC）与位置相关的斯塔克场构成。在斯塔克场存在时，两个里德堡态之间的共振微波跃迁频率随位置变化，因此可以让每一条MFC谱线充当本振（LO）场，并与一个里德堡蒸气池共振耦合。这样，每个蒸气池都利用原子外差探测接收宽带微波信号中落在其瞬时带宽内的那一部分，从而同时完成对宽带微波信号的测量。在原理性实验中，我们利用含21条谱线的MFC场演示了瞬时带宽为210 MHz的微波接收机，同时实现了326.6 nV cm⁻¹ Hz⁻¹ᐟ²的整体灵敏度。原则上，只要有足够多且功率足够的MFC谱线，该方法就能实现接收机的任意瞬时带宽。这项工作为设计和发展可用于雷达、通信和频谱监测的可扩展微波接收机铺平了道路。

Abstract (English)
Rydberg atoms have great potential for microwave (MW) measurements due to their high sensitivity, broad carrier bandwidth, and traceability. However, the narrow instantaneous bandwidth of the MW receiver limits its applications. Improving the instantaneous bandwidth of the receiver is an ongoing challenge. Here, we report on the achievement of an arbitrary instantaneous bandwidth MW receiver via a linear array of scalable Rydberg vapor cells with Stark comb, where the Stark comb consists of an MW frequency comb (MFC) and a position-dependent Stark field. In the presence of the Stark field, the resonance MW transition frequency between two Rydberg states is position dependent, so that we can make each MFC line act as a local oscillator (LO) field to resonantly couple one Rydberg cell. Thus, each cell receives part of a broadband MW signal within its instantaneous bandwidth using atomic heterodyne detection, achieving the measurements of the broadband MW signal simultaneously. In our proof-of-principle experiment, we demonstrate the MW receiver with 210~MHz instantaneous bandwidth using an MFC field with 21 lines. Meanwhile, we achieve an overall sensitivity of 326.6~nVcm$^{-1}$Hz$^{-1/2}$. In principle, the method allows for achieving an arbitrary instantaneous bandwidth of the receiver, provided we have enough MFC lines with enough power. Our work paves the way to design and develop a scalable MW receiver for applications in radar, communication, and spectrum monitoring.

Yuechun Jiao, Yuwen Yin, Yunhui He, Jinlian Hu, Cheng Lu, Jingxu Bai, Zhengyang Bai, Weibin Li, Suotang Jia, Jianming Zhao
Commun. Phys. (2026)
10.1038/s42005-026-02941-9

利用逻辑量子比特探测稀有事件的量子传感Rare-Event Quantum Sensing Using Logical Qubits

★ 编辑推荐

提出借助量子纠错的综合征提取，以高阶关联区分信号与噪声；在按 ε³ 比例牺牲部分信号的同时延长逻辑相干时间，使随机时刻出现的稀有信号探测灵敏度优于常规传感方案。

我们提出一种利用量子纠错（QEC）在噪声环境中探测稀有信号的新协议。该协议的关键特征是通过不同的高阶关联来区分信号与噪声，而这借助 QEC 中综合征提取期间发生的非线性处理得以实现。在这一方案中，QEC 发挥两方面作用。第一，它以记录一个被削减的、随机的逻辑相位 ϕ_L = O(ε³) 为代价，牺牲掉一部分信号 ε。第二，它校正物理噪声并延长用于信号采集的（逻辑）相干时间。对于在局域马尔可夫噪声背景下于随机时刻出现的稀有信号，我们明确证明了本方法相比更常规的传感策略具有更高的灵敏度。

Abstract (English)
We present a novel protocol to detect rare signals in a noisy environment using quantum error correction (QEC). The key feature of our protocol is the discrimination between signal and noise through distinct higher-order correlations, realized by the nonlinear processing that occurs during syndrome extraction in QEC. In this scheme, QEC has two effects. First, it sacrifices part of the signal ε by recording a reduced, stochastic, logical phase ϕ L = O ( ε 3 ) . Second, it corrects the physical noise and extends the (logical) coherence time for signal acquisition. For rare signals occurring at random times in the presence of local Markovian noise, we explicitly demonstrate an improved sensitivity of our approach over more conventional sensing strategies.

Robert Ott, Torsten V. Zache, Soonwon Choi, Adam M. Kaufman, and Hannes Pichler

利用毫米波实现超导量子比特的读出Millimeter Wave Readout of a Superconducting Qubit

★ Physics 精选

把 34.7 GHz 毫米波腔与 3.1 GHz 的 transmon 量子比特以 1.3 GHz 速率强耦合，用超过 10 的腔—比特频率比抑制驱动引起的共振跃迁，在百光子量级驱动下仍无跃迁，无需量子极限放大器即获得超过 99% 的读出保真度。

毫米波正逐渐成为连接和增强里德堡原子、光力学和超导量子比特等不同量子平台的使能技术。在本快报中，我们探索毫米波光子与常规 transmon 量子比特之间的相互作用，特别是用于量子比特读出。我们研究了一个电路量子电动力学系统，它由频率为 ω_r = 2π × 34.7 GHz 的毫米波腔和频率为 ω_q = 2π × 3.1 GHz 的 transmon 量子比特组成，二者以 g = 2π × 1.3 GHz 的速率耦合。由于腔与量子比特之间失谐如此之大，ω_r/ω_q > 10，我们能够抑制驱动引起的不需要的共振态跃迁，从而实现用于量子比特读出的强驱动。即使以超过 100 个光子驱动，我们也测得没有共振态跃迁，并用超过 100 个光子读出量子比特状态，在无需量子极限放大器辅助的情况下实现大于 99% 的测量保真度。

Abstract (English)
Millimeter waves are emerging as an enabling technology for connecting and enhancing different quantum platforms such as Rydberg atoms, optomechanics, and superconducting qubits. In this Letter, we explore the interaction between millimeter wave photons and conventional transmon qubits, specifically for qubit readout. We study a circuit quantum electrodynamic system consisting of a millimeter wave cavity at ω r = 2 π × 34.7 GHz and a transmon qubit at ω q = 2 π × 3.1 GHz coupled at rate g = 2 π × 1.3 GHz . With such a large detuning between cavity and qubit, ω r / ω q > 10 , we are able to suppress drive induced unwanted resonant state transitions, enabling strong drives for qubit readout. We measure no resonant state transitions even when driving with more than 100 photons and read out the qubit state with more than 100 photons to achieve a measurement fidelity greater than 99% without the aid of a quantum limited amplifier.

超越量子比特极限的超导三能级量子链路Superconducting Qutrit Link beyond the Qubit Limit

★ 编辑推荐量子纠缠

在两个独立封装的节点间构建超导三能级量子链路，用同一信道的不同时间模式分别传递 |e⟩ 与 |f⟩ 分量，任意三能级态转移平均保真度达 83.68%，并重建出超越量子比特界限的远程纠缠、密集编码容量与 CGLMP 参数。

超导微波链路已实现量子比特之间的确定性态转移与远程纠缠，但确定性链路迄今只利用了有效二维的传输希尔伯特空间。这里我们展示一条连接两个独立封装节点、由微波信道相连的超导三能级量子链路。每个节点将 transmon 三能级系统、传输谐振腔和可调 Purcell 滤波接口结合在一起，使两个远程微波光子接口在频率和带宽上都能匹配。我们实现了两种跃迁选择性、光子介导的操作，在同一信道的不同时间模式中分别转移 |e⟩ 和 |f⟩ 三能级分量。我们对任意三能级态的转移进行了层析表征，得到平均转移态保真度 83.68% 和三能级过程保真度 77.12%，二者均超过经典三能级转移基准，也超过用有效量子比特信道传输任意三能级态所能达到的最佳平均保真度。利用部分转移操作，我们重建出负度为 0.730 的远程双三能级态、由层析推断的密集编码容量 2.273 比特，以及由层析推断的 Collins-Gisin-Linden-Massar-Popescu（CGLMP）参数 I_3 = 2.332，全部超越相应的量子比特界限或局域界限。这些结果表明，该超导微波链路把 transmon 固有的三能级结构用作真正的高维通信资源。

Abstract (English)
Superconducting microwave links have enabled deterministic state transfer and remote entanglement between qubits, but deterministic links have so far operated with an effectively two-dimensional transmitted Hilbert space. Here we demonstrate a superconducting qutrit link between two independently packaged nodes connected by a microwave channel. Each node combines a transmon qutrit, a transmission resonator, and a tunable Purcell-filter interface, allowing the two remote microwave-photon interfaces to be matched in both frequency and bandwidth. We implement two transition-selective photon-mediated operations that transfer the | e ⟩ and | f ⟩ qutrit components in distinct temporal modes of the same channel. We tomographically characterize arbitrary qutrit-state transfer, obtaining a mean transferred-state fidelity of 83.68% and a qutrit process fidelity of 77.12%, exceeding both the classical qutrit-transfer benchmark and the best possible average fidelity of an effective qubit channel used to transmit an arbitrary qutrit. Using partial-transfer operations, we reconstruct a remote two-qutrit state with negativity 0.730, a tomography-inferred dense-coding capacity of 2.273 bits, and a tomography-inferred Collins-Gisin-Linden-Massar-Popescu (CGLMP) parameter I 3 = 2.332 , all beyond the corresponding qubit or local bounds. These results demonstrate a superconducting microwave link that uses the native three-level structure of transmons as a genuine high-dimensional communication resource.

基于人工光梯度的囚禁离子纠缠Entangling Ions with Engineered Light Gradients

★ 编辑推荐

针对集体运动模式光谱拥挤导致的旁观模式非共振耦合，提出由横向、含时结构光力驱动的几何相位纠缠方案，在保留单离子寻址能力的同时降低光谱拥挤影响；在最多含 12 个离子的晶体中实现错误率低于 5×10^-3 的双量子比特门。

集体运动模式的光谱拥挤会对旁观模式产生非共振耦合，从而限制囚禁离子量子处理器中纠缠相互作用的保真度。我们引入一种由横向、含时结构光力驱动的几何相位纠缠相互作用。通过在与光传播方向正交的平面内施加该力，我们在保留单离子寻址能力的同时降低了光谱拥挤的影响。只要量子比特态经历差分 AC Stark 频移，该方案就与任意量子比特编码兼容。我们在单个势阱中最多包含 12 个离子的离子晶体上实验实现了错误率低于 5 × 10^-3 的高保真双量子比特门。这些结果确立了梯度场光频移门作为在光谱拥挤的囚禁离子系统中产生高保真纠缠的可扩展途径。

Abstract (English)
Spectral crowding of collective motional modes limits the fidelity of entangling interactions in trapped-ion quantum processors by inducing off-resonant coupling to spectator modes. We introduce a geometric-phase entangling interaction driven by a transverse, time-dependent structured-light force. By applying the force in a plane orthogonal to the optical propagation direction, we reduce the effects of spectral crowding while preserving single-ion addressing. The scheme is compatible with arbitrary qubit encodings, provided that the qubit states experience a differential AC Stark shift. We experimentally realize high-fidelity two-qubit gates with error rates below 5 × 10 − 3 in ion crystals containing up to 12 ions confined within a single potential well. These results establish gradient-field light-shift gates as a scalable approach to high-fidelity entanglement generation in spectrally crowded trapped-ion systems.

Abstract (English)
We show that a bound impurity in a Bose-Einstein condensate can be directly mapped to an Unruh-DeWitt detector interacting with a relativistic quantum field. We provide explicit experimental parameters for an implementation using K 41 impurities coupled to a Rb 87 condensate via finite-time Feshbach tuning. As an application, we study the extraction of vacuum entanglement from distant regions of the condensate and find viable parameters for the implementation of entanglement harvesting.

量子信号学习：传感经典场性质的量子优势Quantum Advantage for Sensing Properties of Classical Fields

量子纠缠

提出量子信号学习框架及仅需双模压缩、无源光学和静态零拍测量的量子增强方案，可同时估计经典信号的多个属性，并把散粒噪声压至真空水平以下，证明其在电磁关联测量、干涉腔实时反馈与傅里叶域匹配滤波等任务上相对无纠缠策略具有指数级优势。

现代精密实验常用玻色子传感器在量子噪声极限区探测未知经典场，此时真空涨落限制了常规读出。我们提出量子信号学习——一种将计量学推广到更广泛的属性学习情境的传感框架，并提出一种量子增强方案，可同时估计经典信号的许多属性，且散粒噪声被压制到真空水平以下。我们的方案只需双模压缩、无源光学和静态零拍测量，并能够从同一实验数据集事后对许多属性进行经典估计。我们证明该方案可为常见经典传感任务带来量子加速，包括测量电磁关联、对干涉腔进行实时反馈控制以及傅里叶域匹配滤波。为确立这些分离，我们引入一种最优输运条件化方法，并证明相对于所有无纠缠策略的最坏情况指数级分离，以及对零拍和外差基线的实用加速。我们进一步表明，当把压缩视为资源时，使用压缩光的方案能比任何相干经典探针指数级更快地传感结构化经典背景。

Abstract (English)
Modern precision experiments often probe unknown classical fields with bosonic sensors in quantum-noise-limited regimes where vacuum fluctuations limit conventional readout. We introduce quantum signal learning, a sensing framework that extends metrology to a broader property-learning setting, and propose a quantum-enhanced protocol that simultaneously estimates many properties of a classical signal with shot noise suppressed below the vacuum level. Our scheme requires only two-mode squeezing, passive optics, and static homodyne measurements, and enables post hoc classical estimation of many properties from the same experimental dataset. We prove that our protocol enables a quantum speedup for common classical sensing tasks, including measuring electromagnetic correlations, real-time feedback control of interferometric cavities, and Fourier-domain matched filtering. To establish these separations, we introduce an optimal-transport conditioning method and show both worst-case exponential separations from all entanglement-free strategies and practical speedups over homodyne and heterodyne baselines. We further show that when squeezing is treated as a resource, a protocol with squeezed light can sense a structured classical background exponentially faster than any coherent classical probe.

Jordan Cotler, Daine L. Danielson, and Ishaan Kannan

泡利传播实现强关联量子系统的快速经典模拟Pauli Propagation Enables Fast Classical Simulation of Strongly Correlated Quantum Systems

提出变分双括号流算法，结合贪心算符选择、系数涨落截断与能量方差外推，在128量子比特晶格上获得低于1%的基态能量误差，速度远超DMRG。

强关联量子系统的基态能量估计仍然是计算物理和化学中的一个核心挑战。虽然像密度矩阵重正化群（DMRG）这样的张量网络方法为一维系统提供了高效解，但更高维问题仍然困难。在这里，我们提出一种变分双括号流（vDBF）算法，它利用泡利传播——一种最初为量子电路经典模拟而开发的技术——来高效近似基态能量。通过将贪心算符选择与基于系数的涨落截断以及能量方差外推相结合，我们对多达 128 个量子比特的海森堡和 Hubbard 晶格获得了低于 1% 的相对误差。对于 10 × 10 海森堡晶格（100 个量子比特），vDBF 在单个 CPU 线程上约 2 分钟内获得准确结果，而 DMRG 在 64 个线程上需要超过 50 小时。对于量子蒙特卡洛难以处理的系统，也获得了准确结果：在最大阻挫的 8 × 8 J1–J2 晶格和空穴掺杂的 8 × 8 Hubbard 模型上，最严格的阈值外推到已发表最佳变分估计的千分之几以内。我们进一步在六苯并蔻的 84 量子比特 π 价活性空间上测试 vDBF，其中最可靠的外推关联能与 DMRG 吻合在 1% 以内。这些结果表明，在量子优势基准测试背景下发展的经典模拟技术，可以为多体物理提供实用工具。另见 8 幅图。

Abstract (English)
Ground-state energy estimation for strongly correlated quantum systems remains a central challenge in computational physics and chemistry. While tensor network methods like Density Matrix Renormalization Group (DMRG) provide efficient solutions for one-dimensional systems, higher-dimensional problems remain difficult. Here we present a variational double bracket flow (vDBF) algorithm that leverages Pauli Propagation, a technique originally developed for classical simulation of quantum circuits, to efficiently approximate ground-state energies. By combining greedy operator selection with coefficient-based fluctuation truncation and energy-variance extrapolation, we obtain sub-1% relative errors for Heisenberg and Hubbard lattices of up to 128 qubits. For a 10 × 10 Heisenberg lattice (100 qubits), vDBF obtains accurate results in approximately 2 min on a single CPU thread, compared to over 50 h on 64 threads for DMRG. Accurate results are also obtained for systems that are difficult for quantum Monte Carlo: on the maximally frustrated 8 × 8 J 1 – J 2 lattice and the hole-doped 8 × 8 Hubbard model, the tightest thresholds extrapolate to within a few tenths of a percent of the best published variational estimates. We further test vDBF on the 84-qubit π -valence active space of hexabenzocoronene, where the most reliable extrapolated correlation energies agree with DMRG to within 1%. These results demonstrate that classical simulation techniques developed in the context of quantum advantage benchmarking can provide practical tools for many-body physics. See 8 more figures

在光晶格中构建任意相干原子重排Constructing Arbitrary Coherent Rearrangements in Optical Lattices

借助光超晶格与离散线性光学的类比，用Clements方案由双阱阵列中的隧穿和相位移动构造任意N维单粒子幺正变换，实现可扩展的全连接原子重排。

对光晶格中超冷原子运动自由度的相干控制，为利用有质量粒子实现可编程量子动力学提供了一条有前景的途径。我们提出并分析了一种实现超冷原子相干重排的方案，它对应于单粒子运动态上的任意幺正变换。利用光超晶格中的动力学与离散线性光学之间的类比，我们采用 Clements 方案，从双阱阵列中的隧穿和相位移动系统地构造任意全局 N 维单粒子幺正变换。隧穿被全局控制，而局域操作则通过位点分辨的势移动实现。我们数值研究了该方案对强度噪声和寻址串扰的敏感性。我们确定了由这种幺正构造所支持的关键子程序，包括离散傅里叶变换和非原生哈密顿量的实现。将该方案推广到二维，可实现全对全的原子重排，其电路深度随原子数亚线性增长，为原子重排提供了一种高密度、高度可扩展的方法。另见 1 幅图。

Abstract (English)
Coherent control of motional degrees of freedom of ultracold atoms in optical lattices offers a promising route toward programmable quantum dynamics with massive particles. We propose and analyze a scheme for implementing coherent rearrangement of ultracold atoms, corresponding to arbitrary unitary transformations on single-particle motional states. Exploiting an analogy between dynamics in optical superlattices and discrete linear optics, we employ the Clements scheme to systematically construct any global N -dimensional single-particle unitary from tunneling and phase shifts in arrays of double wells. Tunneling is controlled globally, while local operations are achieved through site-resolved potential shifts. We numerically investigate the susceptibility of the scheme to intensity noise and addressing crosstalk. We identify key subroutines enabled by this unitary construction, including the discrete Fourier transform and the implementation of non-native Hamiltonians. Extending the scheme to two dimensions enables all-to-all atomic rearrangement with a circuit depth that scales sublinearly with the atom number, providing a high-density and highly scalable approach to atom rearrangement. 超导GKP量子比特的逻辑门与读出建模Modeling Logical Gates and Readout of Superconducting Gottesman-Kitaev-Preskill Qubits

针对GKP比特理论噪声模型与实际超导器件条件的脱节，用随机位移信道近似损耗与退相位，分析Clifford门误差扩散并改进解码器，同时提升零差读出效率。

Gottesman-Kitaev-Preskill (GKP) 码是实现容错量子计算的一条激动人心的途径，因为高斯资源和 GKP 泡利本征态制备足以实现通用量子计算。然而，GKP 量子比特在理论上设计用来纠正的噪声模型——均匀随机位移误差——与实际超导器件中影响 GKP 量子比特的条件之间存在脱节：真实噪声信道、逻辑门和低效测量。在这项工作中，我们从三个方面弥合这一差距。首先，我们用随机位移信道近似损耗和退相位对近似 GKP 码态的影响，并证明该近似与数值结果吻合良好。其次，我们分析 GKP Clifford 门的误差扩散性质，并描述在实现每个门之后修改解码器如何能将门的不保真度降低多个数量级。最后，我们考虑零差测量低效对逻辑态读出的影响，并分析一种利用量子轨迹理论提高测量效率的方案。另见14幅图。

Abstract (English)
Optical interfaces that connect long-lived spin qubits to photons are a central requirement for quantum networking and distributed quantum information processing. At present, solid-state atomic defects are leading candidates because of their inherent spin and optical coherence 1,2 . Building on these advancements, synthetically tailored molecular systems represent a fundamental change in the field, using precise atomic control and consistent bottom-up assembly 3,4 . However, a molecular platform that concurrently satisfies the demands of a robust spin–photon interface—namely, the combination of bright fluorescence, high spectral stability and long ground-state spin lifetimes—has yet to be realized at the single-molecule level. Here we show that a triplet ground-state carbene molecule, embedded within a structurally matched host crystal, functions as a robust spin–photon interface with single-molecule addressability. The system exhibits narrow ZPLs, spectral stability over more than an hour, spin-selective optical transitions and single-molecule optically detected magnetic resonance. Coherent control yields millisecond-scale dynamical-decoupling coherence and tens-of-milliseconds spin relaxation at a temperature of 4.5 K. These results establish molecular qubits as a viable platform for single-emitter quantum optics 5,6 while preserving the advantages of bottom-up chemical design 7 and processable materials 8–10 .

Abstract (English)
Silicon is a leading material platform for photonic quantum technologies. However, a key challenge remains the efficient integration of high-performance single-photon sources in the near infrared. Here we present an all-silicon single-photon source at 1217 nm wavelength, fabricated by nanoscale positioning of a single W center in a circular Bragg grating cavity. The operation wavelength corresponds to the W center’s zero-phonon line that is enhanced by the cavity. Under above-gap continuous-wave excitation, we obtain a saturated photon count rate of 1.29 × 10 6 counts/s with pronounced photon antibunching ( g (2) (0) = 0.06 ± 0.02). Triggered operation under weak pulsed excitation produces single photons with 93 ± 2% purity. At saturation, we reveal purity degradation due to repumping processes, that could be mitigated using selective excitation schemes in the future. These results represent a major step towards on-demand sources of indistinguishable single photons in silicon photonics.

Abstract (English)
Realizing photonic quantum computing at scale requires integrated circuits that combine ultra-low loss with fast, low-power, low-hysteresis, and low-crosstalk reconfiguration. These requirements are particularly challenging at visible wavelengths, where many quantum resource-state generators, including single-photon sources and quantum memories, naturally operate. Low-confinement silicon nitride waveguides offer the requisite loss performance, but conventional thermo-optic modulators dissipate significant static power, driving thermal crosstalk and precluding cryogenic operation. Visible-wavelength piezo-optomechanical circuits avoid these drawbacks, but existing demonstrations rely either on high-confinement waveguides with propagation losses of 35-100 dB/m, or on low-confinement platforms using foundry-incompatible lead zirconate titanate (PZT). Here we combine piezo-optomechanical actuation with a CMOS-foundry-fabricated, low-confinement silicon nitride platform, achieving 2.6 dB/m propagation loss at 780 nm, megahertz-scale modulation bandwidth, a half-wave voltage-length product of 2.8 V ⋅ m, and negligible hysteresis. We demonstrate reconfigurable Mach-Zehnder interferometers with 0.63 dB loss per spiral phase shifter, enabling deep, actively reconfigurable visible-wavelength quantum photonic circuits.

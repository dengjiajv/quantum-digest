可解量子多体动力学中的时间复杂性层级Temporal Complexity Hierarchies in Solvable Quantum Many-Body Dynamics

借助几何群论工具，为一类砖墙式量子线路的影响矩阵确立时间纠缠熵的三种不同标度层级，进而区分经典与量子时间关联，并给出量子记忆的操作性度量与实验方案。

影响矩阵（IM）通过将多时关联编码为张量网络态，为刻画非平衡量子多体动力学提供了强有力的框架。理解其计算复杂度与底层动力学之间的关系，无论对理论洞察还是实际应用都至关重要，然而除少数案例研究外，这一问题仍基本未被探索。本文针对从可积到混沌区间的一族砖墙式量子线路研究该问题。借助几何群论的工具，我们识别出时间纠缠熵的三种定性不同的标度行为，从而为这些模型的 IM 精确张量网络表示所需的计算资源建立起层级结构。我们进一步分析 IM 的记忆结构，并区分经典时间关联与量子时间关联。特别地，对某些例子，我们识别出有效经典的 IM，它们允许用高效的蒙特卡罗算法计算多时关联。在更一般、不存在 IM 显式经典描述的情形下，我们引入一种量子记忆的操作性度量并给出相应的实验方案，还讨论了表现出长时真正量子关联的例子。我们的结果在量子多体动力学与群论之间建立了新的联系，为理解 IM 的复杂性及其与动力学物理特性之间的微妙关联提供了新的见解。

Abstract (English)
The influence matrix (IM) provides a powerful framework for characterizing nonequilibrium quantum many-body dynamics by encoding multitime correlations into tensor-network states. Understanding how its computational complexity relates to underlying dynamics is crucial for both theoretical insight and practical utility yet remains largely unexplored despite a few case studies. Here, we address this question for a family of brickwork quantum circuits ranging from integrable to chaotic regimes. Using tools from geometric group theory, we identify three qualitatively distinct scalings of temporal entanglement entropy, establishing a hierarchy of computational resources required for accurate tensor-network representations of the IM for these models. We further analyze the memory structure of the IM and distinguish between classical and quantum temporal correlations. In particular, for certain examples, we identify effectively classical IMs that admit an efficient Monte Carlo algorithm for computing multitime correlations. In more generic settings without an explicit classical description of the IM, we introduce an operational measure of quantum memory with an experimental protocol and discuss examples exhibiting long-time genuinely quantum correlations. Our results establish a new connection between quantum many-body dynamics and group theory, providing fresh insights into the complexity of the IM and its intricate connection to the physical characteristics of the dynamics. 可扩展的量子计算机与量子网络需要将量子处理节点与高效的光-物质界面结合起来，以便在局域或长距离量子网络中分发量子信息。中性原子阵列既可与里德堡态耦合，从而在通用处理架构中实现高保真量子门，也可与光学腔耦合，从而实现与光子之间的界面。然而，将这两种能力结合起来，使原子阵列以光学腔的模式耦合到高激发里德堡态，一直是一个悬而未决的挑战。本文提出一种新型腔耦合里德堡阵列，实现了这一长期目标。我们在可扩展的光镊阵列中制备、探测并控制单个原子，使它们与高精细度光学腔的光学模式强耦合，并以可控方式将它们激发到里德堡态。我们表明，与光学腔的高协同度耦合——通过原子存在时腔共振的色散位移予以展示——以及强里德堡相互作用——通过原子阵列中里德堡耦合的集体增强予以展示——可以在我们的装置中于同一空间位置同时实现。我们提出的实验平台为若干新方向开辟了道路，包括量子网络节点的实现、长程相互作用开放量子系统的量子模拟，以及利用高保真里德堡控制的光子态工程。

Abstract (English)
Scalable quantum computers and quantum networks require the combination of quantum processing nodes with efficient light-matter interfaces to distribute quantum information in local or long-distance quantum networks. Neutral-atom arrays have both been coupled to Rydberg states to enable high-fidelity quantum gates in universal processing architectures, and to optical cavities to realize interfaces to photons. However, combining these two capabilities and coupling atom arrays to highly excited Rydberg states in the mode of an optical cavity has been an outstanding challenge. Here we present a novel cavity-coupled Rydberg array that achieves this long-standing goal. We prepare, detect, and control individual atoms in a scalable optical tweezer array, couple them strongly to the optical mode of a high-finesse optical cavity and excite them in a controlled way to Rydberg states. We show that high-cooperativity coupling to an optical cavity—demonstrated via the dispersive shift of the resonance of the cavity in presence of the atoms—and strong Rydberg interactions—demonstrated via the collective enhancement of Rydberg coupling in the atomic array—can be achieved in our setup at the same spatial location. Our presented experimental platform opens the path to several new directions, including the realization of quantum network nodes, quantum simulation of long-range interacting, open quantum systems, and photonic-state engineering leveraging high-fidelity Rydberg control.

The dynamics of isolated quantum systems following a sudden quench has a central role in many areas of material science, high-energy physics and quantum chemistry1–4. Such far-from-equilibrium quantum dynamics—which features complex phenomena with implications for thermalization, non-equilibrium phase transitions and Floquet phase engineering5–8—is challenging to study numerically, in particular, in high-dimensional systems9,10. Here we use a programmable neutral-atom quantum simulator11 to systematically explore quench dynamics in spin models with up to 180 qubits. By initializing the system in a product state and performing quenches across a broad parameter space, we discover several stable, qualitatively distinct dynamical regimes. Together, these results establish a programmable route to high-energy prethermal phase structure: long-lived dynamical phases, their resonant breakdown and a first-order dynamical phase transition that is not inherited from the equilibrium phase boundaries. A programmable neutral-atom quantum simulator is used to explore quench dynamics in spin models with up to 180 qubits, revealing prethermal dynamical phase transitions and resonant melting.

量子信道在钻石距离度量下的最优学习Optimal learning of quantum channels in diamond distance

Abstract (English)
The evolution of quantum states can be described by quantum channels. The task of estimating an unknown quantum channel, known as process tomography, is a central problem in quantum information theory. A long-standing open question is how many uses of an unknown channel are required to learn it to a precision quantified by the standard metric for distinguishing quantum processes, known as the diamond distance. Here we address this by establishing the query complexity of channel tomography with optimal dependence on the dimensions of the input and output spaces and the channel’s Kraus rank at any fixed constant accuracy in diamond distance. 量子力学相互作用势支持低于解离阈值的局域束缚态，或高于阈值的离域散射态。尽管束缚态在能量上是孤立的，散射态却将量子系统与环境模式耦合，使耗散和损失成为开放系统的内禀性质。连续谱中的束缚态是一个例外，因为相消干涉使其局域在散射连续谱之内。理论预言，这种态可以通过两个耦合到共同连续谱的 Feshbach 共振之间的干涉产生。在这里，我们利用 Floquet 工程相干耦合两个可调 Feshbach 共振，在 6Li 原子的超冷碰撞中形成了这种干涉稳定的态。在临界参数点，与连续谱的弹性和非弹性耦合同时消失，从而在解离阈值之上产生一个分子态。因此，共振散射特征消失，碰撞原子彼此变得有效透明。完整的耦合通道计算和一个最小非厄米模型定量再现了我们的观测，并将该态识别为 Friedrich–Wintgen 连续谱束缚态。这些结果展示了量子干涉如何控制量子物质的开放性，并能够实现非厄米哈密顿量的工程化。

Abstract (English)
Quantum-mechanical interaction potentials support localized bound states below the dissociation threshold or delocalized scattering states above it. Although bound states are energetically isolated, scattering states couple a quantum system to environmental modes, making dissipation and loss intrinsic to open systems. Bound states in the continuum are an exception, as destructive interference keeps them localized within the scattering continuum. Theory predicts that such states can arise through interference between two Feshbach resonances coupled to a common continuum. Here we form such an interference-stabilized state in ultracold collisions of 6 Li atoms by coherently coupling two tunable Feshbach resonances using Floquet engineering. At a critical parameter point, elastic and inelastic coupling to the continuum vanish, producing a molecular state above the dissociation threshold. Consequently, resonant scattering features disappear and the colliding atoms become effectively transparent to one another. Full coupled-channel calculations and a minimal non-Hermitian model quantitatively reproduce our observations and identify the state as a Friedrich–Wintgen bound state in the continuum. These results show how quantum interference can control openness in quantum matter and enable the engineering of non-Hermitian Hamiltonians.

主体间一致性追问的是：同一物理系统的不同观察者在依据共享理论进行推理时，何时会在各自的概率判断上达成一致。经典概率论以一致性定理的形式给出了一个基准 [R. J. Aumann, Ann. Stat. 4, 1236–1239 (1976)]。我们在有限维三体情形下研究经典一致性定理的量子力学（QM）对应，其中两个观察者通过局部投影测量获取信息。我们用共同确定性来刻画他们的相互认知，其形式化表述为关于某一感兴趣事件之概率的无限层确定性算符层级。我们首先证明，若观察者的测量彼此对易且与所关注的性质对易，则共同确定性迫使他们的概率判断相同，从而给出经典一致性定理的一个量子力学对应。随后我们构造了一族单参数的 qutrit–qubit–qubit 模型，其中一个观察者的测量与所关注性质不对易，且会出现对分歧的共同确定性——这是一种量子力学特有的现象。其机制在于：被其中一个观察者（而非另一个观察者）的测量所区分的各分支之间存在相干性。当测量结果被存储于经典寄存器时，由此产生的退相位会消除这种相干性并恢复一致。最后，我们证明量子力学不允许极端的 0-1 构型，即 Alice 确定某性质成立，同时又确定 Bob 确定其不成立。这些结果旨在把量子力学中关于依赖观察者的事实之争，转化为关于量子力学中主体间一致或分歧何时能够得以维持的精确数学条件。

Abstract (English)
Intersubjective consistency asks when different observers of a physical system, reasoning from a shared theory, will agree on their probability assessments. Classical probability theory provides a benchmark in the form of the Agreement Theorem [R. J. Aumann, Ann. Stat. 4 , 1236–1239 (1976)]. We study the quantum mechanics (QM) analog to the classical Agreement Theorem in a finite-dimensional tripartite setting in which two observers obtain information via local projective measurements. We represent their mutual awareness by common certainty, which is formally an infinite hierarchy of certainty operators concerning the probability of an event of interest. We first prove that if the observers’ measurements commute with one another and with the property of interest, common certainty forces their probability assessments to coincide, thereby recovering a QM analog to the classical Agreement Theorem. We then construct a one-parameter family of qutrit–qubit–qubit models in which one observer’s measurement does not commute with the property and common certainty of disagreement occurs—a distinctively QM phenomenon. The mechanism is coherence between branches distinguished by one but not the other observer’s measurement. When measurement outcomes are stored in a classical register, the resulting dephasing removes this coherence and restores agreement. Finally, we prove that QM does not permit the extreme 0 - 1 configuration in which Alice is certain of a property and is also certain that Bob is certain of its negation. These results aim to turn debate in QM about observer-dependent facts into precise mathematical conditions for when intersubjective agreement or disagreement in QM can be sustained.

利用逻辑量子比特实现稀有事件的量子传感Rare-Event Quantum Sensing Using Logical Qubits

★ 编辑推荐

借助量子纠错综合提取过程中的非线性处理，以高阶关联区分稀有信号与噪声，用逻辑相位ϕ_L=O(ε³)记录部分信号并延长相干时间，灵敏度优于常规方案。

本文提出一种利用量子纠错 (QEC) 在噪声环境中探测稀有信号的新协议。该协议的关键特征是：通过 QEC 中综合提取过程所发生的非线性处理，利用不同的高阶关联来区分信号与噪声。在这一方案中，QEC 具有两重作用。第一，它以记录一个被压缩的、随机的逻辑相位 ϕ_L = O(ε³) 为代价，牺牲掉一部分信号 ε。第二，它纠正物理噪声并延长用于信号采集的（逻辑）相干时间。对于在局域马尔可夫噪声背景下随机时刻出现的稀有信号，我们明确证明该方法的灵敏度优于更常规的传感策略。

Abstract (English)
We present a novel protocol to detect rare signals in a noisy environment using quantum error correction (QEC). The key feature of our protocol is the discrimination between signal and noise through distinct higher-order correlations, realized by the nonlinear processing that occurs during syndrome extraction in QEC. In this scheme, QEC has two effects. First, it sacrifices part of the signal ε by recording a reduced, stochastic, logical phase ϕ L = O ( ε 3 ) . Second, it corrects the physical noise and extends the (logical) coherence time for signal acquisition. For rare signals occurring at random times in the presence of local Markovian noise, we explicitly demonstrate an improved sensitivity of our approach over more conventional sensing strategies.

利用毫米波腔实现超导量子比特的高保真读出Millimeter Wave Readout of a Superconducting Qubit

★ Physics 精选

将34.7 GHz毫米波腔与3.1 GHz transmon强耦合，借ω_r/ω_q>10的大失谐抑制驱动引起的寄生跃迁，在超过100光子驱动下以99%以上保真度读出比特态，无需量子极限放大器。

毫米波正逐渐成为一种使能技术，用于连接并增强里德堡原子、光力学和超导量子比特等不同的量子平台。本文探索毫米波光子与常规 transmon 量子比特之间的相互作用，特别是用于量子比特读出。我们研究了一个电路量子电动力学系统，它由频率 ω_r = 2π × 34.7 GHz 的毫米波腔与频率 ω_q = 2π × 3.1 GHz 的 transmon 量子比特组成，二者以速率 g = 2π × 1.3 GHz 耦合。由于腔与量子比特之间失谐如此之大，ω_r/ω_q > 10，我们得以抑制驱动所诱导的不需要的共振态跃迁，从而实现用于量子比特读出的强驱动。即使驱动光子数超过 100，我们也未测到任何共振态跃迁，并以超过 100 个光子读出量子比特态，在无量子极限放大器辅助的情况下实现高于 99% 的测量保真度。

Abstract (English)
Millimeter waves are emerging as an enabling technology for connecting and enhancing different quantum platforms such as Rydberg atoms, optomechanics, and superconducting qubits. In this Letter, we explore the interaction between millimeter wave photons and conventional transmon qubits, specifically for qubit readout. We study a circuit quantum electrodynamic system consisting of a millimeter wave cavity at ω r = 2 π × 34.7 GHz and a transmon qubit at ω q = 2 π × 3.1 GHz coupled at rate g = 2 π × 1.3 GHz . With such a large detuning between cavity and qubit, ω r / ω q > 10 , we are able to suppress drive induced unwanted resonant state transitions, enabling strong drives for qubit readout. We measure no resonant state transitions even when driving with more than 100 photons and read out the qubit state with more than 100 photons to achieve a measurement fidelity greater than 99% without the aid of a quantum limited amplifier.

超越量子比特极限的超导三能级量子链路Superconducting Qutrit Link beyond the Qubit Limit

★ 编辑推荐量子纠缠

在两个独立封装的超导节点间建立微波三能级链路，实现任意三能级态转移，平均保真度83.68%，并重建出负度0.730的远程双三能级态。

超导微波链路已使量子比特之间的确定性态转移与远程纠缠成为可能，但迄今为止，确定性链路所传输的希尔伯特空间实际上仍是二维的。本文演示了两个由微波通道连接的独立封装节点之间的超导三能级链路。每个节点将一个 transmon 三能级系统、一个传输谐振腔和一个可调 Purcell 滤波接口结合在一起，使两个远程微波光子接口在频率与带宽上都能相互匹配。我们实现了两种跃迁选择的光子介导操作，在同一通道的不同时间模式中分别转移 |e⟩ 与 |f⟩ 三能级分量。我们对任意三能级态的转移进行了层析表征，得到平均转移态保真度 83.68% 与三能级过程保真度 77.12%，二者均超过经典三能级转移基准，也超过用等效量子比特通道传输任意三能级态时所能达到的最佳平均保真度。利用部分转移操作，我们重建了负度为 0.730 的远程双三能级态、由层析推断的密集编码容量 2.273 比特，以及由层析推断的 Collins-Gisin-Linden-Massar-Popescu (CGLMP) 参数 I₃ = 2.332，均超出相应的量子比特界限或局域界限。这些结果表明，该超导微波链路把 transmon 固有的三能级结构用作真正的高维通信资源。

Abstract (English)
Superconducting microwave links have enabled deterministic state transfer and remote entanglement between qubits, but deterministic links have so far operated with an effectively two-dimensional transmitted Hilbert space. Here we demonstrate a superconducting qutrit link between two independently packaged nodes connected by a microwave channel. Each node combines a transmon qutrit, a transmission resonator, and a tunable Purcell-filter interface, allowing the two remote microwave-photon interfaces to be matched in both frequency and bandwidth. We implement two transition-selective photon-mediated operations that transfer the | e ⟩ and | f ⟩ qutrit components in distinct temporal modes of the same channel. We tomographically characterize arbitrary qutrit-state transfer, obtaining a mean transferred-state fidelity of 83.68% and a qutrit process fidelity of 77.12%, exceeding both the classical qutrit-transfer benchmark and the best possible average fidelity of an effective qubit channel used to transmit an arbitrary qutrit. Using partial-transfer operations, we reconstruct a remote two-qutrit state with negativity 0.730, a tomography-inferred dense-coding capacity of 2.273 bits, and a tomography-inferred Collins-Gisin-Linden-Massar-Popescu (CGLMP) parameter I 3 = 2.332 , all beyond the corresponding qubit or local bounds. These results demonstrate a superconducting microwave link that uses the native three-level structure of transmons as a genuine high-dimensional communication resource.

用人工设计的光梯度实现离子纠缠Entangling Ions with Engineered Light Gradients

★ 编辑推荐

用与光传播方向正交的含时结构光力驱动几何相位纠缠相互作用，缓解集体运动模式的光谱拥挤，在单个势阱中多达 12 个离子的晶体上实现错误率低于 5×10⁻³ 的两量子比特门。

集体运动模式的光谱拥挤会通过引起对旁观模式的非共振耦合，限制囚禁离子量子处理器中纠缠相互作用的保真度。我们提出一种由横向的、含时结构光力驱动的几何相位纠缠相互作用。通过在与光传播方向正交的平面内施加该力，我们减轻了光谱拥挤的影响，同时保留了单离子寻址能力。只要量子比特态经历差分 AC Stark 频移，该方案就与任意量子比特编码兼容。我们在单个势阱中囚禁多达 12 个离子的离子晶体上实验实现了错误率低于 5 × 10⁻³ 的高保真两量子比特门。这些结果确立了梯度场光频移门作为在光谱拥挤的囚禁离子系统中产生高保真纠缠的可扩展途径。

Abstract (English)
Spectral crowding of collective motional modes limits the fidelity of entangling interactions in trapped-ion quantum processors by inducing off-resonant coupling to spectator modes. We introduce a geometric-phase entangling interaction driven by a transverse, time-dependent structured-light force. By applying the force in a plane orthogonal to the optical propagation direction, we reduce the effects of spectral crowding while preserving single-ion addressing. The scheme is compatible with arbitrary qubit encodings, provided that the qubit states experience a differential AC Stark shift. We experimentally realize high-fidelity two-qubit gates with error rates below 5 × 10 − 3 in ion crystals containing up to 12 ions confined within a single potential well. These results establish gradient-field light-shift gates as a scalable approach to high-fidelity entanglement generation in spectrally crowded trapped-ion systems.

Abstract (English)

对经典场性质进行传感的量子优势Quantum Advantage for Sensing Properties of Classical Fields

量子纠缠

提出量子信号学习框架，仅用双模压缩、无源光学与静态零拍测量即可同时估计经典信号的多种性质并压制散粒噪声，证明其在电磁关联测量、干涉腔反馈控制与傅里叶域匹配滤波等任务上具有指数级或实用量子加速。

现代精密实验常常在量子噪声极限下用玻色传感器探测未知经典场，此时真空涨落限制了常规读出。我们提出量子信号学习——一个将计量学推广到更广泛的性质学习情形的传感框架，并提出一种量子增强协议，能够同时估计经典信号的许多性质，并将散粒噪声抑制到真空水平以下。我们的方案只需要双模压缩、无源光学元件和静态零拍测量，并且能够从同一实验数据集中对许多性质进行经典估计。我们证明该协议可为常见的经典传感任务带来量子加速，包括测量电磁关联、干涉腔的实时反馈控制以及傅里叶域匹配滤波。为确立这些分离，我们引入一种最优输运条件化方法，并证明了相对于所有无纠缠策略的最坏情况指数级分离，以及相对于零拍和外差基准的实用加速。我们进一步表明，当把压缩视为一种资源时，采用压缩光的协议能够比任何相干经典探针以指数级更快的速度感知结构化的经典背景。

Abstract (English)
Modern precision experiments often probe unknown classical fields with bosonic sensors in quantum-noise-limited regimes where vacuum fluctuations limit conventional readout. We introduce quantum signal learning, a sensing framework that extends metrology to a broader property-learning setting, and propose a quantum-enhanced protocol that simultaneously estimates many properties of a classical signal with shot noise suppressed below the vacuum level. Our scheme requires only two-mode squeezing, passive optics, and static homodyne measurements, and enables classical estimation of many properties from the same experimental dataset. We prove that our protocol enables a quantum speedup for common classical sensing tasks, including measuring electromagnetic correlations, real-time feedback control of interferometric cavities, and Fourier-domain matched filtering. To establish these separations, we introduce an optimal-transport conditioning method and show both worst-case exponential separations from all entanglement-free strategies and practical speedups over homodyne and heterodyne baselines. We further show that when squeezing is treated as a resource, a protocol with squeezed light can sense a structured classical background exponentially faster than any coherent classical probe.

Jordan Cotler, Daine L. Danielson, and Ishaan Kannan

对光晶格中超冷原子运动自由度的相干控制，为利用大质量粒子实现可编程量子动力学提供了一条有前景的途径。本文提出并分析了一种实现超冷原子相干重排的方案，它对应于单粒子运动态上的任意幺正变换。利用光学超晶格中的动力学与离散线性光学之间的类比，我们采用 Clements 方案，从双阱阵列中的隧穿与相移系统地构造任意 N 维全局单粒子幺正变换。隧穿被全局控制，而局域操作则通过具有格点分辨能力的势移实现。我们用数值方法研究了该方案对强度噪声与寻址串扰的敏感性。我们指出了这一幺正构造所使能的关键子程序，包括离散傅里叶变换以及非原生哈密顿量的实现。把该方案推广到二维，可实现全连通的原子重排，其线路深度随原子数亚线性增长，从而为原子重排提供一种高密度、高度可扩展的途径。

Abstract (English)
Coherent control of motional degrees of freedom of ultracold atoms in optical lattices offers a promising route toward programmable quantum dynamics with massive particles. We propose and analyze a scheme for implementing coherent rearrangement of ultracold atoms, corresponding to arbitrary unitary transformations on single-particle motional states. Exploiting an analogy between dynamics in optical superlattices and discrete linear optics, we employ the Clements scheme to systematically construct any global N -dimensional single-particle unitary from tunneling and phase shifts in arrays of double wells. Tunneling is controlled globally, while local operations are achieved through site-resolved potential shifts. We numerically investigate the susceptibility of the scheme to intensity noise and addressing crosstalk. We identify key subroutines enabled by this unitary construction, including the discrete Fourier transform and the implementation of non-native Hamiltonians. Extending the scheme to two dimensions enables all-to-all atomic rearrangement with a circuit depth that scales sublinearly with the atom number, providing a high-density and highly scalable approach to atom rearrangement. Gottesman-Kitaev-Preskill (GKP) 码是实现容错量子计算的一条令人振奋的途径，因为高斯资源加上 GKP 泡利本征态的制备就足以实现通用量子计算。然而，GKP 量子比特在理论上被设计用来纠正的噪声模型——均匀随机位移误差——与实际超导器件中影响 GKP 量子比特的条件之间存在脱节：真实噪声信道、逻辑门以及低效率的测量。本文从三个方面弥合这一差距。第一，我们用随机位移信道近似损耗与退相位对近似 GKP 码态的影响，并表明该近似与数值计算吻合良好。第二，我们分析 GKP Clifford 门的误差扩散性质，并说明在每实现一个门之后对解码器作相应修改，可将门失真降低多个数量级。第三，我们考虑零差测量低效率对逻辑态读出的影响，并利用量子轨迹理论分析一种提高测量效率的方案。

In this work we bridge this gap in three ways. Second, we analyze the error-spreading properties of GKP Clifford gates and describe how a modification in the decoder following the implementation of each gate can reduce the gate infidelity by multiple orders of magnitude. Finally, we consider the effect of homodyne measurement inefficiencies on logical state readout and analyze a scheme to improve the measurement efficiency using the theory of quantum trajectories. 利用里德堡阻塞在光镊中快速制备单原子Fast Single-Atom Preparation in Optical Tweezers via Rydberg Blockade

提出利用光镊内里德堡阻塞与自电离，在微秒尺度逐个移除多余原子，把多原子概率压至1%的同时保留58.2%的单原子填充，比光辅助碰撞快两个量级。

可连续补充的光镊阵列将使中性原子量子比特能够支撑无限深度的量子线路。限制这类系统循环时间的一个关键瓶颈，是如何从最初装载了多于一个原子的光镊中移除原子。在传统的光辅助碰撞技术中，缓慢的碰撞动力学把移除多余原子所需的时间尺度限制在数毫秒。本文提出并演示了一种方案，利用光镊内的里德堡阻塞与自电离，在微秒时间尺度上从多原子占据的光镊中选择性地逐个移除原子。我们在 ¹⁷¹Yb 的两种互补机制中演示了该协议。当从基态进行双光子里德堡激发时，我们在 64.8 μs 内把多原子概率降至 1%，同时在 58.2(2)% 的光镊中保留单原子，这与相同实验条件下光辅助碰撞所达到的填充率相当，但速度快两个数量级以上。当从亚稳态 ³P₀ 进行单光子激发时，单原子损耗的降低使填充率提高到 74.8(3)%，代价是需要额外的时间开销把原子制备到 ³P₀。最终填充率受制于一种尚无法解释的两体损耗机制；若能解决该问题，则有望实现快速、准确定性的装载。

Abstract (English)
Continuously replenished optical tweezer arrays will unlock unlimited-depth quantum circuits with neutral atom qubits. A key bottleneck limiting the cycle time of these systems is removing atoms from tweezers initially loaded with more than one atom. In the conventional technique of light-assisted collisions, slow collisional dynamics limit the timescale for removing excess atoms to several milliseconds. Abstract (English)
Silicon is a leading material platform for photonic quantum technologies. However, a key challenge remains the efficient integration of high-performance single-photon sources in the near infrared. Here we present an all-silicon single-photon source at 1217 nm wavelength, fabricated by nanoscale positioning of a single W center in a circular Bragg grating cavity. The operation wavelength corresponds to the W center’s zero-phonon line that is enhanced by the cavity. Under above-gap continuous-wave excitation, we obtain a saturated photon count rate of 1.29 × 10 6 counts/s with pronounced photon antibunching ( g (2) (0) = 0.06 ± 0.02). Triggered operation under weak pulsed excitation produces single photons with 93 ± 2% purity. At saturation, we reveal purity degradation due to repumping processes, that could be mitigated using selective excitation schemes in the future. These results represent a major step towards on-demand sources of indistinguishable single photons in silicon photonics.

面向可见光量子光子电路的超低损耗压电光机低限制氮化硅平台Ultra-low loss piezo-optomechanical low-confinement silicon nitride platform for visible wavelength quantum photonic circuits

将压电光机驱动与 CMOS 代工制造的低限制氮化硅平台结合，在 780 nm 实现 2.6 dB/m 传输损耗、兆赫兹调制带宽、2.8 V·m 半波电压-长度积与可忽略迟滞，并演示每个螺旋相移器损耗仅 0.63 dB 的可重构马赫-曾德尔干涉仪。

实现规模化的光子量子计算需要兼具超低损耗与快速、低功耗、低迟滞、低串扰重构能力的集成电路。这些要求在可见光波段尤为具有挑战性，因为许多量子资源态产生器（包括单光子源和量子存储器）天然工作在可见光波段。低限制氮化硅波导提供了所需的损耗性能，但常规热光调制器会耗散大量静态功率，导致热串扰并妨碍低温运行。可见光波段的压电光机电路避免了这些缺点，但已有的演示要么依赖传输损耗为 35–100 dB/m 的高限制波导，要么依赖使用与代工厂不兼容的锆钛酸铅（PZT）的低限制平台。在此我们将压电光机驱动与 CMOS 代工厂制造的低限制氮化硅平台相结合，在 780 nm 处实现 2.6 dB/m 的传输损耗、兆赫兹量级的调制带宽、2.8 V·m 的半波电压-长度积以及可忽略的迟滞。我们演示了每个螺旋相移器损耗为 0.63 dB 的可重构马赫-曾德尔干涉仪，从而实现了深度主动可重构的可见光波段量子光子电路。

Abstract (English)
Realizing photonic quantum computing at scale requires integrated circuits that combine ultra-low loss with fast, low-power, low-hysteresis, and low-crosstalk reconfiguration. These requirements are particularly challenging at visible wavelengths, where many quantum resource-state generators, including single-photon sources and quantum memories, naturally operate. Low-confinement silicon nitride waveguides offer the requisite loss performance, but conventional thermo-optic modulators dissipate significant static power, driving thermal crosstalk and precluding cryogenic operation. Visible-wavelength piezo-optomechanical circuits avoid these drawbacks, but existing demonstrations rely either on high-confinement waveguides with propagation losses of 35-100 dB/m, or on low-confinement platforms using foundry-incompatible lead zirconate titanate (PZT). Here we combine piezo-optomechanical actuation with a CMOS-foundry-fabricated, low-confinement silicon nitride platform, achieving 2.6 dB/m propagation loss at 780 nm, megahertz-scale modulation bandwidth, a half-wave voltage-length product of 2.8 V ⋅ m, and negligible hysteresis. We demonstrate reconfigurable Mach-Zehnder interferometers with 0.63 dB loss per spiral phase shifter, enabling deep, actively reconfigurable visible-wavelength quantum photonic circuits.

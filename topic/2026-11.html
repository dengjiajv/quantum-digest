超越量子比特极限的超导三能级量子链路Superconducting Qutrit Link beyond the Qubit Limit

★ 编辑推荐量子纠缠

在两个独立封装的节点间构建超导三能级量子链路，用同一信道的不同时间模式分别传递 |e⟩ 与 |f⟩ 分量，任意三能级态转移平均保真度达 83.68%，并重建出超越量子比特界限的远程纠缠、密集编码容量与 CGLMP 参数。

超导微波链路已实现量子比特之间的确定性态转移与远程纠缠，但确定性链路迄今只利用了有效二维的传输希尔伯特空间。这里我们展示一条连接两个独立封装节点、由微波信道相连的超导三能级量子链路。每个节点将 transmon 三能级系统、传输谐振腔和可调 Purcell 滤波接口结合在一起，使两个远程微波光子接口在频率和带宽上都能匹配。我们实现了两种跃迁选择性、光子介导的操作，在同一信道的不同时间模式中分别转移 |e⟩ 和 |f⟩ 三能级分量。我们对任意三能级态的转移进行了层析表征，得到平均转移态保真度 83.68% 和三能级过程保真度 77.12%，二者均超过经典三能级转移基准，也超过用有效量子比特信道传输任意三能级态所能达到的最佳平均保真度。利用部分转移操作，我们重建出负度为 0.730 的远程双三能级态、由层析推断的密集编码容量 2.273 比特，以及由层析推断的 Collins-Gisin-Linden-Massar-Popescu（CGLMP）参数 I_3 = 2.332，全部超越相应的量子比特界限或局域界限。这些结果表明，该超导微波链路把 transmon 固有的三能级结构用作真正的高维通信资源。

Abstract (English)
Superconducting microwave links have enabled deterministic state transfer and remote entanglement between qubits, but deterministic links have so far operated with an effectively two-dimensional transmitted Hilbert space. Here we demonstrate a superconducting qutrit link between two independently packaged nodes connected by a microwave channel. Each node combines a transmon qutrit, a transmission resonator, and a tunable Purcell-filter interface, allowing the two remote microwave-photon interfaces to be matched in both frequency and bandwidth. We implement two transition-selective photon-mediated operations that transfer the | e ⟩ and | f ⟩ qutrit components in distinct temporal modes of the same channel. We tomographically characterize arbitrary qutrit-state transfer, obtaining a mean transferred-state fidelity of 83.68% and a qutrit process fidelity of 77.12%, exceeding both the classical qutrit-transfer benchmark and the best possible average fidelity of an effective qubit channel used to transmit an arbitrary qutrit. Using partial-transfer operations, we reconstruct a remote two-qutrit state with negativity 0.730, a tomography-inferred dense-coding capacity of 2.273 bits, and a tomography-inferred Collins-Gisin-Linden-Massar-Popescu (CGLMP) parameter I 3 = 2.332 , all beyond the corresponding qubit or local bounds. These results demonstrate a superconducting microwave link that uses the native three-level structure of transmons as a genuine high-dimensional communication resource.

Xiang Li, Zheng-Yang Mei, Yang He, Si-Lu Zhao, Yan-Jun Liu, Xiao-Hui Song, Kai Xu, Zhong-Cheng Xiang, Dong-Ning Zheng, and Heng Fan

玻色极化子作为相对论 Unruh-DeWitt 探测器Bose Polarons as Relativistic Unruh-DeWitt Detectors: Entanglement Harvesting from Bose-Einstein Condensates

★ 编辑推荐量子纠缠

证明玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚中的束缚杂质可直接映射为与相对论量子场耦合的 Unruh-DeWitt 探测器，并给出 K-41 杂质经有限时间 Feshbach 调节耦合 Rb-87 凝聚的实验参数；据此找到从凝聚遥远区域提取真空纠缠的可行条件。

我们证明，玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚中的束缚杂质可以直接映射为与相对论量子场相互作用的 Unruh-DeWitt 探测器。我们给出了利用 K-41 杂质通过有限时间 Feshbach 调节与 Rb-87 凝聚耦合的实验实现参数。作为一个应用，我们研究了从凝聚的遥远区域提取真空纠缠，并找到了实现纠缠收获的可行参数。

Abstract (English)
We show that a bound impurity in a Bose-Einstein condensate can be directly mapped to an Unruh-DeWitt detector interacting with a relativistic quantum field. We provide explicit experimental parameters for an implementation using K 41 impurities coupled to a Rb 87 condensate via finite-time Feshbach tuning. As an application, we study the extraction of vacuum entanglement from distant regions of the condensate and find viable parameters for the implementation of entanglement harvesting.

T. Rick Perche, Francesco Gozzini, and Markus K. Oberthaler
Phys. Rev. Lett. 137, 170401 (2026)

量子信号学习：传感经典场性质的量子优势Quantum Advantage for Sensing Properties of Classical Fields

量子纠缠

提出量子信号学习框架及仅需双模压缩、无源光学和静态零拍测量的量子增强方案，可同时估计经典信号的多个属性，并把散粒噪声压至真空水平以下，证明其在电磁关联测量、干涉腔实时反馈与傅里叶域匹配滤波等任务上相对无纠缠策略具有指数级优势。

现代精密实验常用玻色子传感器在量子噪声极限区探测未知经典场，此时真空涨落限制了常规读出。我们提出量子信号学习——一种将计量学推广到更广泛的属性学习情境的传感框架，并提出一种量子增强方案，可同时估计经典信号的许多属性，且散粒噪声被压制到真空水平以下。我们的方案只需双模压缩、无源光学和静态零拍测量，并能够从同一实验数据集事后对许多属性进行经典估计。我们证明该方案可为常见经典传感任务带来量子加速，包括测量电磁关联、对干涉腔进行实时反馈控制以及傅里叶域匹配滤波。为确立这些分离，我们引入一种最优输运条件化方法，并证明相对于所有无纠缠策略的最坏情况指数级分离，以及对零拍和外差基线的实用加速。我们进一步表明，当把压缩视为资源时，使用压缩光的方案能比任何相干经典探针指数级更快地传感结构化经典背景。

Abstract (English)
Modern precision experiments often probe unknown classical fields with bosonic sensors in quantum-noise-limited regimes where vacuum fluctuations limit conventional readout. We introduce quantum signal learning, a sensing framework that extends metrology to a broader property-learning setting, and propose a quantum-enhanced protocol that simultaneously estimates many properties of a classical signal with shot noise suppressed below the vacuum level. Our scheme requires only two-mode squeezing, passive optics, and static homodyne measurements, and enables post hoc classical estimation of many properties from the same experimental dataset. We prove that our protocol enables a quantum speedup for common classical sensing tasks, including measuring electromagnetic correlations, real-time feedback control of interferometric cavities, and Fourier-domain matched filtering. To establish these separations, we introduce an optimal-transport conditioning method and show both worst-case exponential separations from all entanglement-free strategies and practical speedups over homodyne and heterodyne baselines. We further show that when squeezing is treated as a resource, a protocol with squeezed light can sense a structured classical background exponentially faster than any coherent classical probe.

Jordan Cotler, Daine L. Danielson, and Ishaan Kannan
Phys. Rev. X 16, 041025 (2026)
10.1103/6ps1-mw9l

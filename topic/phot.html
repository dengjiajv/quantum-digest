通过微腔中色心的纳米级定位实现硅基明亮纯净单光子源Bright and pure single-photon source in silicon by nanoscale positioning of a color center in a microcavity

集成光子

在圆形布拉格光栅微腔中纳米级定位单个 W 色心，制成 1217 nm 全硅单光子源，饱和计数率达 1.29×10^6 counts/s，g^(2)(0)=0.06±0.02，脉冲激发下纯度 93%±2%。

硅是光子量子技术的主要材料平台。然而，在近红外波段高效集成高性能单光子源仍是一项关键挑战。在此，我们展示了一个波长为 1217 nm 的全硅单光子源，它通过将单个 W 色心纳米级定位在圆形布拉格光栅腔中制备而成。工作波长对应于被腔增强的 W 色心零声子线。在带隙以上连续波激发下，我们获得了 1.29×10^6 counts/s 的饱和光子计数率，并伴有显著的光子反聚束（g^(2)(0)=0.06±0.02）。在弱脉冲激发下进行触发操作，产生的单光子纯度为 93±2%。在饱和条件下，我们揭示了由再抽运过程引起的纯度下降，未来可利用选择性激发方案加以缓解。这些结果代表着向硅光子学中按需不可区分单光子源迈出的重要一步。

Abstract (English)
Silicon is a leading material platform for photonic quantum technologies. However, a key challenge remains the efficient integration of high-performance single-photon sources in the near infrared. Here we present an all-silicon single-photon source at 1217 nm wavelength, fabricated by nanoscale positioning of a single W center in a circular Bragg grating cavity. The operation wavelength corresponds to the W center’s zero-phonon line that is enhanced by the cavity. Under above-gap continuous-wave excitation, we obtain a saturated photon count rate of 1.29 × 10 6 counts/s with pronounced photon antibunching ( g (2) (0) = 0.06 ± 0.02). Triggered operation under weak pulsed excitation produces single photons with 93 ± 2% purity. At saturation, we reveal purity degradation due to repumping processes, that could be mitigated using selective excitation schemes in the future. These results represent a major step towards on-demand sources of indistinguishable single photons in silicon photonics.

Baptiste Lefaucher, Yoann Baron, Jean-Baptiste Jager, Vincent Calvo, Christian Elsässer, Giuliano Coppola, Frédéric Mazen, Sébastien Kerdilès, Félix Cache, Anaïs Dréau, Jean-Michel Gérard

No. 02

面向可见光量子光子电路的超低损耗压电光机低限制氮化硅平台Ultra-low loss piezo-optomechanical low-confinement silicon nitride platform for visible wavelength quantum photonic circuits

集成光子

把压电光机驱动与 CMOS 代工的低限制氮化硅平台结合，在 780 nm 实现 2.6 dB/m 传输损耗、兆赫兹调制带宽与可忽略迟滞，并演示了深度可重构的可见光量子光子线路。

实现规模化光子量子计算需要把超低损耗与快速、低功耗、低迟滞、低串扰的可重构性结合起来的集成电路。这些要求在可见光波段尤其具有挑战性，因为许多量子资源态发生器（包括单光子源与量子存储器）天然工作在可见光波段。低限制氮化硅波导提供了所需的损耗性能，但传统热光调制器耗散显著的静态功率，导致热串扰并排除低温运行的可能。可见光波段的压电光机线路避免了这些缺点，但已有的演示要么依赖传输损耗为 35–100 dB/m 的高限制波导，要么依赖使用与代工不兼容的锆钛酸铅（PZT）的低限制平台。这里我们把压电光机驱动与 CMOS 代工制造的低限制氮化硅平台相结合，在 780 nm 处实现 2.6 dB/m 的传输损耗、兆赫兹量级的调制带宽、2.8 V·m 的半波电压–长度积以及可忽略的迟滞。我们演示了每个螺旋相移器损耗为 0.63 dB 的可重构马赫–曾德尔干涉仪，从而实现深度、可主动重构的可见光波段量子光子线路。

Abstract (English)
Realizing photonic quantum computing at scale requires integrated circuits that combine ultra-low loss with fast, low-power, low-hysteresis, and low-crosstalk reconfiguration. These requirements are particularly challenging at visible wavelengths, where many quantum resource-state generators, including single-photon sources and quantum memories, naturally operate. Low-confinement silicon nitride waveguides offer the requisite loss performance, but conventional thermo-optic modulators dissipate significant static power, driving thermal crosstalk and precluding cryogenic operation. Visible-wavelength piezo-optomechanical circuits avoid these drawbacks, but existing demonstrations rely either on high-confinement waveguides with propagation losses of 35-100 dB/m, or on low-confinement platforms using foundry-incompatible lead zirconate titanate (PZT). Here we combine piezo-optomechanical actuation with a CMOS-foundry-fabricated, low-confinement silicon nitride platform, achieving 2.6 dB/m propagation loss at 780 nm, megahertz-scale modulation bandwidth, a half-wave voltage-length product of 2.8 V ⋅ m, and negligible hysteresis. We demonstrate reconfigurable Mach-Zehnder interferometers with 0.63 dB loss per spiral phase shifter, enabling deep, actively reconfigurable visible-wavelength quantum photonic circuits.

Mayank Mishra, Gwangho Choi, Wenhua He, Gina M. Talcott, Katherine Kearney, Michael Gehl, Andrew Leenheer, Daniel Dominguez, Nils T. Otterstrom, Matt Eichenfield
Nat. Commun. (2026)
10.1038/s41467-026-77969-0

No. 03

克莱因瓶拓扑光子电路中的非厄米奇异拓扑Non-Hermitian exceptional topology on a Klein bottle photonic circuit

集成光子

在可重构硅光芯片上实现定义于克莱因瓶这一不可定向参数空间的两带非厄米哈密顿量，测得费米弧取向表明存在无法局域湮灭的同号奇异点，即奇异点单极。

非厄米物理揭示了厄米体系无法企及的大量非常规波动现象，其中奇异点（EPs）驱动着增强的灵敏度、非互易输运以及非厄米简并所独有的拓扑行为。在此，我们展示了一种可扩展、可重构的硅基光子集成电路，能够高精度地模拟任意非厄米时间演化。利用这一可编程平台，我们实现了定义在克莱因瓶拓扑上的两带非厄米哈密顿量——克莱因瓶是一种不可定向的参数空间，它允许在可定向流形上被禁止的奇异相。通过片上振幅-相位重构协议，我们在参数空间的多个点上恢复了完整的复哈密顿量，并在实验上标绘出虚本征值能隙闭合处所对应的费米弧。所测费米弧的取向揭示出非平凡的奇异拓扑：它意味着存在无法在克莱因瓶上局域湮灭的同号奇异点（或奇异点单极）。我们的结果首次在光子系统中实现了不可定向流形上的奇异拓扑，并为探索与经典和量子光子学相关的奇异非厄米与拓扑模型建立了通用平台。

Abstract (English)
Non-Hermitian physics has unlocked a wealth of unconventional wave phenomena beyond the reach of Hermitian systems, with exceptional points (EPs) driving enhanced sensitivity, nonreciprocal transport, and topological behavior unique to non-Hermitian degeneracies. Here, we present a scalable and reconfigurable silicon photonic integrated circuit capable of emulating arbitrary non-Hermitian time evolution with high precision. Using this programmable platform, we implement a two-band non-Hermitian Hamiltonian defined on a Klein-bottle topology, a nonorientable parameter space that enables exceptional phases forbidden on orientable manifolds. Through an on-chip amplitude-and-phase reconstruction protocol, we retrieve the full complex Hamiltonian at multiple points in parameter space and experimentally map the associated Fermi arc where the imaginary eigenvalue gap closes. The orientation of the measured Fermi arc reveals a nontrivial exceptional topology: it implies the presence of same-charge EPs (or an EP monopole) that cannot annihilate locally on the Klein bottle. Our results demonstrate the first photonic realization of exceptional topology on a nonorientable manifold and establish a versatile platform for exploring exotic non-Hermitian and topological models relevant to classical and quantum photonics.

Ze-Sheng Xu, J. Lukas K. König, Andrea Cataldo, Rohan Yadgirkar, Govind Krishna, Venkatesh Deenadayalan, Val Zwiller, Stefan Preble, Emil J. Bergholtz, Jun Gao, Ali W. Elshaari
Light Sci. Appl. 15 (2026)
10.1038/s41377-026-02516-x

基于金刚石色心的量子网络：原理、进展与展望Quantum networks using color defects in diamond: principles, progress, and perspectives

量子密码集成光子

综述金刚石色心的光学与自旋性质，梳理量子网络各构建模块的近期进展与城际尺度网络演示，并讨论基础与实验层面的挑战及可能的解决途径。

大规模量子网络将在量子通信、分布式量子计算、传感与计量等领域实现量子信息科学全新的应用。要构建这类网络的节点，金刚石色心是最有希望的候选者之一。它们优异的光学性质、快速的自旋量子比特操控和长的自旋相干时间，使其非常适合量子信息处理与量子存储应用。此外，金刚石纳米光子结构与光子集成电路异质集成方面的最新进展，使这些系统更加高效，并更适合可扩展的量子处理器架构。在这篇综合性综述中，我们讨论这些系统的光学与自旋性质、量子网络各构建模块的近期进展、城域网尺度量子网络的演示，以及这些系统在基础与实验层面所面临的挑战和可能的解决方案。

Abstract (English)
Large-scale quantum networks will enable entirely new applications of quantum information science in fields such as quantum communication, distributed quantum computing, sensing, and metrology. To build nodes of such networks, diamond color defects are one of the promising candidates. Their excellent optical properties, fast spin-qubit control, and long spin coherence times make them well suited for quantum information processing and quantum memory applications. Additionally, recent advances in the heterogeneous integration of diamond nanophotonic structures with photonic integrated circuits have made these systems more efficient and well suited for scalable quantum processor architectures. In this comprehensive review, we discuss the optical and spin properties of these systems, recent progress in the building blocks of quantum networks, and demonstrations of metropolitan-scale quantum networks, as well as the challenges associated with these systems at both the fundamental and experimental levels, along with potential solutions.

Ayan Majumder, Cem Güney Torun, Tim Schröder, Gregor Pieplow, Prem Kumar, Kasturi Saha
Optica 13, 2319 (2026)
10.1364/optica.607038

芯片集成微谐振腔孤子光频梳的阿秒级同步Attosecond-level synchronization of chip-integrated microresonator soliton combs

集成光子

芯片微谐振腔孤子光频梳接收光纤双音定时参考，借克尔非线性在25或300 GHz重频下实现低于400 as的积分相对定时抖动，无需主动稳定。

同步激光振荡器对于探测化学、材料科学和生物学中最快的过程（直至阿秒时间尺度）至关重要。在自由电子激光器或射电望远镜等科学设施中，紧密同步也至关重要，并且与多节点网络中的通信和信息技术日益相关。当前基于锁模激光器的同步方法能够达到所需性能，但其复杂性、成本和尺寸阻碍了在多节点网络中的部署。在此，我们展示了工作于25 GHz或300 GHz脉冲重复频率的芯片集成微谐振腔孤子光频梳之间的阿秒级同步。为实现同步，每个振荡器通过光纤接收一对连续波激光器作为双音定时参考。这些激光器为微梳提供功率，克尔非线性同步使积分相对定时抖动低于400 as（1 kHz至1 MHz），且无需任何主动稳定。该方法可为大型设施、数据中心、解耦计算、导航和量子网络提供可扩展的精密定时；最终，它可能催生芯片集成阿秒光子学。

Abstract (English)
Synchronized laser oscillators are essential for probing the fastest processes in chemistry, materials science, and biology down to attosecond timescales. Tight synchronization is also crucial at scientific facilities such as free-electron lasers or radio telescopes and is increasingly relevant to communication and information technologies in multi-node networks. Current synchronization approaches based on mode-locked lasers achieve the required performance, but their complexity, cost, and size hinder deployment in multi-node networks. Here, we demonstrate attosecond-level synchronization between chip-integrated microresonator soliton combs operating at either 25 or 300 GHz pulse repetition rate. For synchronization, each oscillator receives over fiber a pair of continuous-wave lasers as a two-tone timing reference. The lasers power the microcombs, and Kerr-nonlinear synchronization results in integrated relative timing jitter below 400 as (1 kHz to 1 MHz), without any active stabilization. This approach enables scalable precision timing for large facilities, data centers, disaggregated computing, navigation, and quantum networks; ultimately, it may lead to chip-integrated attosecond photonics.

Alexander E. Ulanov, Bastian Ruhnke, Theia El Sharkawy, Thibault Wildi, Kemal Şafak, Franz X. Kärtner, Tobias Herr
Optica 13, 2312 (2026)
10.1364/optica.605393

No. 06

面向可见光集成光子学的零静态功耗紧凑可扩展相位调制器Compact scalable phase modulator with zero static power consumption for visible integrated photonics

集成光子

在 Si₃N₄ 波导上叠加氧化铟锡电容结构，利用电荷积累改变折射率，实现可见光波段 VπL 仅 0.5 V·cm、静态功耗为零的紧凑相位调制器，并给出带宽与半波电压的折中关系。

可见光波段的光调制器从生物光子学到量子科学都有深远的应用。在绝缘体上硅波导平台上实现此类光相位调制器，此前主要局限于利用热光效应、液晶技术以及压电-光机械效应，而这些方法在调制速度与调制效率上都受到限制。在此，我们报道了据我们所知首个在可见光波段高度可扩展的紧凑相位调制器，它通过电荷积累效应改变位于 Si₃N₄ 波导之上的氧化铟锡电容叠层的折射率。该相位调制器架构在零静态功耗下实现了0.5 V·cm的调制效率 VπL。带宽与 Vπ 成反比：对于400 µm长的调制器，最小 Vπ 值为12.4±0.4 V，相应的相位调制器损耗为2.5 dB；而对于10 µm长的调制器，最高 −3 dB 带宽为150 MHz，相应的相位调制器损耗为0.06 dB。通过提升可见光波段相位调制器的能效与带宽，这项工作使得从生物技术到量子传感与计算的大规模、高速集成光子系统的实现成为可能。

Abstract (English)
Optical modulators in the visible regime have far-reaching applications from biophotonics to quantum science. Implementations of such optical phase modulators on a silicon-on-insulator waveguide platform have been mainly limited to utilization of the thermo-optic effect, liquid crystal technology, as well as piezo-optomechanical effects which are limited in modulation speed and modulation efficiency. Here, we report, to our knowledge, the demonstration of the first highly scalable compact phase modulator in the visible regime based on altering the refractive index of an indium-tin oxide capacitive stack over a Si 3 N 4 waveguide through the charge accumulation effect. The phase modulator architecture achieves a modulation efficiency, V π L, of 0.5V⋅cm at a zero static power consumption. Bandwidth and V π scale inversely, with the minimum V π value being 12.4±0.4V for a 400 µm long modulator and a corresponding phase modulator loss of 2.5 dB, and the highest −3dB bandwidth being 150 MHz for a 10 µm long modulator with a corresponding phase modulator loss of 0.06 dB. By enhancing the energy efficiency and bandwidth of phase modulators in the visible regime, this work enables implementation of large-scale and high-speed integrated photonic systems with applications from biotechnology to quantum sensing and computing.

Neil MacFarlane, Firooz Aflatouni

No. 02

基于人工光梯度的囚禁离子纠缠Entangling Ions with Engineered Light Gradients

★ 编辑推荐量子纠缠

针对集体运动模式光谱拥挤导致的旁观模式非共振耦合，提出由横向、含时结构光力驱动的几何相位纠缠方案，在保留单离子寻址能力的同时降低光谱拥挤影响；在最多含 12 个离子的晶体中实现错误率低于 5×10^-3 的双量子比特门。

集体运动模式的光谱拥挤会对旁观模式产生非共振耦合，从而限制囚禁离子量子处理器中纠缠相互作用的保真度。我们引入一种由横向、含时结构光力驱动的几何相位纠缠相互作用。通过在与光传播方向正交的平面内施加该力，我们在保留单离子寻址能力的同时降低了光谱拥挤的影响。只要量子比特态经历差分 AC Stark 频移，该方案就与任意量子比特编码兼容。我们在单个势阱中最多包含 12 个离子的离子晶体上实验实现了错误率低于 5 × 10^-3 的高保真双量子比特门。这些结果确立了梯度场光频移门作为在光谱拥挤的囚禁离子系统中产生高保真纠缠的可扩展途径。

Abstract (English)
Spectral crowding of collective motional modes limits the fidelity of entangling interactions in trapped-ion quantum processors by inducing off-resonant coupling to spectator modes. We introduce a geometric-phase entangling interaction driven by a transverse, time-dependent structured-light force. By applying the force in a plane orthogonal to the optical propagation direction, we reduce the effects of spectral crowding while preserving single-ion addressing. The scheme is compatible with arbitrary qubit encodings, provided that the qubit states experience a differential AC Stark shift. We experimentally realize high-fidelity two-qubit gates with error rates below 5 × 10 − 3 in ion crystals containing up to 12 ions confined within a single potential well. These results establish gradient-field light-shift gates as a scalable approach to high-fidelity entanglement generation in spectrally crowded trapped-ion systems.

Tommaso Faorlin, Lorenz Panzl, Phoebe Grosser, Pablo Viñas, Alan Kahan, Walter Joseph Hörmann, Yannick Weiser, Giovanni Cerchiari, Thomas Feldker, Alexander Erhard, Georg Jacob, Juris Ulmanis, Rainer Blatt, Alejandro Bermudez, and Thomas Monz
Phys. Rev. Lett. 137, 170601 (2026)
10.1103/4smy-6w2n

No. 03

玻色极化子作为相对论 Unruh-DeWitt 探测器Bose Polarons as Relativistic Unruh-DeWitt Detectors: Entanglement Harvesting from Bose-Einstein Condensates

★ 编辑推荐量子纠缠

证明玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚中的束缚杂质可直接映射为与相对论量子场耦合的 Unruh-DeWitt 探测器，并给出 K-41 杂质经有限时间 Feshbach 调节耦合 Rb-87 凝聚的实验参数；据此找到从凝聚遥远区域提取真空纠缠的可行条件。

我们证明，玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚中的束缚杂质可以直接映射为与相对论量子场相互作用的 Unruh-DeWitt 探测器。我们给出了利用 K-41 杂质通过有限时间 Feshbach 调节与 Rb-87 凝聚耦合的实验实现参数。作为一个应用，我们研究了从凝聚的遥远区域提取真空纠缠，并找到了实现纠缠收获的可行参数。

Abstract (English)
We show that a bound impurity in a Bose-Einstein condensate can be directly mapped to an Unruh-DeWitt detector interacting with a relativistic quantum field. We provide explicit experimental parameters for an implementation using K 41 impurities coupled to a Rb 87 condensate via finite-time Feshbach tuning. As an application, we study the extraction of vacuum entanglement from distant regions of the condensate and find viable parameters for the implementation of entanglement harvesting.

T. Rick Perche, Francesco Gozzini, and Markus K. Oberthaler
Phys. Rev. Lett. 137, 170401 (2026)
10.1103/54t2-pmf8

No. 04

量子信号学习：传感经典场性质的量子优势Quantum Advantage for Sensing Properties of Classical Fields

量子纠缠

提出量子信号学习框架及仅需双模压缩、无源光学和静态零拍测量的量子增强方案，可同时估计经典信号的多个属性，并把散粒噪声压至真空水平以下，证明其在电磁关联测量、干涉腔实时反馈与傅里叶域匹配滤波等任务上相对无纠缠策略具有指数级优势。

现代精密实验常用玻色子传感器在量子噪声极限区探测未知经典场，此时真空涨落限制了常规读出。我们提出量子信号学习——一种将计量学推广到更广泛的属性学习情境的传感框架，并提出一种量子增强方案，可同时估计经典信号的许多属性，且散粒噪声被压制到真空水平以下。我们的方案只需双模压缩、无源光学和静态零拍测量，并能够从同一实验数据集事后对许多属性进行经典估计。我们证明该方案可为常见经典传感任务带来量子加速，包括测量电磁关联、对干涉腔进行实时反馈控制以及傅里叶域匹配滤波。为确立这些分离，我们引入一种最优输运条件化方法，并证明相对于所有无纠缠策略的最坏情况指数级分离，以及对零拍和外差基线的实用加速。我们进一步表明，当把压缩视为资源时，使用压缩光的方案能比任何相干经典探针指数级更快地传感结构化经典背景。

Abstract (English)
Modern precision experiments often probe unknown classical fields with bosonic sensors in quantum-noise-limited regimes where vacuum fluctuations limit conventional readout. We introduce quantum signal learning, a sensing framework that extends metrology to a broader property-learning setting, and propose a quantum-enhanced protocol that simultaneously estimates many properties of a classical signal with shot noise suppressed below the vacuum level. Our scheme requires only two-mode squeezing, passive optics, and static homodyne measurements, and enables post hoc classical estimation of many properties from the same experimental dataset. We prove that our protocol enables a quantum speedup for common classical sensing tasks, including measuring electromagnetic correlations, real-time feedback control of interferometric cavities, and Fourier-domain matched filtering. To establish these separations, we introduce an optimal-transport conditioning method and show both worst-case exponential separations from all entanglement-free strategies and practical speedups over homodyne and heterodyne baselines. We further show that when squeezing is treated as a resource, a protocol with squeezed light can sense a structured classical background exponentially faster than any coherent classical probe.

Jordan Cotler, Daine L. Danielson, and Ishaan Kannan
Phys. Rev. X 16, 041025 (2026)
10.1103/6ps1-mw9l

No. 05

直接产生通信波段自旋—光子纠缠的贝尔非定域性Bell nonlocality with directly generated telecom-band spin–photon entanglement

量子纠缠

用铷原子 1530 nm 偶极跃迁加腔辅助，从单个原子直接发出 C 波段通信光子，得到保真度 91.4% 的自旋—光子纠缠，并以 2.455(77) > 2 的贝尔不等式违背验证了非定域性。

量子非定域性通常通过量子网络中的纠缠分发来体现，是量子信息科学的基石。要实现纠缠的长距离分发，信息载体（即飞行光子）必须工作在光纤损耗最低的通信波段。尽管已有大量工作致力于在 C 波段通信光子与各类静止自旋之间直接产生纠缠，量子非定域性的验证仍是一项突出的挑战。本文利用铷原子中波长为 1530 nm 的偶极跃迁并结合腔辅助方案，实现了单个原子对 C 波段通信光子的共振激发与直接发射，产生自旋—光子纠缠，测得的贝尔态保真度超过 91.4%。随后，我们利用这对高质量纠缠对，观测到 2.455(77) > 2 的贝尔不等式违背，从而验证了贝尔非定域性。这些结果将单原子量子发射体的波长拓展至通信 C 波段，获得了足够高保真度的自旋—光子纠缠，最终实现了贝尔非定域性的验证。

Abstract (English)
Quantum nonlocality, typically revealed through entanglement distribution across quantum networks, is a cornerstone of quantum information science. Long-distance distribution of entanglement requires the information carrier, i.e., flying photons, to operate in the minimum-loss telecom band of optical fiber. While extensive efforts have been devoted to the direct generation of entanglement between C-band telecom photons and various stationary spins, the verification of quantum nonlocality remains an outstanding challenge. Here, utilizing a dipole transition in rubidium atoms with a wavelength of 1530 nm and a cavity-assisted protocol, we achieve resonant excitation and direct emission of C-band telecom photons from a single atom, generating spin–photon entanglement with a measured Bell state fidelity exceeding 91.4%. We then verify Bell nonlocality by observing a Bell inequality violation of 2.455(77) > 2 using this high-quality entangled pair. These results extend the wavelength of a single-atom quantum emitter to the telecom C-band, achieving sufficiently high-fidelity spin–photon entanglement to finally verify Bell nonlocality.

Dong-Yu Huang, Jian Wang, Xiao-Long Zhou, Ze-Min Shen, Si-Jian He, Qi-Yang Huang, Yi-Jia Liu, Yu-Shu Chen, Quan Jiang, Chuan-Feng Li, Guang-Can Guo
Nat. Commun. (2026)
10.1038/s41467-026-79099-z

No. 06

利用真空—单光子数叠加态违背贝尔不等式Bell inequality violation with vacuum–one-photon number superposition states

量子纠缠

单个量子点产生的真空—单光子数叠加态经分束器离域化，后选择制备出时间模式纠缠，并用Franson型干涉仪观测到CHSH贝尔不等式的明显违背。

纠缠是量子技术的核心资源，而光子纠缠的实现必然依赖于与物质的相互作用。共振荧光源于驱动场与二能级系统之间的相干相互作用，在量子光学中起着关键作用。在此，我们利用来自单个量子点的真空—单光子数叠加态，实验演示了后选择时间模式纠缠的高质量产生；这一方法在概念上与基于全同单光子的早期方案一致，但此前一直缺乏高质量的实验实现。通过分束器使该叠加态离域化，我们实现了后选择的时间模式纠缠，并利用Franson型干涉术观测到对Clauser–Horn–Shimony–Holt贝尔不等式的明显违背。我们的方案无需多光子产生，简化了实验要求，并为固态纠缠光子源建立了一条可扩展的路径。

Abstract (English)
Entanglement is a central resource in quantum technologies, and the realization of photonic entanglement necessarily relies on interaction with matter. Resonance fluorescence, originating from the coherent interaction between a driving field and a two-level system, plays a pivotal role in quantum optics. Here, we experimentally demonstrate high-quality generation of post-selected temporal-mode entanglement using vacuum–one-photon number superposition states from a single quantum dot, an approach conceptually aligned with early proposals based on identical single photons, yet whose high-quality experimental realization has remained absent. By delocalizing this superposition via a beam splitter, we realize post-selected temporal-mode entanglement and observe a clear violation of the Clauser–Horn–Shimony–Holt Bell inequality using Franson-type interferometry. Our scheme removes the need for multiphoton generation, simplifies the experimental requirements, and establishes a scalable pathway toward solid-state entangled photon sources.

Zi-Qi Zeng, Jian Wang, Xiu-Bin Liu, Xu-Jie Wang, Li Liu, Hanqing Liu, Haiqiao Ni, Zhichuan Niu, Carlos Antón-Solanas, Bang Wu, Zhiliang Yuan
Optica 13, 2306 (2026)
10.1364/optica.601049

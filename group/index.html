强噪声环境下的高速测量设备无关量子密钥分发High-Rate Measurement-Device-Independent Quantum Key Distribution under Intense Noise

实现重复频率 2.5 GHz 的测量设备无关量子密钥分发系统，借助优势蒸馏在 425 kHz 噪声计数下仍能生成安全密钥，30 dB 信道衰减下密钥率达 kbps 量级。

测量设备无关量子密钥分发（MDI-QKD）是通向实用化量子通信的一条有前景的路径，但当前的高性能演示往往伴随高昂的开销，并且只能在孤立的环境中运行。在此，我们展示了一套高速 MDI-QKD 系统，其重复频率达到前所未有的 2.5 GHz，并通过引入优势蒸馏方法验证了其强大的噪声容限。在 425 kHz 的噪声计数下仍能高效生成安全密钥，这比孤立系统高出 4 个数量级。在 30 dB 信道衰减下，无需笨重且资源密集的探测装置即可实现 kbps 量级的安全密钥率。本工作为 QKD 的部署建立了一套完整的实用化方案，解决了在同时具备高安全性、高密钥率、环境鲁棒性和物理紧凑性的条件下实现实用量子安全网络这一长期难题。

Abstract (English)
Measurement-device-independent quantum key distribution (MDI-QKD) is a promising path toward practical quantum communication, but current high-performance demonstrations come at prohibitive overhead and isolated environments. Here, we present a high-speed MDI-QKD system operating at an unprecedented repetition frequency of 2.5 GHz and verify its strong noise tolerance by incorporating the advantage distillation method. The secure key is efficiently generated under 425 kHz noise counts, which is 4 orders of magnitude higher than isolated systems. A secure key rate with a kbps level is achieved under 30 dB channel attenuation without bulky, resource-intensive detection devices. Our Letter establishes a complete practical solution for QKD deployment and resolves the persistent difficulty of realizing practical quantum secure networks with advanced security, a high key rate, environmental robustness, and physical compactness.

可扩展量子网络中的全无调制器量子密钥分发架构Fully modulator-free architecture for quantum key distribution in scalable quantum networks

提出并实验演示首个全无调制器的直接调制量子密钥分发架构，利用注入锁定与相干干涉同时控制相位和强度，在 30 dB 信道损耗下获得 4.78 kbps 的安全密钥率。

摘要 量子密钥分发（QKD）网络是迈向未来量子互联网的基础一步，已成为密集研究的焦点。类比经典光网络的架构可以看到，大容量骨干网通常依赖复杂的外调制，而对成本敏感的接入层则更青睐更简单、更紧凑、更经济的直接调制。作为量子网络的基石，当今的 QKD 网络同样具有这种骨干—接入的层级结构，但其实现仍严重依赖外调制器，致使接入层中直接调制 QKD 的生态位明显空缺。在此，我们提出并实验演示了首个全无调制器、全自由度的直接调制 QKD 架构，填补了这一空白。发射端将光注入锁定与相干光干涉相结合，无需外调制器即可同时实现相位与强度控制，从而能够制备任意量子态，同时保持与标准时间Bin（time-bin）编码接收端的天然兼容。我们的实验实现采用相对廉价的激光二极管和无源光学元件，在 30 dB 信道损耗下实现了 4.78 kbps 的安全密钥率，与传统的基于调制器的系统性能相当。通过填补 QKD 生态中长期存在的直接调制空白，本工作为接入层的部署难题提供了实用解决方案，并为紧凑、可扩展的 QKD 实现开辟了一条可行的新方向。

Abstract (English)
Abstract Quantum key distribution (QKD) networks represent a foundational step toward the future quantum internet and have become a focus of intensive research. By analogy with the architecture of classical optical networks, we can observe that the high-capacity backbone typically relies on complex external modulation, while the cost-sensitive access layer prefers simpler, more compact, and more economical direct modulation. As the cornerstone of quantum networks, today’s QKD networks share this backbone-access hierarchy, yet their implementations remain heavily reliant on external modulators, leaving a clear ecological niche unfilled for directly modulated QKD in the access layer. Here, we propose and experimentally demonstrate the first fully modulator-free, full-degree-of-freedom direct-modulation QKD architecture that fills this niche. The transmitter combines optical injection locking with coherent optical interference to achieve simultaneous phase and intensity control without external modulators, enabling arbitrary quantum-state preparation while preserving inherent compatibility with standard time-bin-encoding receivers. Built with relatively inexpensive laser diodes and passive optical components, our experimental implementation achieves a secure key rate of 4.78 kbps at 30 dB channel loss, matching the performance of conventional modulator-based systems. By filling the long-standing direct-modulation niche in the QKD ecosystem, this work delivers a practical solution to the access-layer deployment challenge and opens a viable new direction for compact, scalable QKD implementation.

具有自适应子空间划分的高维纠缠量子网络High-dimensional entangled quantum networks with adaptive subspace division

构建城域尺度高维纠缠网络，采用偏振—时间Bin混合编码与主动子空间划分，传输距离较以往网络级高维实现提升三个数量级，并可与经典信号共存。

量子通信网络对安全信息传输和分布式量子技术至关重要。高维纠缠是此类网络的一种有前景的资源，因为它能够提升信息容量并提供更大的潜力。然而，其实际优势尚未得到充分探索，现有演示在真实条件下也未显示出相对于二维网络的明显优势。在此，我们展示了一个具有主动子空间划分的城域尺度高维纠缠网络，在密钥率、灵活性和抗噪性方面展现出潜在的实际优势。我们采用偏振—时间Bin混合编码，在单模光纤信道中分发高维纠缠。其传输距离比此前网络级高维实现高出三个数量级，同时主动相位补偿支持长期稳定运行。我们的网络还可与经典信号共存，其可重构的子空间划分提供了实用量子网络所需的适应性。这些结果展示了通往大规模、高性能量子通信网络的一条实用路径。

Abstract (English)
Quantum communication networks are essential for secure information transfer and distributed quantum technologies. High-dimensional entanglement is a promising resource for such networks because it can increase the information capacity and offer greater potential. However, its practical advantages have not been fully explored, and existing demonstrations have not shown clear benefits over two-dimensional networks under realistic conditions. Here, we demonstrate a metropolitan-scale high-dimensional entangled network with active subspace division, showing potential practical benefits in key rate, flexibility, and noise resilience. Using hybrid polarization-time-bin encoding, we distribute high-dimensional entanglement over single-mode fiber channels. The transmission distance exceeds previous network-level high-dimensional implementations by three orders of magnitude, while active phase compensation supports long-term stable operation. Our network can also coexist with classical signals, and its reconfigurable subspace division provides the adaptability needed for practical quantum networks. These results show a practical route toward large-scale, high-performance quantum communication networks.

基于相干态的相对论性位置验证协议Relativistic position verification with coherent states

在信息论框架下结合量子光学与相对论，利用相位随机化弱相干态，由相距2 km的两个验证者以优于75 m的精度安全验证证明者位置，并完成实验实现。

安全地确定一个实体的位置，可以支撑基于位置的交易授权和高价值目标追踪等方案。然而，在不可信证明者情形下，经典方法无法提供安全的位置验证，这意味着证明者可能就其实际位置误导验证者。在此，我们在信息论框架内结合量子光学与相对论，提出并实验实现了一种安全的位置验证协议。利用相位随机化弱相干态，相距2 km的两个验证者以优于75 m的精度安全地验证了证明者的位置。这些结果确立了安全的位置基认证作为一种实际可能性，为金融交易、灾害响应和认证安全通信中的应用铺平了道路。

Abstract (English)
Abstract A secure approach to determining the position of an entity can enable schemes such as position-based authorization for transactions and tracking of high-value targets. However, classical methods are incapable of providing secure position verification in the untrusted-prover case, meaning that a prover can mislead verifiers about its actual position. Here we propose and experimentally realize a secure position verification protocol that combines quantum optics and relativity within an information-theoretic framework. Using phase-randomized weak coherent states, two verifiers separated by 2 km securely verify the prover’s position with an accuracy better than 75 m. These results establish secure position-based authentication as a practical possibility, paving the way for applications in financial transactions, disaster response and authenticated secure communications.

满足纯度约束的可认证量子随机数生成Certified Quantum Randomness within Purity Constraints

提出一种基于测量不确定性的半器件无关量子随机数发生器，在纯度约束下估计条件最小熵，无需对光源与测量装置精确建模，并在商用光纤系统上实现了指数级的安全随机数扩展。

量子随机数发生器（QRNG）利用量子力学产生真正的随机性，但实际的实现方案会受到器件缺陷的影响，这些缺陷可能危及安全性。我们提出了一种基于纯度约束下测量不确定性的半器件无关 QRNG，用于估计条件最小熵。该协议不需要对二维光源和可数维测量装置进行详细的建模，并且对于测量装置所遭受的量子攻击仍然保持安全。我们揭示了由自然满足的纯度条件所隐含的一种不确定关系，利用测试态定义了随机性参数 C，并借助态与测量的幺正旋转对称性，将其与可提取随机性的解析界联系起来。利用广义熵积累定理，我们把单轮界推广到多轮，并获得了可组合的、能够抵御相干攻击的安全性。在标准商用光纤系统上实现时，这一高容忍度的协议实现了指数级的安全随机数扩展，并且适用于集成光子平台。

Abstract (English)
Quantum random number generators (QRNGs) exploit quantum mechanics to produce true randomness, but practical implementations suffer from device imperfections that can compromise security. We propose a semi–device-independent QRNG based on measurement uncertainty under purity constraints for conditional min-entropy estimation. Our protocol requires no detailed modeling of the two-dimensional source and the countable-dimensional measurement and remains secure against quantum attacks on measurement devices. We uncover an implicit uncertainty relation arising from naturally satisfied purity conditions, define a randomness parameter C using test states, and connect it to an analytical bound on extractable randomness via unitary rotation symmetries of states and measurements. Using the generalized entropy accumulation theorem, we extend the single-round bound to multirounds and obtain composable security against coherent attacks. Implemented on a standard commercial fiber system, our high-tolerance protocol achieves exponential secure randomness expansion and is suitable for integrated-photonic platforms.

Xing Lin, Rong Wang, Zhen Qiang Yin, Shuang Wang

紧凑态制备与探测的高速量子密钥分发High-rate quantum key distribution with compact state preparation and detection

量子密钥分发（QKD）是实现信息论安全通信的一项颇具前景的技术。QKD 系统的实用性对于大规模网络部署至关重要，它既要求高的密钥生成速率，也要求量子态制备与探测模块的小型化。在这里，我们报道了一套可部署的 QKD 系统，其在 10 km 处实现了 60.33 ± 0.03 Mbps、在 100 km 处实现了 3.08 ± 0.20 Mbps 的安全密钥速率。该系统集成了一个双平行马赫–曾德尔编码器，通过紧凑设计中的单步调制实现对偏振态与诱骗态的高保真、统一制备。同时，我们研制了工作在 2.5 GHz 的小型化单光子雪崩探测器，在保持低噪声的同时实现了高计数率。这一高性能且紧凑的方案，为需要高容量和高带宽的城市安全通信网络确立了一条可行的路线。

Abstract (English)
Quantum key distribution (QKD) is a promising technology for information-theoretic secure communications. The practicality of QKD systems is critical for large-scale network deployment, requiring both high key generation rate and miniaturized modules for quantum-state preparation and detection. Here, we report a deployable QKD system that achieves secure key rates of 60.33 ± 0.03 Mbps at 10 km and 3.08 ± 0.20 Mbps at 100 km. Meanwhile, we developed miniaturized single-photon avalanche detectors operating at 2.5 GHz, achieving high count rates while maintaining low noise. This high-performance and compact solution establishes a feasible roadmap for urban secure communication networks that require high capacity and bandwidth.

基于厚氮化硅的宽带偏振无关马赫–曾德尔干涉仪Broadband polarization-insensitive Mach–Zehnder interferometer for quantum manipulation based on thick silicon nitride

在厚度 650 nm 的氮化硅平台上设计并制备偏振无关马赫–曾德尔干涉仪，用粒子群优化的多模干涉器与分段变宽波导补偿材料双折射，单光子干涉可见度约 98.7%，偏振相关损耗低于 0.3 dB。

偏振无关的光子器件对于减轻光子传输过程中随机偏振演化所带来的不利影响、并实现对光的鲁棒量子操控至关重要。该工作在厚度为 650 nm 的氮化物平台上设计并演示了一种偏振无关的马赫–曾德尔干涉仪（MZI）。我们采用具有定制边缘形状和亚波长光栅结构的粒子群优化多模干涉器来设计该器件，并结合干涉仪两臂中分段变宽的波导设计，以补偿其固有的材料双折射。对所制备 MZI 的实验结果表明，即使在主动偏振扰动的情况下，单光子干涉可见度仍保持在约 98.7%，同时在 147 nm 的 1 dB 带宽内实现了小于 0.3 dB 的偏振相关损耗。这些特性使其成为量子密钥分发等集成量子光子系统中颇具前景的元件。

Abstract (English)
Polarization-insensitive photonic devices are essential for mitigating the adverse effects of random polarization evolution during photon transmission and implementing robust quantum manipulation of light. In this work, we design and demonstrate a polarization-insensitive Mach–Zehnder interferometer (MZI) on a nitride platform with the thickness of 650 nm. We design the device by adopting a particle-swarm-optimized multimode interferometer with tailored edge shapes and sub-wavelength grating structures, combined with a segmented width-varied waveguide design in the interferometer arms to compensate for their intrinsic material birefringences. The experimental results of the fabricated MZI maintain a single-photon interference visibility about 98.7% even under active polarization scrambling, while a polarization-dependent loss < 0.3 dB over a 147 nm 1 dB bandwidth has been achieved. These properties make it a promising component for integrated quantum photonic systems such as quantum key distribution.

Abstract (English)
Linewidth is a key parameter reflecting the coherence of single-frequency lasers. However, flicker noise can cause linewidth broadening much larger than the Lorentzian component. Considering that interference visibility can serve as an effective indicator of phase noise magnitude, in this work, we quantify flicker noise as Gaussian linewidth and analyze the relation between interference visibility and laser linewidth. We find that the logarithm of visibility depends quadratically on the delay: the linear term is due to white noise, and the quadratic term arises from flicker noise. In addition, this approach effectively reduces the dependence of the linewidth measurement experiment on the length of the delay fiber, with shorter fibers helping to minimize environmental disturbances. More importantly, the introduction of the Gaussian linewidth provides a more comprehensive characterization of the laser linewidth.

超越量子比特极限的超导三能级量子链路Superconducting Qutrit Link beyond the Qubit Limit

★ 编辑推荐量子纠缠

在两个独立封装的超导节点间建立微波三能级链路，实现任意三能级态转移，平均保真度83.68%，并重建出负度0.730的远程双三能级态。

超导微波链路已使量子比特之间的确定性态转移与远程纠缠成为可能，但迄今为止，确定性链路所传输的希尔伯特空间实际上仍是二维的。本文演示了两个由微波通道连接的独立封装节点之间的超导三能级链路。每个节点将一个 transmon 三能级系统、一个传输谐振腔和一个可调 Purcell 滤波接口结合在一起，使两个远程微波光子接口在频率与带宽上都能相互匹配。我们实现了两种跃迁选择的光子介导操作，在同一通道的不同时间模式中分别转移 |e⟩ 与 |f⟩ 三能级分量。我们对任意三能级态的转移进行了层析表征，得到平均转移态保真度 83.68% 与三能级过程保真度 77.12%，二者均超过经典三能级转移基准，也超过用等效量子比特通道传输任意三能级态时所能达到的最佳平均保真度。利用部分转移操作，我们重建了负度为 0.730 的远程双三能级态、由层析推断的密集编码容量 2.273 比特，以及由层析推断的 Collins-Gisin-Linden-Massar-Popescu (CGLMP) 参数 I₃ = 2.332，均超出相应的量子比特界限或局域界限。这些结果表明，该超导微波链路把 transmon 固有的三能级结构用作真正的高维通信资源。

Abstract (English)
Superconducting microwave links have enabled deterministic state transfer and remote entanglement between qubits, but deterministic links have so far operated with an effectively two-dimensional transmitted Hilbert space. Here we demonstrate a superconducting qutrit link between two independently packaged nodes connected by a microwave channel. Each node combines a transmon qutrit, a transmission resonator, and a tunable Purcell-filter interface, allowing the two remote microwave-photon interfaces to be matched in both frequency and bandwidth. We implement two transition-selective photon-mediated operations that transfer the | e ⟩ and | f ⟩ qutrit components in distinct temporal modes of the same channel. We tomographically characterize arbitrary qutrit-state transfer, obtaining a mean transferred-state fidelity of 83.68% and a qutrit process fidelity of 77.12%, exceeding both the classical qutrit-transfer benchmark and the best possible average fidelity of an effective qubit channel used to transmit an arbitrary qutrit. Using partial-transfer operations, we reconstruct a remote two-qutrit state with negativity 0.730, a tomography-inferred dense-coding capacity of 2.273 bits, and a tomography-inferred Collins-Gisin-Linden-Massar-Popescu (CGLMP) parameter I 3 = 2.332 , all beyond the corresponding qubit or local bounds. These results demonstrate a superconducting microwave link that uses the native three-level structure of transmons as a genuine high-dimensional communication resource.

利用里德堡阻塞在光镊中快速制备单原子Fast Single-Atom Preparation in Optical Tweezers via Rydberg Blockade

提出利用光镊内里德堡阻塞与自电离，在微秒尺度逐个移除多余原子，把多原子概率压至1%的同时保留58.2%的单原子填充，比光辅助碰撞快两个量级。

可连续补充的光镊阵列将使中性原子量子比特能够支撑无限深度的量子线路。限制这类系统循环时间的一个关键瓶颈，是如何从最初装载了多于一个原子的光镊中移除原子。在传统的光辅助碰撞技术中，缓慢的碰撞动力学把移除多余原子所需的时间尺度限制在数毫秒。本文提出并演示了一种方案，利用光镊内的里德堡阻塞与自电离，在微秒时间尺度上从多原子占据的光镊中选择性地逐个移除原子。我们在 ¹⁷¹Yb 的两种互补机制中演示了该协议。当从基态进行双光子里德堡激发时，我们在 64.8 μs 内把多原子概率降至 1%，同时在 58.2(2)% 的光镊中保留单原子，这与相同实验条件下光辅助碰撞所达到的填充率相当，但速度快两个数量级以上。当从亚稳态 ³P₀ 进行单光子激发时，单原子损耗的降低使填充率提高到 74.8(3)%，代价是需要额外的时间开销把原子制备到 ³P₀。最终填充率受制于一种尚无法解释的两体损耗机制；若能解决该问题，则有望实现快速、准确定性的装载。

Abstract (English)
Continuously replenished optical tweezer arrays will unlock unlimited-depth quantum circuits with neutral atom qubits. A key bottleneck limiting the cycle time of these systems is removing atoms from tweezers initially loaded with more than one atom. In the conventional technique of light-assisted collisions, slow collisional dynamics limit the timescale for removing excess atoms to several milliseconds. Here, we propose and demonstrate a scheme for selectively removing one atom at a time from multiply occupied tweezers on a microsecond timescale, using intra-tweezer Rydberg blockade and autoionization. We demonstrate the protocol in Yb 171 in two complementary regimes. With two-photon Rydberg excitation from the ground state, we reduce the multi-atom probability to 1% in 64.8 μ s , while retaining single atoms in 58.2 ( 2 ) % of the tweezers, which is comparable to the filling fraction achieved with light-assisted collisions under the same experimental conditions, but over two orders of magnitude faster. With single-photon excitation from the metastable state P 0 3 , reduced single-atom loss enables a higher filling fraction of 74.8 ( 3 ) % , at the cost of additional temporal overhead to prepare the atoms in P 0 3 . The final filling fraction is limited by an unexplained two-body loss mechanism, which, if solved, could enable fast, quasi-deterministic loading.

Yiyi Li, Vernon M. Hughes, Michael Peper, Yicheng Bao, Chenyuan Li, Sanzhar Bissenali, and Jeff D. Thompson

基于三重态卡宾分子的单分子自旋—光子接口A single-molecule spin–photon interface

★ 封面文章

把三重态卡宾分子嵌入结构匹配的宿主晶体，制成可单分子寻址的自旋—光子接口，零声子线窄、光谱稳定逾一小时，4.5 K下动力学去耦相干达毫秒量级。

把长寿命自旋量子比特与光子连接起来的光学接口，是量子网络与分布式量子信息处理的核心需求。目前，固态原子缺陷因其固有的自旋相干性与光学相干性而成为领先的候选体系。在这些进展的基础上，可人工剪裁的分子体系代表着该领域的一次根本性变革，它依靠精确的原子级控制与一致的自下而上组装。然而，一个能够同时满足稳健自旋—光子接口各项要求——即明亮荧光、高光谱稳定性与长基态自旋寿命三者兼备——的分子平台，此前尚未在单分子层面得到实现。本文表明，一种嵌入结构匹配宿主晶体中的三重态基态卡宾分子，可以充当具有单分子可寻址性的稳健自旋—光子接口。该体系表现出狭窄的零声子线、超过一小时的光谱稳定性、自旋选择性光学跃迁以及单分子光探测磁共振。相干控制给出毫秒量级的动力学去耦相干时间，并在 4.5 K 温度下实现数十毫秒的自旋弛豫。这些结果确立了分子量子比特作为单发射体量子光学可行平台的地位，同时保留了自下而上化学设计与可加工材料的优势。

Abstract (English)
Optical interfaces that connect long-lived spin qubits to photons are a central requirement for quantum networking and distributed quantum information processing. At present, solid-state atomic defects are leading candidates because of their inherent spin and optical coherence1,2. Building on these advancements, synthetically tailored molecular systems represent a fundamental change in the field, using precise atomic control and consistent bottom-up assembly3,4. However, a molecular platform that concurrently satisfies the demands of a robust spin–photon interface—namely, the combination of bright fluorescence, high spectral stability and long ground-state spin lifetimes—has yet to be realized at the single-molecule level. Here we show that a triplet ground-state carbene molecule, embedded within a structurally matched host crystal, functions as a robust spin–photon interface with single-molecule addressability. The system exhibits narrow ZPLs, spectral stability over more than an hour, spin-selective optical transitions and single-molecule optically detected magnetic resonance. Coherent control yields millisecond-scale dynamical-decoupling coherence and tens-of-milliseconds spin relaxation at a temperature of 4.5 K. These results establish molecular qubits as a viable platform for single-emitter quantum optics5,6 while preserving the advantages of bottom-up chemical design7 and processable materials8–10. A triplet carbene molecule in a tailored crystal provides a stable single-molecule spin–photon interface with millisecond coherence, advancing chemically engineered molecular qubits for quantum networking.

微腔中色心的纳米级定位实现硅基明亮纯净单光子源Bright and pure single-photon source in silicon by nanoscale positioning of a color center in a microcavity

把单个W色心纳米级定位在圆形布拉格光栅微腔中，制成1217 nm全硅单光子源，饱和计数1.29×10⁶ counts/s，g⁽²⁾(0)=0.06，脉冲触发纯度93%。

硅是光子量子技术的领先材料平台。然而，如何在近红外波段高效集成高性能单光子源仍是一项关键挑战。本文提出一种波长为 1217 nm 的全硅单光子源，它通过将单个 W 色心以纳米级精度定位在圆形布拉格光栅微腔中制成。其工作波长对应 W 色心的零声子线，该零声子线被微腔所增强。在带隙以上的连续波激发下，我们获得 1.29×10⁶ counts/s 的饱和光子计数率，并伴随显著的光子反聚束（g⁽²⁾(0) = 0.06 ± 0.02）。在弱脉冲激发下的触发式运行产生纯度为 93 ± 2% 的单光子。在饱和条件下，我们揭示了由再泵浦过程导致的纯度下降，未来可通过选择性激发方案加以缓解。这些结果是朝着硅基光子学中按需产生的不可区分单光子源迈出的重要一步。

Abstract (English)
Silicon is a leading material platform for photonic quantum technologies. However, a key challenge remains the efficient integration of high-performance single-photon sources in the near infrared. Here we present an all-silicon single-photon source at 1217 nm wavelength, fabricated by nanoscale positioning of a single W center in a circular Bragg grating cavity. The operation wavelength corresponds to the W center’s zero-phonon line that is enhanced by the cavity. Under above-gap continuous-wave excitation, we obtain a saturated photon count rate of 1.29 × 10 6 counts/s with pronounced photon antibunching ( g (2) (0) = 0.06 ± 0.02). Triggered operation under weak pulsed excitation produces single photons with 93 ± 2% purity. At saturation, we reveal purity degradation due to repumping processes, that could be mitigated using selective excitation schemes in the future. These results represent a major step towards on-demand sources of indistinguishable single photons in silicon photonics.

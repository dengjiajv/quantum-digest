里德堡原子阵列中 Ising 临界性奇异对称的介子谱学Meson Spectroscopy of Exotic Symmetries of Ising Criticality in Rydberg Atom Arrays

在里德堡原子阵列中同时实现 Ising 链与梯子构型，单链临界点测到与 E₈ 一致的质量谱，弱耦合梯子中首次看到 Ising 激发重组为 D₈⁽¹⁾ 理论预言的束缚态谱。

Ising 模型是探索量子临界系统中演生对称性的标准平台。一维 Ising 链的临界点由共形 Ising 场论描述，该场论在存在磁微扰时仍可积，从而产生与例外李代数 E₈ 相联系的有质量粒子。把两条 Ising 链弱耦合为梯子会破坏这种可积性，并预言会把解耦链的基本扭结激发束缚起来，产生由 D₈⁽¹⁾ 理论描述的更丰富的激发谱。E₈ 激发的实验迹象可以说已在自旋链材料 CoNb₂O₆ 的散射研究中被观察到，但直接观测由链间耦合产生的束缚态谱一直未能实现。这里我们在里德堡原子量子处理单元中探测这些演生对称性，利用其可调几何同时实现链与梯子构型。我们识别出与单链临界点处 E₈ 一致的质量谱，并在弱耦合梯子中首次报道了 Ising 激发重组为 D₈⁽¹⁾ 对称性所预言的束缚态谱的迹象。我们的结果展示了里德堡平台在研究量子多体系统对称性演生方面的能力，并为准粒子相互作用、几何与临界性之间的相互影响提供了一个直接窗口。

Abstract (English)
The Ising model serves as a canonical platform for exploring emergent symmetry in quantum critical systems. The critical point of the 1D Ising chain is described by a conformal Ising field theory, which remains integrable in the presence of a magnetic perturbation, leading to massive particles associated with the exceptional Lie algebra E 8 . Weakly coupling two Ising chains into a ladder breaks this integrability and is predicted to bind the elementary kink excitations of the decoupled chains, giving rise to a richer spectrum of excitations described by the D 8 ( 1 ) theory. Experimental signatures of E 8 excitations have arguably been observed in scattering studies of the spin chain material CoNb 2 O 6 , but direct observation of the bound-state spectrum emerging from interchain coupling has remained elusive. Here, we probe these emergent symmetries in a Rydberg atom quantum processing unit, leveraging its tunable geometry to realize both chain and ladder configurations. We identify mass spectra consistent with E 8 at the single-chain critical point and, in the weakly coupled ladder, report the first signatures of the reorganization of Ising excitations into the bound-state spectrum predicted by D 8 ( 1 ) symmetry. Our results demonstrate the power of Rydberg platforms for investigating symmetry emergence in quantum many-body systems and provide a direct window into the interplay of quasiparticle interactions, geometry, and criticality.

Joseph Vovrosh, Julius de Hond, Sergi Julià-Farré, Johannes Knolle, and Alexandre Dauphin

面向可见光量子光子电路的超低损耗压电光机低限制氮化硅平台Ultra-low loss piezo-optomechanical low-confinement silicon nitride platform for visible wavelength quantum photonic circuits

把压电光机驱动与 CMOS 代工低限制氮化硅平台结合，在 780 nm 实现 2.6 dB/m 传播损耗与 2.8 V·m 半波电压—长度积，演示每相移器损耗 0.63 dB 的可重构马赫—曾德尔干涉仪。

实现规模化的光子量子计算需要把超低损耗与快速、低功耗、低迟滞、低串扰的可重构性结合起来的集成电路。这些要求在可见光波段尤其具有挑战性，因为包括单光子源与量子存储器在内的许多量子资源态产生器天然工作在该波段。低限制氮化硅波导具备所需的损耗性能，但传统热光调制器会耗散大量静态功率，引发热串扰并妨碍低温运行。可见光波段的压电光机电路可避免这些缺陷，但已有的演示要么依赖传播损耗为 35–100 dB/m 的高限制波导，要么依赖使用与代工不兼容的锆钛酸铅（PZT）的低限制平台。这里我们把压电光机驱动与 CMOS 代工制造的低限制氮化硅平台结合起来，在 780 nm 实现 2.6 dB/m 的传播损耗、兆赫兹量级的调制带宽、2.8 V·m 的半波电压—长度积以及可忽略的迟滞。我们演示了每个螺旋相移器损耗为 0.63 dB 的可重构马赫—曾德尔干涉仪，从而使深度主动可重构的可见光波段量子光子电路成为可能。

Abstract (English)
Realizing photonic quantum computing at scale requires integrated circuits that combine ultra-low loss with fast, low-power, low-hysteresis, and low-crosstalk reconfiguration. These requirements are particularly challenging at visible wavelengths, where many quantum resource-state generators, including single-photon sources and quantum memories, naturally operate. Low-confinement silicon nitride waveguides offer the requisite loss performance, but conventional thermo-optic modulators dissipate significant static power, driving thermal crosstalk and precluding cryogenic operation. Visible-wavelength piezo-optomechanical circuits avoid these drawbacks, but existing demonstrations rely either on high-confinement waveguides with propagation losses of 35-100 dB/m, or on low-confinement platforms using foundry-incompatible lead zirconate titanate (PZT). Here we combine piezo-optomechanical actuation with a CMOS-foundry-fabricated, low-confinement silicon nitride platform, achieving 2.6 dB/m propagation loss at 780 nm, megahertz-scale modulation bandwidth, a half-wave voltage-length product of 2.8 V ⋅ m, and negligible hysteresis. We demonstrate reconfigurable Mach-Zehnder interferometers with 0.63 dB loss per spiral phase shifter, enabling deep, actively reconfigurable visible-wavelength quantum photonic circuits.

Mayank Mishra, Gwangho Choi, Wenhua He, Gina M. Talcott, Katherine Kearney, Michael Gehl, Andrew Leenheer, Daniel Dominguez, Nils T. Otterstrom, Matt Eichenfield
Nat. Commun. (2026)
10.1038/s41467-026-77969-0

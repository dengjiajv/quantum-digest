⁸⁷Sr SU(N) 费米-哈伯德体系的自旋分辨量子气体显微成像Spin-Resolved Microscopy of Sr 87 SU ( N ) Fermi-Hubbard Systems

为⁸⁷Sr 费米气体构建量子气体显微镜，借助 689 nm 窄互组合线的冷却与荧光实现单原子自旋分辨，可在单次实验中读出十个自旋态的占据并观测拉莫尔进动。

量子气体显微镜可直接接触哈伯德模型的相，从微观层面揭示相互作用、SU(2) 磁性以及掺杂之间复杂的竞争。碱土（类）费米子通过实现更高对称性并给出 SU(N) 哈伯德模型，扩展了自旋-1/2 范式，其丰富的相图仍有待揭示。尽管具有基础研究价值，对 SU(N) 量子系统的微观探索仍难以实现。在此，我们报道了用于费米子⁸⁷Sr 的量子气体显微镜的实现。我们的成像方案基于 689 nm 窄互组合线上的冷却与荧光，能够实现自旋分辨的单原子探测。通过实施自旋选择性光泵浦协议，我们在单次实验实现中确定十个自旋态各自的占据，这是探测格点分辨磁关联的关键能力。我们通过在全自旋-9/2 基态流形上观测单粒子拉莫尔进动来对方法进行基准测试，并利用包含测得探测保真度的 SU(N) 费米-哈伯德链数值模拟，证明其分辨多体自旋关联的能力。我们的结果确立⁸⁷Sr 量子气体显微术是研究 SU(N) 费米-哈伯德模型中奇异磁性的有力方法，并为量子模拟、计算和计量学研究提供探测工具。另见 4 幅图。

Abstract (English)
Quantum-gas microscopes provide direct access to the phases of the Hubbard model, bringing microscopic insight into the complex competition between interactions, SU(2) magnetism, and doping. Alkaline-earth(-like) fermions extend this spin- 1 / 2 paradigm by realizing higher symmetries and giving access to SU ( N ) Hubbard models, with rich phase diagrams to be unveiled. Despite its fundamental interest, a microscopic exploration of SU ( N ) quantum systems has remained elusive. Here, we report the realization of a quantum-gas microscope for fermionic Sr 87 . Our imaging scheme, based on cooling and fluorescence on the narrow intercombination line at 689 nm, enables spin-resolved single-atom detection. By implementing a spin-selective optical pumping protocol, we determine the occupation of each of the ten spin states in a single experimental realization, a crucial capability for probing site-resolved magnetic correlations. We benchmark our method by observing single-particle Larmor precession across the full spin- 9 / 2 ground-state manifold and use numerical simulations of SU ( N ) Fermi-Hubbard chains, incorporating the measured detection fidelities, to demonstrate its capability to resolve many-body spin correlations. Our results establish Sr 87 quantum-gas microscopy as a powerful approach to study exotic magnetism in the SU ( N ) Fermi-Hubbard model and provide a detection tool for studies in quantum simulation, computation, and metrology. 原子级配位工程量子点调控线粒体能量救援以促进糖尿病骨再生Atomic-level coordination-engineered quantum dots orchestrate mitochondrial energy rescue for diabetic bone regeneration

★ 封面文章

针对糖尿病骨再生中的细胞能量危机，识别出经线粒体转移与骨免疫结果耦合、可治疗靶向的关键线粒体供体细胞，并设计原子级配位工程量子点，降低ROS催化反应能垒、激活CaMKKβ-AMPK轴，恢复功能性线粒体转移。

糖尿病骨再生因细胞能量危机而严重受损，该危机扰乱了骨免疫过程。线粒体转移是一种潜在的代谢救援机制，但在糖尿病中这一过程可能被劫持并传播炎症。现有的线粒体保护策略未能解决再生组织中细胞间线粒体转移的时空动态问题。在此，我们识别出关键的线粒体供体细胞，即一个通过线粒体转移与骨免疫结果功能耦合、可作为治疗靶点的细胞群体。我们设计了一种基于原子级配位工程量子点的纳米治疗剂，以恢复功能性线粒体转移、维持能量稳态。这些量子点显著降低了ROS催化反应的能垒并保护线粒体。此外，其Mn配位降解片段在糖尿病中激活CaMKKβ-AMPK信号轴。线粒体质量控制与转移效率的增强使巨噬细胞成为合格的线粒体供体。因此，本工作提供了一种纠正病理性线粒体转移、挽救糖尿病骨修复的变革性途径。作者识别出骨免疫过程中的有害线粒体转移是糖尿病骨愈合受损的关键驱动因素，并设计量子点以增强能量调节、恢复功能性线粒体转移。

Abstract (English)
Diabetic bone regeneration is critically impaired by a cellular energy crisis that disrupts osteoimmunological processes. While mitochondrial transfer represents a potential metabolic rescue mechanism, this process can be hijacked in diabetes mellitus to propagate inflammation. Current mitochondria-protecting strategies fail to address the spatiotemporal dynamics of intercellular mitochondrial transfer in regenerative tissues. Here, we identify key mitochondrial donor cells, a therapeutically targetable cell population functionally coupled to osteoimmunological outcomes via mitochondrial transfer. We design a nanotherapeutic based on atomic-level coordination-engineered quantum dots to restore functional mitochondrial transfer for energy homeostasis. These quantum dots markedly reduce the ROS catalytic reaction energy barrier and protect mitochondria. Furthermore, their Mn-coordinated degradation fragments activate the CaMKKβ-AMPK signaling axis in diabetes mellitus. This enhanced mitochondrial quality control and transfer efficiency render macrophages competent mitochondria donors. Consequently, our work provides a transformative approach to correct pathological mitochondrial transfer and rescue diabetic bone repair. Here the authors identify deleterious mitochondrial transfer in osteoimmunological processes as a key driver of impaired diabetic bone healing and engineer quantum dots to boost energy-regulation and restore functional mitochondrial transfer.

人工智能辅助高光谱干涉术与单细胞色散成像AI-assisted hyperspectral interferometry and single-cell dispersion imaging

集成光子

提出通用偏振共路干涉术GPCPI，放宽传统共路干涉的偏振限制，可同步测得振幅与相位，并用深度自编码器分析相位二阶导数；相位稳定性较现有技术提升一个数量级，已用于超表面相位传感与单细胞色散成像。

干涉测量技术是从光学系统中提取相位信息的关键手段，可用于精确测量色散以及对微扰进行高灵敏度探测。与常规光谱学方法相比，相位传感虽然灵敏度更高，但这种灵敏度也往往使系统更容易受到振动等外部因素的干扰，从而引入不稳定性和噪声。本工作展示了一种宽带、人工智能增强的干涉测量方法，称为通用偏振共路干涉术（GPCPI），它放宽了传统共路干涉术的偏振限制。该偏振解耦特性使振幅与相位能够同时测量，并结合深度神经自编码器，通过分析相位分布的二阶导数映射来检测光谱中的相位异常，从而提高宽带相位测量的精度。与当前最先进的干涉测量技术相比，该方法使相位稳定性提升了一个数量级，从而获得更高的相位传感精度。等离激元超表面相位传感和高光谱单细胞色散成像展示了该方法相对于常规光谱学的性能与灵敏度。我们改编的深度学习模型 ConvNeXt V2 能够以最小噪声实现相位变化的单次、实时追踪。受细胞培养样品影响的干涉条纹揭示了正常皮肤细胞（CCD-32Sk）与癌变皮肤细胞（COLO-829）的指纹特征，作为原理验证，展示了通过高光谱色散成像在单细胞水平上进行细胞分类和疾病诊断的应用。所提出的干涉测量技术为宽带相位测量和单细胞色散成像提供了一种可靠、紧凑且稳定的解决方案，可应用于计量学、分子诊断、药物研发和量子传感。

Abstract (English)
Interferometry techniques are essential for extracting phase information from optical systems, enabling precise measurements of dispersion and highly sensitive detection of perturbations. While phase sensing offers enhanced sensitivity compared to conventional spectroscopy methods, this sensitivity often makes systems more vulnerable to external factors such as vibrations, introducing instability and noise. In this work, we demonstrate a broadband and AI-enhanced interferometry method, denoted general polarization common-path interferometry (GPCPI), that relaxes the polarization constraints of traditional common-path interferometry. The polarization decoupling feature enables simultaneous amplitude and phase measurements supplemented with deep neural autoencoders to detect phase anomalies in the spectrum through the analysis of second-order derivative mapping of the phase profile, enhancing the accuracy of broadband phase measurements. The approach enables an order of magnitude improvement in phase stability compared to state-of-the-art interferometry techniques, leading to higher accuracy in phase sensing. Plasmonic metasurface phase sensing and hyperspectral single-cell dispersion imaging demonstrate the capability and sensitivity of the method over conventional spectroscopy. Our own adapted version of deep learning model, ConvNeXt V2, enables single-shot and real-time tracking of phase variation with minimized noise. Interference fringes affected by the cell-cultured samples reveal the fingerprints of the normal (CCD-32Sk) versus cancerous (COLO-829) skin cells, demonstrating application in cell classification and disease diagnosis at single-cell level through hyperspectral dispersion imaging as a proof of principle. The proposed interferometry technique offers a reliable, compact, and stable solution for broadband phase measurements and single-cell dispersion imaging for applications in metrology, molecular diagnostics, drug discovery, and quantum sensing.

Kamyar Behrouzi, Tanveer Ahmed Siddique, Megan Teng, Walid Redjem, Liwei Lin, Boubacar Kante

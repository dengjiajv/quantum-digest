光腔与里德堡原子阵列强耦合的实验实现Realization of a Cavity-Coupled Rydberg Array

在可扩展光镊阵列中，于同一空间位置同时实现原子与高精细度光腔模式的高协同度耦合和集体增强的里德堡相互作用，为量子网络节点与长程相互作用开放系统的模拟铺路。

可扩展的量子计算机与量子网络需要将量子处理节点与高效的光与物质相互作用界面结合起来，以便在局域或长距离量子网络中分发量子信息。中性原子阵列既可与里德堡态耦合，从而在通用处理架构中实现高保真量子门；也可与光学腔耦合，从而实现与光子的界面。然而，将这两种能力结合起来、使原子阵列以光学腔模式耦合到高激发里德堡态，一直是一项悬而未决的挑战。本文提出一种新型的腔耦合里德堡阵列，实现了这一长期目标。我们在可扩展的光镊阵列中制备、探测并操控单个原子，使其与高精细度光学腔的光学模式强耦合，并以可控方式激发到里德堡态。我们表明，与光学腔的高协同度耦合——通过原子存在时腔共振的色散频移加以演示——以及强里德堡相互作用——通过原子阵列中里德堡耦合的集体增强加以演示——可以在我们的装置中于同一空间位置同时实现。这一实验平台为若干新方向开辟了道路，包括量子网络节点的实现、长程相互作用开放量子系统的量子模拟，以及利用高保真里德堡控制的光子态工程。

Abstract (English)
Scalable quantum computers and quantum networks require the combination of quantum processing nodes with efficient light-matter interfaces to distribute quantum information in local or long-distance quantum networks. Neutral-atom arrays have both been coupled to Rydberg states to enable high-fidelity quantum gates in universal processing architectures, and to optical cavities to realize interfaces to photons. However, combining these two capabilities and coupling atom arrays to highly excited Rydberg states in the mode of an optical cavity has been an outstanding challenge. Here we present a novel cavity-coupled Rydberg array that achieves this long-standing goal. We prepare, detect, and control individual atoms in a scalable optical tweezer array, couple them strongly to the optical mode of a high-finesse optical cavity and excite them in a controlled way to Rydberg states. We show that high-cooperativity coupling to an optical cavity—demonstrated via the dispersive shift of the resonance of the cavity in presence of the atoms—and strong Rydberg interactions—demonstrated via the collective enhancement of Rydberg coupling in the atomic array—can be achieved in our setup at the same spatial location. Our presented experimental platform opens the path to several new directions, including the realization of quantum network nodes, quantum simulation of long-range interacting, open quantum systems, and photonic-state engineering leveraging high-fidelity Rydberg control.

Jacopo De Santis, Balázs Dura-Kovács, Mehmet Öncü, Adrien Bouscal, Dimitrios Vasileiadis, and Johannes Zeiher

量子信道在钻石距离度量下的最优学习Optimal learning of quantum channels in diamond distance

确定了以钻石距离为精度标准时信道层析的最优查询复杂度，算法基于 Choi 态的纯态层析归约，对输入输出维度和 Kraus 秩均达最优，并统一涵盖态、酉变换与测量的层析。

量子态的演化可以用量子信道描述。估计未知量子信道的任务被称为过程层析，是量子信息论中的核心问题。一个长期悬而未决的问题是：要以区分量子过程的标准度量——即所谓的钻石距离——所量化的精度学习一个未知信道，需要多少次使用该信道。本文通过确立信道层析的查询复杂度来解决这一问题，其在任意固定的钻石距离常数精度下，对输入与输出空间维度以及信道的 Kraus 秩均达到最优依赖。我们设计了一个算法来给出上界，并证明了与该精度相匹配的下界。我们的算法遵循对过程的 Choi 态表示执行最优层析的策略，并表明信道层析可归约为已得到深入研究的纯态层析问题。由于信道将态、酉变换与测量作为特例包含在内，我们的协议为这些任务的层析提供了统一框架。

Abstract (English)
The evolution of quantum states can be described by quantum channels. The task of estimating an unknown quantum channel, known as process tomography, is a central problem in quantum information theory. A long-standing open question is how many uses of an unknown channel are required to learn it to a precision quantified by the standard metric for distinguishing quantum processes, known as the diamond distance. Here we address this by establishing the query complexity of channel tomography with optimal dependence on the dimensions of the input and output spaces and the channel’s Kraus rank at any fixed constant accuracy in diamond distance. We design an algorithm that establishes an upper bound and prove a matching lower bound for learning a channel to the given accuracy. Our algorithm follows the strategy of performing optimal tomography on the Choi-state representation of the process, and we show that channel tomography can be reduced to the well-studied problem of pure-state tomography. As channels subsume states, unitaries and measurements as special cases, our protocol provides a unified framework for tomography of these tasks.

Antonio Anna Mele, Lennart Bittel
Nat. Phys. (2026)
10.1038/s41567-026-03499-7

突破泊松噪声极限的宏观光子计数Macroscopic photon counting beating the Poisson noise limit

将八个光子数分辨超导纳米线探测器复用到 128 个时间模式，实现 0 到 9000 余个光子的精确计数，突破泊松噪声极限 4.1 dB。

光子计数是量子光学的基石。在此，我们演示了从 0 到超过 9000 个光子的精确计数，在整个范围内至少突破泊松噪声极限 4.1 dB。我们在每个脉冲最多 276 个光子时实现了优于单光子的精度。为此，我们将八个本征具备光子数分辨能力的超导纳米线单光子探测器复用到 128 个时间模式上。我们对 1024 个探测单元逐一进行了基于模型的表征，以获得最优精度。我们开展量子探测器层析，重建了整个器件的正算符取值测度（POVM），其包含 1.38×10^8 个矩阵元。在实验 80 kHz 的重复频率下，我们可以精确计数对应于 71 pW 光功率的光子，弥合了从单光子测量到高灵敏度光功率计之间的差距。如此规模的光子数分辨探测器，以及用于分析它的工具，对于刻画大尺度量子态以及精密计量和光功率标准中的任务将日益重要。

Abstract (English)
Photon counting is a cornerstone of quantum optics. Here, we demonstrate precisely counting from 0 to over 9000 photons, beating the Poisson noise limit by at least 4.1 dB across this range. We achieve sub-single-photon precision up to 276 photons per pulse. To do so, we multiplex eight intrinsically photon-number-resolving superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors across 128 temporal modes. We use a model-informed characterization of each of the 1024 detection bins, for optimal precision. We perform quantum detector tomography to reconstruct the positive operator-valued measures (POVMs) of the complete device, which consists of 1.38⋅10 8 matrix elements. At the repetition rate of our experiment of 80 kHz, we can precisely count photons corresponding to an optical power of 71 pW, bridging the gap from single-photon measurements to high-sensitivity optical power meters. A photon-number-resolving detector of this size, and the tools used to analyze it, will become increasingly important to characterize large quantum states as well as tasks in precision metrology and optical power standards.

Timon Schapeler, Fabian Schlue, Isabell Mischke, Michael Stefszky, Benjamin Brecht, Christine Silberhorn, Tim J. Bartley

10.1364/optica.604389

量子一致定理：共同确定性与观察者之间的共识The quantum agreement theorem

在有限维三体情形下研究经典 Aumann 一致定理的量子对应：当两个观察者的测量彼此对易且与所关注属性对易时，共同确定性迫使其概率判断一致；并构造 qutrit–qubit–qubit 模型族，证明测量不对易时可出现对分歧的共同确定性。

主体间一致性所追问的是：一个物理系统的不同观察者依据共享的理论进行推理时，何时会在其概率判断上取得一致。经典概率论以一致定理的形式给出了基准[R. J. Aumann, Ann. Stat. 4, 1236–1239 (1976)]。我们在有限维三体情形下研究经典一致定理的量子力学（QM）对应版本，其中两个观察者通过局域投影测量获取信息。我们用共同确定性来表示他们的相互知晓，形式上它是关于某一感兴趣事件概率的无限层级确定性算符。我们首先证明，若观察者的测量彼此对易且与所关注的属性对易，则共同确定性迫使其概率判断趋于一致，从而恢复经典一致定理的 QM 对应。随后我们构造了一个单参数的 qutrit–qubit–qubit 模型族，其中一个观察者的测量与所关注属性不对易，并出现对分歧的共同确定性——这是一种独特的 QM 现象。其机制在于：某些分支被一个观察者的测量所区分、却不被另一个观察者的测量所区分，而这些分支之间存在相干性。当测量结果被存储到经典寄存器中时，由此产生的退相干会消除这种相干性并恢复一致。最后，我们证明 QM 不允许极端的 0–1 构型，即 Alice 确定某属性成立、同时又确定 Bob 确定其不成立。这些结果旨在把 QM 中关于观察者相关事实的争论，转化为精确的数学条件，以刻画 QM 中主体间的一致或分歧在何时能够维持。

Abstract (English)
Intersubjective consistency asks when different observers of a physical system, reasoning from a shared theory, will agree on their probability assessments. Classical probability theory provides a benchmark in the form of the Agreement Theorem [R. J. Aumann, Ann. Stat. 4 , 1236–1239 (1976)]. We study the quantum mechanics (QM) analog to the classical Agreement Theorem in a finite-dimensional tripartite setting in which two observers obtain information via local projective measurements. We represent their mutual awareness by common certainty, which is formally an infinite hierarchy of certainty operators concerning the probability of an event of interest. We first prove that if the observers’ measurements commute with one another and with the property of interest, common certainty forces their probability assessments to coincide, thereby recovering a QM analog to the classical Agreement Theorem. We then construct a one-parameter family of qutrit–qubit–qubit models in which one observer’s measurement does not commute with the property and common certainty of disagreement occurs—a distinctively QM phenomenon. The mechanism is coherence between branches distinguished by one but not the other observer’s measurement. When measurement outcomes are stored in a classical register, the resulting dephasing removes this coherence and restores agreement. Finally, we prove that QM does not permit the extreme 0 - 1 configuration in which Alice is certain of a property and is also certain that Bob is certain of its negation. These results aim to turn debate in QM about observer-dependent facts into precise mathematical conditions for when intersubjective agreement or disagreement in QM can be sustained.

María García Díaz, Adam Brandenburger, Giannicola Scarpa
PNAS 123 (2026)
10.1073/pnas.2613021123

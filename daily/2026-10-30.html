No. 01

利用逻辑量子比特实现稀有事件的量子传感Rare-Event Quantum Sensing Using Logical Qubits

★ 编辑推荐

借助量子纠错在噪声环境中探测稀有信号，利用综合征提取中的非线性处理以高阶关联区分信号与噪声，对随机时刻出现的稀有信号灵敏度优于常规方案。

我们提出一种利用量子纠错（QEC）在噪声环境中探测稀有信号的新协议。该协议的关键特征是利用不同的高阶关联来区分信号与噪声，这通过量子纠错中综合征提取过程里发生的非线性处理来实现。在该方案中，量子纠错有两方面作用。第一，它通过记录一个约化、随机的逻辑相位 ϕ_L = O(ε³) 而牺牲掉一部分信号 ε。第二，它校正物理噪声并延长用于信号采集的（逻辑）相干时间。对于在局域马尔可夫噪声下随机时刻出现的稀有信号，我们明确证明该方法相比更常规的传感策略具有更高的灵敏度。

Abstract (English)
We present a novel protocol to detect rare signals in a noisy environment using quantum error correction (QEC). The key feature of our protocol is the discrimination between signal and noise through distinct higher-order correlations, realized by the nonlinear processing that occurs during syndrome extraction in QEC. In this scheme, QEC has two effects. First, it sacrifices part of the signal ε by recording a reduced, stochastic, logical phase ϕ L = O ( ε 3 ) . Second, it corrects the physical noise and extends the (logical) coherence time for signal acquisition. For rare signals occurring at random times in the presence of local Markovian noise, we explicitly demonstrate an improved sensitivity of our approach over more conventional sensing strategies.

Robert Ott, Torsten V. Zache, Soonwon Choi, Adam M. Kaufman, and Hannes Pichler

No. 02

利用毫米波实现超导量子比特的态读取Millimeter Wave Readout of a Superconducting Qubit

★ Physics 精选

把 34.7 GHz 毫米波腔与 3.1 GHz transmon 强耦合，以超大失谐抑制驱动引起的共振跃迁，百光子量级驱动下仍无杂散跃迁，无需量子限放大器即实现超过 99% 的读取保真度。

毫米波正成为连接并增强里德堡原子、光机械与超导量子比特等不同量子平台的使能技术。在本快报中，我们探索毫米波光子与常规 transmon 量子比特之间的相互作用，特别是用于量子比特读取。我们研究一个电路量子电动力学系统，它由频率 ω_r = 2π × 34.7 GHz 的毫米波腔与频率 ω_q = 2π × 3.1 GHz 的 transmon 量子比特以速率 g = 2π × 1.3 GHz 耦合而成。由于腔与量子比特之间失谐如此之大，ω_r/ω_q > 10，我们能够抑制驱动引起的不需要的共振态跃迁，从而实现用于量子比特读取的强驱动。即使驱动光子数超过 100，我们也没有测到共振态跃迁，并用超过 100 个光子读出量子比特态，在无需量子限放大器的情况下实现了超过 99% 的测量保真度。

Abstract (English)
Millimeter waves are emerging as an enabling technology for connecting and enhancing different quantum platforms such as Rydberg atoms, optomechanics, and superconducting qubits. In this Letter, we explore the interaction between millimeter wave photons and conventional transmon qubits, specifically for qubit readout. We study a circuit quantum electrodynamic system consisting of a millimeter wave cavity at ω r = 2 π × 34.7 GHz and a transmon qubit at ω q = 2 π × 3.1 GHz coupled at rate g = 2 π × 1.3 GHz . With such a large detuning between cavity and qubit, ω r / ω q > 10 , we are able to suppress drive induced unwanted resonant state transitions, enabling strong drives for qubit readout. We measure no resonant state transitions even when driving with more than 100 photons and read out the qubit state with more than 100 photons to achieve a measurement fidelity greater than 99% without the aid of a quantum limited amplifier.

Akash V. Dixit, Zachary L. Parrott, Dennis Chunikhin, Trevyn F. Q. Larson, Bradley D. Hauer, and John D. Teufel

No. 03

在光晶格中构建任意相干原子重排Constructing Arbitrary Coherent Rearrangements in Optical Lattices

类比光学超晶格与离散线性光学，用 Clements 方案在双阱阵列中由全局隧穿与局域势移构造任意 N 维单粒子幺正变换，推广到二维可实现电路深度随原子数亚线性增长的全对全原子重排。

光晶格中超冷原子运动自由度的相干控制，为实现大质量粒子的可编程量子动力学提供了一条有前景的途径。我们提出并分析了一种实现超冷原子相干重排的方案，它对应于单粒子运动态上的任意幺正变换。利用光学超晶格中的动力学与离散线性光学之间的类比，我们采用 Clements 方案，从双阱阵列中的隧穿与相移出发系统地构造任意 N 维单粒子幺正变换。隧穿被全局控制，而局域操作则通过位点分辨的势移实现。我们用数值方法研究了该方案对强度噪声与寻址串扰的敏感性。我们确认了这一幺正构造所支持的关键子程序，包括离散傅里叶变换与非原生哈密顿量的实现。将方案推广到二维，可实现全对全的原子重排，其电路深度随原子数亚线性增长，为原子重排提供了一种高密度、高可扩展性的途径。

Abstract (English)
Coherent control of motional degrees of freedom of ultracold atoms in optical lattices offers a promising route toward programmable quantum dynamics with massive particles. We propose and analyze a scheme for implementing coherent rearrangement of ultracold atoms, corresponding to arbitrary unitary transformations on single-particle motional states. Exploiting an analogy between dynamics in optical superlattices and discrete linear optics, we employ the Clements scheme to systematically construct any global N -dimensional single-particle unitary from tunneling and phase shifts in arrays of double wells. Tunneling is controlled globally, while local operations are achieved through site-resolved potential shifts. We numerically investigate the susceptibility of the scheme to intensity noise and addressing crosstalk. We identify key subroutines enabled by this unitary construction, including the discrete Fourier transform and the implementation of non-native Hamiltonians. Extending the scheme to two dimensions enables all-to-all atomic rearrangement with a circuit depth that scales sublinearly with the atom number, providing a high-density and highly scalable approach to atom rearrangement.

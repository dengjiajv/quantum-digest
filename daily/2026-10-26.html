自由互信息与高阶乱序关联函数Free Mutual Information and Higher-Point Out-of-Time-Ordered Correlators

引入源自自由概率论的自由互信息作为量子混沌的新判据，给出其「库仑气体」表达式，并证明它等于所有高阶 OTOC 的加权和，从而比四点 OTOC 更敏锐地区分物理空间中的扩散与算子空间中的局域化。

我们引入一个称为自由互信息（FMI）的量，它改编自自由概率论中的概念，作为量子混沌的一种新的物理度量。该量刻画了随时间演化的算子在希尔伯特空间上所有可能算子构成的空间中的扩散，而该空间随自由度数目呈双指数增长。因此，它提供了比已充分理解的物理空间中算子增长现象更为精细的算子扩散概念。我们推导了两个适用于任何物理系统的核心结果：第一，用乘积算子 A(t)B 的本征值表示两个可观测量 A(t) 与 B 的 FMI 的显式「库仑气体」公式；第二，将 FMI 表示为所有高阶乱序关联函数（OTOC）加权求和的一般关系。第二个结果为高阶 OTOC 提供了精确的信息论解释：它们共同量化了算子的遍历性以及趋近自由性的程度。鉴于近期在实验测量高阶 OTOC 方面的进展，这一物理解释尤其有用。我们在包括随机幺正电路和混沌自旋链在内的多种混沌系统中识别出 FMI 与高阶 OTOC 的普适行为，这表明双指数算子空间中的扩散是量子多体混沌的一般特征。同时，FMI 在包括某些幺正设计在内的各种非混沌系统中的非普适行为表明，存在算子在物理空间中扩散但在算子空间中仍保持局域的情形。因此，FMI 是比标准四点 OTOC 更敏锐的混沌判据。

Abstract (English)
We introduce a quantity called the free mutual information (FMI), adapted from concepts in free probability theory, as a new physical measure of quantum chaos. This quantity captures the spreading of a time-evolved operator in the space of all possible operators on the Hilbert space, which is doubly exponential in the number of degrees of freedom. It thus provides a finer notion of operator spreading than the well-understood phenomenon of operator growth in physical space. We derive two central results which apply in any physical system: first, an explicit “Coulomb gas” formula for the FMI of two observables A ( t ) and B in terms of the eigenvalues of the product operator A ( t ) B ; and second, a general relation expressing the FMI as a weighted sum of all higher-point out-of-time-ordered correlators (OTOCs). This second result provides a precise information-theoretic interpretation for the higher-point OTOCs as collectively quantifying operator ergodicity and the approach to freeness. This physical interpretation is particularly useful in light of recent progress in experimentally measuring higher-point OTOCs. We identify universal behaviors of the FMI and higher-point OTOCs across a variety of chaotic systems, including random unitary circuits and chaotic spin chains, which indicate that spreading in the doubly exponential operator space is a generic feature of quantum many-body chaos. At the same time, the non-generic behavior of the FMI in various non-chaotic systems, including certain unitary designs, shows that there are cases where an operator spreads in physical space but remains localized in operator space. The FMI is thus a sharper diagnostic of chaos than the standard four-point OTOC. See 17 more figures
